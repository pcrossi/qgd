# Terminological agreement

## Scope

This entry records the vocabulary contract introduced in manuscript section
`01.2 - Acordo terminológico`.

The goal is to avoid repeatedly naming every historical source and to prevent
GDQ terms from being silently replaced by external formalisms.

## Definitions fixed by the chapter

- `GDQ`: the theory developed in the manuscript, not a generic label for
  quantum gravity.
- `official action`: only the fundamental GDQ action, not auxiliary reduced
  actions.
- `bulk`: Hermitian complex domain of the official action.
- `physical spacetime`: Lorentzian geometry reconstructed or projected from
  the bulk.
- `cosmological geometry`: global background such as `T^5 x S^3`, not
  automatically identical to the local bulk.
- `density`: the field `rho`, positive on the regular domain.
- `phase`: the field `S_R`, including possible holonomy around defects.
- `measure`: the official weighted measure `U`.
- `torsion`: the real three-form of the Hermitian torsional connection.
- `stoma`: internal defect or density opening associated with a topological
  core.
- `measurement`: interaction correlating an object sector with a stable
  macroscopic record.

## Important separation

The section explicitly separates:

- flow parameter `tau`;
- physical time `t`;
- complex causal variable `z_tau`;
- cosmological space `T^5 x S^3`;
- local bulk `R^4 x T^4`;
- Kähler geometry;
- Hermitian geometry with torsion.

## Status

Defined and active. Later chapters may specialize terms, but must say so
explicitly.
