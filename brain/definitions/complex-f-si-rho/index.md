# Complex f, S_I and rho

The complex field $f$ decomposes as

$$
f=-\frac{S_I-iS_R}{\hbar}.
$$

Therefore

$$
S_I=-\hbar\,\operatorname{Re}f,
\qquad
S_R=\hbar\,\operatorname{Im}f,
\qquad
\rho=e^{S_I/\hbar}=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2}.
$$

$S_I$ is local. It must not be identified with the global Perelman functional
$\mathcal W$.
