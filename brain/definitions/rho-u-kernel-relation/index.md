# Relation between rho and U

The official relation is

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2},
\qquad
\mathcal U=\frac{\rho}{(4\pi z_\tau)^n}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
(4\pi z_\tau)^n\mathcal U=\rho.
$$

Thus $\mathcal U=\rho$ is only valid after explicitly removing the heat-kernel
factor.
