# Physical Hilbert space

GDQ defines the physical operational Hilbert space by

$$
\mathcal H_{\rm phys}
=
\overline{
\mathcal D_+/
(\mathcal N+\mathcal G)
}.
$$

The inner product is

$$
\langle [F],[G]\rangle
=
\langle \Theta F\,G\rangle_E.
$$

In regular one-particle sectors this reduces to

$$
L^2(N,E,d\Sigma_h).
$$
