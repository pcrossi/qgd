# Physical spacetime immersion and clock form

## Definition

Physical spacetime is a four-dimensional manifold $N$ immersed in the local
bulk:

$$
X:N\longrightarrow M.
$$

The induced metric is

$$
q=X^*g.
$$

Since $g$ is Riemannian and $X$ is an immersion, $q$ is positive definite.

Choose the clock one-form on the active internal circle:

$$
\omega=d\theta_1.
$$

Its pullback is

$$
u=X^*\omega.
$$

The physical sector is

$$
\mathcal C
=
\left\{
X\in{\rm Imm}(N,M):X^*\omega\neq0
\right\}.
$$

## Status

The condition $X^*\omega\neq0$ is a kinematic/topological restriction of the
physical sector. It is not derived as an infinite barrier from the action.
