# Local bulk R4 x T4

## Definition

The current local bulk of GDQ is:

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4.
$$

This is a structural choice of the theory.

## Consequences

Since

$$
T^4=S^1_1\times S^1_2\times S^1_3\times S^1_4,
$$

one has:

$$
\dim_{\mathbb R}M=4+4=8.
$$

With an admitted complex structure:

$$
\dim_{\mathbb C}M=4.
$$

The factors are parallelizable, so:

$$
TM\cong M\times\mathbb R^8,
\qquad
w_2(TM)=0.
$$

Thus spin structures exist. The physical spin structure is not selected by
existence alone.

## Status

Defined structural input. The dimension count and spin existence are verified
consequences. Dynamic selection of `R^4 x T^4` remains open.
