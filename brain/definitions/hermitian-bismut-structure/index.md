# Hermitian-Bismut structure

## Definition

An almost-complex structure is:

$$
J:TM\to TM,
\qquad
J^2=-I.
$$

With integrability and a compatible metric:

$$
g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y),
$$

the pair `(g,J)` is Hermitian. The fundamental form is:

$$
\omega_H(X,Y)=g(JX,Y).
$$

The geometry is Kähler strict only if:

$$
d\omega_H=0.
$$

GDQ admits sectors with Bismut torsion:

$$
H=d_J^c\omega_H.
$$

If `H != 0`, the sector is not Kähler strict.

## Variational status

In the current official action, `g`, `f`, and `bar f` are varied. The structure
`J` is structural data and `H` is derived from `(g,J)` under the chosen
convention. Treating `H` as independent is an effective extension, not a
silent change of the official action.
