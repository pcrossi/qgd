# Geometric flow parameter tau

The GDQ flow parameter is

$$
\tau\in\mathbb R_+,
\qquad
[\tau]=L^2.
$$

It parametrizes geometric/diffusive flow, heat-kernel resolution and scale
windows. It is not the physical chronological time.

The physical time enters through

$$
z_\tau=\tau+i\nu_0t,
\qquad
\nu_0=\frac{\hbar}{2m_0}.
$$

The contour $\gamma$ lives in the $z_\tau$ plane.

The dimensionless and logarithmic variables are

$$
\widehat\tau=\frac{\tau}{\ell_C^2},
\qquad
s=\log\widehat\tau.
$$

For the official bulk $d=8$, $n=4$,

$$
K(\tau)\sim(4\pi\tau)^{-4}.
$$

Do not identify $\tau$ with $t$, $\log\mu$ or an adimensional parameter. Use
$t$, $s$ and $\widehat\tau$ for those roles.
