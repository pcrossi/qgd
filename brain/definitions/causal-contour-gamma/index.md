# Causal contour gamma

## Definition

The causal contour `gamma` specifies how the flow variable traverses the
complexified causal domain. It selects:

- branches;
- orientations;
- pole prescriptions;
- endpoint data;
- conjugate pairings;
- residues that survive.

It is part of the domain of the official action. It is not varied together
with `g`, `f`, and `bar f`.

## Important separation

`gamma` is a contour in the causal complex plane. A material circulation cycle
is denoted by `C` and lives in configuration space or an internal fiber.

Thus:

$$
\gamma\neq C.
$$

## Status

Defined structural element of the official GDQ action.
