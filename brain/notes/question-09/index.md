# Question 09 - Fundamental action

Q9 asks what the fundamental GDQ action is.

## Official action

$$
\mathcal{S}_{\rm GDQ}
=
\int_{\gamma}
\left[
\int_{\mathcal M_\mathbb C}
\frac{\hbar}{\Lambda_C^2}
\left[
\tau
\left(
\mathcal R
+g^{\mu\bar\nu}
\partial_\mu f
\partial_{\bar\nu}\bar f
\right)
+\frac{f+\bar f}{2}
-n
\right]
\mathcal U
\sqrt{\det g}\,
d^{2n}z
\right]
\frac{d\tau}{\tau}.
$$

with

$$
n=4,
\qquad
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4,
\qquad
\mathcal U
=
\frac{e^{-(f+\bar f)/2}}{(4\pi z_\tau)^n}.
$$

The physical stationary action is

$$
S_{\rm phys}=\operatorname{Re}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}.
$$

## Independent variables

$$
g_{\mu\bar\nu},
\qquad
f,
\qquad
\bar f.
$$

$\mathcal U$, $\rho$, $R$, $S_I$, $S_R$ and $\Psi$ are derived.

## Dimensional convention

Q9 must be read with the later dimensional clarification: $\Lambda_C$ in the
official action is the dimensionless Cartan cutoff number in normalized
coordinates. Dimensional quantities are $\ell_C$, $k_C=\ell_C^{-1}$ and
$E_C=\hbar c k_C$.

## Perelman and effective actions

The pure Perelman functional is auxiliary. Effective actions in $N^4$,
perturbative gauge/spin sectors, BRST/Faddeev--Popov descriptions and
propagators are reductions or audit layers, not replacements for the official
action.

## Status

Q9 is closed: the official contour action is the fundamental action. No
central physical equation may be added independently; later equations must be
derived, reduced or audited from this action and declared structures.
