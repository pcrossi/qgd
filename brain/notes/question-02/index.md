# Question 02

Question 02 fixes the mathematical definition of GDQ at the effective
axiomatic level. The original question asked for the dimension of the
fundamental variety; the consolidated answer also fixes the local bulk,
physical spacetime, Lorentzian constitutive metric, spin structure, torsion,
gauge separation and the spectral status of quantitative predictions.

## Core answer

The fundamental local bulk is

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb R}M=8,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb C}M=4.
$$

The bulk remains Riemannian/Hermitian-Bismut. Physical spacetime is a
four-dimensional manifold $N$ immersed in $M$.

## Important non-confusions

- The Lorentzian metric is constitutive on $N$, not a signature change of the
  Riemannian bulk metric.
- The clock form and electromagnetic gauge field are distinct sectors.
- A Bismut 3-form is not the Maxwell 2-form.
- Half-integer circulation follows from the chosen antiperiodic spin sector,
  but does not by itself prove fermionic anticommutation.
- The local effective action discussed historically in Q2 is not the official
  fundamental GDQ action.
