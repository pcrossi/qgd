# Manuscript chapter 01

## Scope

This entry indexes the first manuscript chapter:

`manuscrito/01_initial_problem/`

The chapter states the initial problem of GDQ: the relation between positive
diffusive path measures and complex oscillatory quantum amplitudes cannot be
treated as a simple typographic Wick substitution.

## Main chain

1. Wigner motivates the methodological question: mathematics is effective in
   physics, but predictive success does not remove the need for interpretation.
2. Wiener supplies the positive probabilistic path measure.
3. Feynman supplies the oscillatory amplitude over paths.
4. Wick rotation relates unitary evolution and heat semigroups only under
   analytic, spectral, domain, boundary, and positivity assumptions.
5. Total derivatives and gauge terms show that boundary data are part of the
   physical problem.
6. Madelung separates density and phase, giving continuity plus
   Hamilton-Jacobi-Bohm structure.
7. Nelson handles non-differentiable paths through forward/backward mean
   derivatives and osmotic velocity.
8. A universal diffusion scale is proposed as a conditional bridge to inertial
   response.
9. Perelman-style geometric flow appears as the first natural geometric arena
   where density, heat flow, variational control, and critical geometry coexist.

## Current status

The chapter is foundational and pedagogical, not a closed derivation of the
full GDQ. It correctly classifies the geometric step as a candidate structure,
not as a theorem already proving Lorentzian reconstruction, unitarity, or the
official GDQ action.
