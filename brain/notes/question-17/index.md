# Question 17 - Cauchy problem

Q17 asks whether the GDQ dynamical system has a well-posed Cauchy problem.

The answer concerns the geometric flow in $\tau$, not physical Lorentzian time
$t$.

The classifications are:

- stationary sector: elliptic after gauge fixing;
- geometric flow in $\tau$: quasilinear strongly parabolic after gauge;
- physical evolution in $t$: hyperbolic/unitary in the effective Lorentzian
  layer.

The variables are

$$
U=(g,B,\phi,\chi),
\qquad
f=\phi+i\chi.
$$

Gauge fixing:

- DeTurck gauge for $g$;
- Hodge gauge for $B$;
- Perelman weighted-measure gauge for the dilaton sector when useful.

In gauge, the principal symbol is block diagonal with leading part

$$
\sigma_{\rm principal}(\xi)=|\xi|_g^2 I.
$$

Since the bulk metric is Riemannian positive, the system is strongly
parabolic.

For data in $C^{k,\alpha}$, $k\ge2$, or $H^s$ with $s>d/2+2$ and $d=8$,
there is local existence, uniqueness in gauge, uniqueness modulo
diffeomorphisms geometrically, continuous dependence and continuation while
metric, curvature, torsion and derivatives of $f$ stay controlled.

## Status

Q17 is closed for the local well-posedness of the gauge-fixed geometric flow.
