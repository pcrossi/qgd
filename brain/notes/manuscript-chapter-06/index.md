# Manuscript chapter 06

Chapter 06 builds the global--local bridge from the Einstein cosmological
space to the laboratory chart. Its purpose is to justify when results computed
on $T^5\times S^3$ may be used in the local bulk
$\mathbb R^4\times T^4$ without identifying the two spaces globally.

## Status

The abstract bridge is established as a chain of six lemmas:

1. pointed geometric convergence;
2. transport of fields and measure;
3. convergence of the physical Hessian as quadratic forms;
4. localization and uniform gap;
5. convergence of resolvents and Riesz projectors;
6. separation between transported data and non-transported normalizations.

For the reduced stationary $C_3$ sector, the hypotheses are evaluated and the
bridge is an applied theorem with primitive gap $\Delta_0=1/2$ after removal
of Noether zero modes.

## Boundaries of the result

The chapter does not prove every background of GDQ admissible. It proves how
to transport a sector once the background, physical Hessian, domain and gap are
available. Continuous normalizations such as $Z_Q$ require a separate flux or
DtN/scattering argument, especially for massless channels.

The canonical Madelung identity $\Pi_{S_R}=\rho$ remains a conditional
polarization/reduction issue, not an off-shell identity of the official action.
