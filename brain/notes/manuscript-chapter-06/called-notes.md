## Role of the note

The note proves that the phase current and presymplectic potential are derived
from the official action, but the canonical Madelung identity

$$
\Pi_{S_R}^{\rm lab}=\rho_{\rm lab}
$$

is not an off-shell identity of the official action.

It can be selected in a stationary comoving sector and justified
conditionally by Routh minimization if the required hypotheses are derived:

1. constant positive temporal coefficient $A$;
2. absence of lapse/shift/internal cross terms;
3. absence of flux leakage;
4. positive connected support for $\rho$;
5. primitive phase charge fixed independently;
6. background in the convex minimizing sector.

The note also records that the exponential causal pullback
$\tau_\gamma(t)=\tau_0e^{\kappa t}$ follows if the physical clock map is a
continuous homomorphism from translations to positive dilations. This is a
valid conditional theorem, not yet a derivation of the physical clock map from
the full GDQ measurement dynamics.
