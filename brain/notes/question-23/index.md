# Question 23 - Wallstrom objection

Q23 asks how GDQ prevents arbitrary continuous Madelung circulations.

The phase is not a global real function. The physical object is

$$
e^{iS_R/\hbar}
$$

as a section of a Hermitian line bundle

$$
\Psi\in\Gamma(L),
\qquad
L\to M^\ast,
\qquad
M^\ast=M\setminus Z_\rho.
$$

The integral class is

$$
c_1(L)
=
\left[\frac{F_A}{2\pi}\right]
\in
H^2(M^\ast,\mathbb Z).
$$

For a closed cycle $C$,

$$
\oint_C\nabla S_R\cdot dx=Nh,
\qquad
N\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Non-integer circulation would make the section fail to close globally in the
chosen sector, so it is not an admissible physical state.

Nodes $\rho=0$ are removed from the regular domain and treated as defects or
topological boundaries; their complement carries the cycles around which
holonomy is quantized.

The Poisson summation argument is a consequence of $S^1$ topology, not the
origin of quantization.

Q23 is structurally closed as a global bundle/holonomy resolution.
