## Status of the legacy note

The note discusses possible GDQ treatments of the muon $g-2$ and $B$-meson
flavour anomalies through torsional/geometric impedance. It is preserved as
future phenomenology and not used as proof of the classical correspondence.

Reason: its coefficients, e.g. the geometric elastic coefficient and Fano-like
correction, require independent derivation from the official Hessian and
numerical audit. It must not be cited as an established prediction in Chapter
07.
