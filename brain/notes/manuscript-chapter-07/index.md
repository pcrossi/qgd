# Manuscript chapter 07

Chapter 07 establishes the classical limit and correspondence principle for
GDQ. It starts from the Madelung sector selected in Chapters 5 and 6 and proves
that Hamilton--Jacobi, Hamilton/Newton and Liouville emerge when the Bohm term
is asymptotically small.

## Status

The scalar classical limit is closed conditionally in the regular Madelung
sector. The controlling parameter is

$$
\varepsilon_{\rm cl}
=\frac{\hbar}{pL_\rho}.
$$

When $\varepsilon_{\rm cl}\ll1$, away from nodes, abrupt boundaries and
caustics, the Bohm correction satisfies

$$
\frac{|Q_B|}{T_{\rm cl}}
=O(\varepsilon_{\rm cl}^2).
$$

The chapter also records correspondence of the $U(1)_Q$ sector to Maxwell and
of the macroscopic metric sector to Einstein/Newton. These are sectoral
correspondences using prior GDQ structures, not derivations from the scalar
Hamilton--Jacobi equation alone.

## Scope

The chapter does not prove the Madelung polarisation itself; that belongs to
Chapters 5 and 6. It also does not compute absolute normalisations such as
$Z_Q$, $\alpha$ or $G$. It proves the formal classical shapes and states the
remaining normalisation/background requirements.
