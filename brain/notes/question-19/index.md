# Question 19 - Monotonicity and stability

Q19 asks whether monotonicity of geometric functionals implies soliton
stability.

Answer: not by itself.

The torsional Perelman functionals $\mathcal F_T$ and $\mathcal W_T$ are
Lyapunov tools for the geometric flow. In the convention used,

$$
\frac{d\mathcal F_T}{d\tau}\ge0,
\qquad
\frac{d\mathcal W_T}{d\tau}\ge0.
$$

Their derivatives are sums of squares under the stated regularity, boundary,
normalization and torsion assumptions.

Stability requires:

$$
\text{monotonicity}
+
\text{true critical point}
+
\text{Hessian/Jacobi sign on physical sector}
\Longrightarrow
\text{local/orbital stability}.
$$

The Jacobi operator is

$$
\mathcal J_{\mathfrak S}=D^2\mathcal I_T|_{\mathfrak S}
$$

after DeTurck--Hodge gauge fixing and removal of diffeomorphism, gauge,
translation, rotation, scale and moduli zero modes.

For the neutral Gaussian soliton, the scalar operator reduces to
Ornstein--Uhlenbeck type and is controlled. Charged/spinorial solitons require
sector-specific Hessian evaluation.

## Status

Q19 is conditionally closed: monotonicity is not stability; stability follows
only after Jacobi/Hessian control in the physical sector.
