# Question 21 - Unitary evolution

Q21 asks whether physical GDQ evolution preserves the inner product in physical
time $t$.

In the closed physical sector, evolution is generated by the OS-reconstructed
Hamiltonian

$$
H=H^\dagger,
\qquad
H\ge0.
$$

The unitary group is

$$
U(t)=e^{-itH/\hbar}.
$$

Therefore

$$
U(t)^\dagger U(t)=I
$$

and

$$
\langle U(t)\Psi,U(t)\Phi\rangle
=
\langle\Psi,\Phi\rangle.
$$

Thus

$$
\|\Psi(t)\|^2=\|\Psi(0)\|^2.
$$

The geometric flow in $\tau$ is diffusive/renormalizational and may be
contractive; it is not the physical unitary time evolution. Instabilities,
NESS, decays and irreversible macroscopic behaviour are effective projected or
open-system descriptions, not violations of fundamental unitarity.
