# Question 18 - Solitons as particles

Q18 asks whether GDQ has solitons that can represent particles.

A GDQ soliton is a stationary configuration

$$
\mathfrak S=(g,B,f,\bar f)
$$

solving the stationary Ricci--Bismut/Perelman equations in a fixed gauge and
topological sector.

The torsional stationary equation is schematically

$$
R_{ij}
-\frac14B_{ik\ell}B_j{}^{k\ell}
+\nabla_i\nabla_j\phi
=
\lambda g_{ij},
$$

with

$$
d^\dagger_\phi B=0,
\qquad
dB=0.
$$

There is an explicit neutral reference solution: the Gaussian shrinking
soliton on $\mathbb R^d$ with $g=\delta$, $B=0$ and

$$
\phi(x)=\frac{|x|^2}{4\sigma},
\qquad
\rho_N=(4\pi\sigma)^{-d/2}e^{-|x|^2/(4\sigma)}.
$$

It has finite normalized Perelman free energy and is linearly stable modulo
symmetries, but it is neutral: no charge, spin or torsion.

To validate a physical particle, one must provide a soliton sheet with:
equation residual zero, finite energy, mass, charge, spin, Hessian spectrum,
zero-mode classification, asymptotics and interaction/scattering behaviour.

## Status

Q18 is closed as mathematical criterion plus neutral minimal solution. It does
not prove that electron, proton or neutron have already been fully obtained
without the sector-specific solution and stability data.
