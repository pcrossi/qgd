# Manuscript chapter 03

## Scope

This entry indexes the third manuscript chapter:

`manuscrito/03_complex_causality/`

The chapter resolves the Wick paradox structurally by separating the flow
parameter `tau`, physical time `t`, the causal complex variable `z_tau`, and
the causal contour `gamma`.

## Main chain

1. Wick rotation relates a unitary group and a heat semigroup under
   hypotheses, but it does not explain physical time, causality, boundary
   data, or reconstruction by itself.
2. GDQ separates `t`, `tau`, and `z_tau`.
3. Dimensional homogeneity requires `z_tau=tau+i nu_0 t`.
4. The causal contour `gamma` is part of the official action domain and
   selects branches, orientations, poles, and boundary data.
5. Exact regular forms cancel on closed contours, but periods, monodromy,
   branch cuts, and residues can survive.
6. Retarded, advanced, symmetric, and two-boundary problems are distinct; none
   is equivalent to operational signalling to the past.
7. The action is real on the admissible reflected contour class.
8. Circulation quantization follows from holonomy and integral classes, not
   from residue calculus alone.
9. Microcausality, no-signalling, and unitarity are not proved by this chapter;
   they belong to reconstruction and measurement.

## Current status

Chapter 3 is structurally closed for the causal architecture of GDQ: `gamma`
is not an external Wick rotation but a defining contour class of the official
action. Operational tests remain later tasks.
