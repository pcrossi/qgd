## Current answer

The current GDQ does not dynamically derive the bulk dimension. It defines
the local bulk as

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4.
$$

Therefore

$$
\dim_{\mathbb R}M=8,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb C}M=4.
$$

Thus $n=4$ is a mathematical consequence of the Q2 structural choice, not an
independent extra axiom and not a proven dynamic selection theorem.

## Prohibited current claims

The current theory must not claim that $n=4$ has already been derived from:

- Atiyah--Singer;
- anomaly cancellation;
- the hyperoctahedral group $B_4$;
- a fourth-order operator;
- scripts or fixtures in dimension five;
- old mixed geometries such as $\mathbb C^4\times(T^5\times S^3)$.

Closed for the purpose of defining the theory. Open only as a future programme
