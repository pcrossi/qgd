# Question 20 - Physical Hilbert space

Q20 asks what the physical Hilbert space of GDQ is.

The geometric layer is fundamental. The Hilbert space is the operational layer
reconstructed from Euclidean correlators and reflection positivity.

The physical Hilbert space is

$$
\mathcal H_{\rm phys}
=
\overline{
\mathcal D_+/
(\mathcal N+\mathcal G)
}.
$$

Here $\mathcal D_+$ is the space of positive-time cylindrical functionals,
$\mathcal N$ is the null-norm subspace from reflection positivity, and
$\mathcal G$ is the redundancy sector: diffeomorphism, gauge, chart,
longitudinal and exact-boundary directions.

The product is

$$
\langle [F],[G]\rangle
=
\langle \Theta F\,G\rangle_E.
$$

In a regular one-particle sector,

$$
\mathcal H_1=L^2(N,E,d\Sigma_h),
\qquad
\Psi=\sqrt\rho e^{iS_R/\hbar}.
$$

Observables are self-adjoint operators on dense domains or self-adjoint
elements of local algebras. Physical evolution in time $t$ is

$$
U(t)=e^{-itH/\hbar},
\qquad
H=H^\dagger.
$$

Composite distinguishable systems use tensor products; identical systems use
symmetrized or antisymmetrized sectors.
