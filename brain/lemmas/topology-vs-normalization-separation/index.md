# Topology versus normalization separation

## Statement

The pointed bridge transports relative topological data, localized spectral
clusters, multiplicities and dimensionless ratios under the previous
hypotheses. It does not automatically transport or determine absolute energy
scales, continuous coupling normalizations or detector responses.

## Practical consequence

An integer topological charge can survive the bridge while the physical unit
of charge still requires a symplectic flux or Hessian normalization
calculation.

## Status

Demonstrated as a separation principle in the chapter. Applying it to a given
constant, such as $\alpha$, requires the sector-specific calculation.
