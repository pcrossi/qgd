# Localization and uniform gap

## Statement

If the local physical operator has an isolated cluster $I$ with

$$
\Delta_0
=\operatorname{dist}
\left(
I,\sigma(K_0^{\rm phys})\setminus I
\right)>0,
$$

and the exterior vacuum threshold remains above that cluster, then the
corresponding clusters in the pointed family remain uniformly isolated for
small $\varepsilon$.

## Mechanism

IMS localization controls the split between stoma core and exterior. Agmon
estimates prevent escape of the mode into the growing volume.

## Limitation

The compactification gap $k^2/R^2$ is not sufficient; it vanishes as
$R\to\infty$. The gap must be produced by the physical Hessian of the defect
and its interface response.
