# Global-local pointed limit

## Statement

For

$$
M_\varepsilon
=T^4\times S^1_{\varepsilon^{-1}}\times S^3_{\varepsilon^{-1}},
$$

and for basepoints keeping the stoma in a fixed local chart, the family
converges smoothly in pointed Cheeger--Gromov sense to

$$
T^4\times\mathbb R^4.
$$

## Meaning

The Einstein cosmological space and the laboratory bulk are not globally the
same space. They become locally comparable on pointed charts with controlled
curvature error. This is the geometric foundation of the bridge.

## Limitations

The lemma does not construct an admissible GDQ background or prove spectral
stability. It only proves the geometric local limit and preservation of
relative defect classes that remain inside the transported compact region.
