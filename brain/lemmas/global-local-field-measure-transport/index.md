# Global-local field and measure transport

## Statement

Given a local admissible background

$$
X_0=(g_0,J_0,f_0),
$$

with

$$
H_0=d_{J_0}^c\omega_0,
\qquad
\mathcal U_0
=\frac{e^{-(f_0+\bar f_0)/2}}{(4\pi z_\tau)^4},
$$

one can insert it into the pointed family so that, on compact charts,

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*g_\varepsilon\to g_0,
\qquad
\Phi_\varepsilon^*J_\varepsilon\to J_0,
\qquad
\Phi_\varepsilon^*f_\varepsilon\to f_0,
$$

and therefore

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*H_\varepsilon\to H_0,
\qquad
\Phi_\varepsilon^*\mathcal U_\varepsilon\to\mathcal U_0.
$$

## Status

Conditional on regularity, uniform domination on the causal contour, and a
physical gap allowing correction of cutoff errors.

## Important restriction

$H$ is not introduced as an independent transported field. It remains the
Bismut torsion derived from $g$ and $J$.
