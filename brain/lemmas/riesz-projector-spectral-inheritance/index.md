# Riesz projector spectral inheritance

## Statement

Under Mosco convergence, uniform gap and localization, resolvents converge on
the isolated cluster and the Riesz projectors

$$
P_{\varepsilon,I}
=\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\oint_\Gamma
(K_\varepsilon^{\rm phys}-z)^{-1}\,dz
$$

converge to $P_{0,I}$ after the unitary identifications.

## Consequences

The following are inherited:

- rank;
- multiplicity;
- spectral class;
- symmetry decomposition when the symmetry is transported;
- internal ratios controlled inside the cluster.

## Interpretation

This is the mathematical meaning of transporting the same localized physical
sector from the Einstein background to the local laboratory bulk.
