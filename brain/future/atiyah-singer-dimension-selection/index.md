# Atiyah--Singer dimension selection

## Idea

A future GDQ version may try to derive $n=4$ using an index/anomaly argument
based on a Dirac--Bismut operator with gauge coupling.

## Current status

Future possibility only. It is not part of the current proof of Question 03.

## Required ingredients

Any future proof must specify:

1. exact operator;
2. functional domain;
3. boundary or decay conditions;
4. spinor bundle and auxiliary bundle;
5. gauge group;
6. representations;
7. chiral spectrum;
8. anomaly polynomial;
9. role of Bismut torsion;
10. treatment of noncompactness of $\mathbb R^4\times T^4$;
11. proof that $n=4$ cancels;
12. proof that alternatives fail.

## Guardrail

Old attempts that invoke Atiyah--Singer without these ingredients remain
historical sketches, not a derivation.
