# Q2 mathematical definition

## Axiom

The local fundamental bulk used by GDQ is

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4.
$$

Therefore

$$
\dim_{\mathbb R}M=8,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb C}M=4.
$$

The bulk is smooth, connected, orientable, without boundary, noncompact due
to $\mathbb R^4$, parallelizable and spin.

## Interpretation

This is a structural axiom/definition of the current theory. A future
derivation of why nature selects four complex dimensions would be a different
result and is not part of Q2's closure.

## Relation to the official action

This entry fixes the geometric arena. It does not replace the official GDQ
action by any local effective action.
