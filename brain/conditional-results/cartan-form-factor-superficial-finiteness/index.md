# Cartan form factor superficial finiteness

## Statement

In the auxiliary perturbative audit, the Cartan causal form factor modifies
the Euclidean propagator as

$$
G_C(k_E)
=e^{-k_E^2/\Lambda_C^2}G_0(k_E).
$$

This suppresses polynomial loop integrands:

$$
\int d^4k_E\,
P(k_E)e^{-ck_E^2/\Lambda_C^2}
<\infty,
\qquad c>0.
$$

## No new quadratic poles

Since the exponential factor is entire and has no zeros, multiplying a
quadratic kinetic operator by it does not create new roots:

$$
e^{F(k)}\mathcal K(k)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal K(k)=0.
$$

## Status

Conditional perturbative result. It gives superficial UV finiteness and no
new quadratic ghost poles in the declared sector. It does not prove full
all-orders or nonperturbative finiteness.
