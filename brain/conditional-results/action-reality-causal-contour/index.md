# Action reality on causal contour

## Statement

The GDQ action is real on the admissible causal contour class because the
contour pairs conjugate branches with compatible orientation and reflected
data.

After integrating over the bulk, write:

$$
\omega(\tau)
=\mathscr F(\tau)\frac{d\tau}{\tau}.
$$

The official action is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
=\int_\gamma\omega.
$$

If conjugation `c(z)=bar z` satisfies:

$$
c^*\omega=\overline\omega,
$$

and `gamma_-` is the compatible conjugate branch of `gamma_+`, then:

$$
I_+=\int_{\gamma_+}\omega,
$$

and:

$$
\int_{\gamma_-}\omega=\overline{I_+}.
$$

Thus:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
=I_++\overline{I_+}
=2\operatorname{Re}I_+
\in\mathbb R.
$$

## Limitation

This is not a claim about arbitrary complex contours. Poles, cuts, endpoint
states, fields, differentials, and orientations must also be paired correctly.

Reality of the action is not by itself unitarity.

## Status

Conditionally demonstrated for the admissible GDQ contour class.
