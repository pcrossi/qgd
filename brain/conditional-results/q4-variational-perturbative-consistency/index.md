## Conditions

The perturbative statement depends on:

- regular background;
- controlled causal continuation through $\gamma$;
- physical projection or auxiliary BRST consistency;

## Not claimed

This is not a proof of:

- nonperturbative unitarity;
- all-orders finiteness in every background;
- complete quantum gravity quantisation;
- full Standard Model equivalence;
- derivation of masses, charges or $\alpha$.
