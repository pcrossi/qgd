# Q14 Perelman-Madelung map

On the regular domain $\rho>0$ with locally single-valued phase,

$$
f=-\ln\rho+iS_R/\hbar
$$

is locally equivalent to

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2},
\qquad
S_R=\frac{\hbar}{2i}(f-\bar f).
$$

This correspondence preserves the Madelung continuity and
Hamilton--Jacobi--Bohm equations in the selected sector. It is not a global
bijection.
