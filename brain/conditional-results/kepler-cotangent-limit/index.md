# Kepler as local cotangent limit

## Statement

The radial harmonic potential on the global $S^3$ section is cotangent. In the
local chart $r\ll R_E$,

$$
\frac1{R_E}
\cot\left(\frac r{R_E}\right)
=\frac1r-\frac{r}{3R_E^2}
+O\left(\frac{r^3}{R_E^4}\right).
$$

Therefore the Kepler $1/r$ potential is the local leading term of the global
Einstein-space cotangent Green kernel.

## Limitation

The shape follows from geometry and global compensation. The coupling constant
$\kappa$ is not fixed by the harmonic equation alone; it requires the relevant
sectoral Hessian normalisation.
