# Q17 Cauchy well-posed geometric flow

After DeTurck gauge for the metric and Hodge gauge for torsion, the
Ricci--Bismut/Perelman flow has strongly parabolic principal symbol
$|\xi|_g^2I$. This gives local existence, uniqueness, continuous dependence and
continuation criteria in Hölder or Sobolev spaces, as long as the geometry
stays uniformly controlled.
