# C3 global-local bridge

## Statement

For the stationary reduced three-center Gaussian background with local torsion
balance, Noether closure and cyclic $C_3$ symmetry, the hypotheses of the
global--local bridge are satisfied after removal of common Noether modes.

The physical gap is

$$
\Delta_0
=\min\left\{
\frac32\kappa_{\rm rel}T^2,
\frac1{2\tau}
\right\}>0.
$$

In primitive normalization,

$$
\Delta_0=\frac12.
$$

## Consequences

The bridge transports the trimodal cluster, its multiplicity and the relevant
relative charges/classes from the Einstein background to the laboratory bulk.

## Limitations

This does not prove:

- all GDQ backgrounds have a gap;
- the leptonic Rosen--Morse cluster $n=0,1,17$ is automatically the same
  physical cluster;
- absolute masses or momenta;
- the continuous normalization $Z_Q$;
- a detector response.
