# Q12 metric variation and flow

The official action produces the metric equation by variation in
$g^{\mu\bar\nu}$. The stationary bulk equation is elliptic after gauge fixing,
the associated $\tau$ flow is parabolic after DeTurck gauge fixing, and
physical causal evolution is hyperbolic only after Lorentzian reduction to
$(N,h)$.

Torsion enters by the Bismut connection and by the $H^2$ sector. Conservation
is on-shell and follows from diffeomorphism invariance/Bianchi identity.
