# Lorentzianization by constitutive metric

## Statement

Given a positive metric $q$ and a nonzero one-form $u$, define

$$
s=q^{-1}(u,u)>0
$$

and

$$
h_{\mu\nu}
=q_{\mu\nu}
-2\frac{u_\mu u_\nu}{s}.
$$

Then $h$ is Lorentzian:

$$
\operatorname{sign}(h)=(-,+,+,+),
\qquad
\det h=-\det q\neq0.
$$

The inverse is

$$
h^{\mu\nu}
=q^{\mu\nu}
-2\frac{u^\mu u^\nu}{s}.
$$

## Meaning

The bulk metric does not change signature. Lorentzian signature appears as a
constitutive metric on the physical manifold $N$ after the clock one-form is
selected and nonzero.

## Consequence

The physical time direction does not depend on $\nabla f$ and does not vanish
in regions where the dilaton gradient is zero.
