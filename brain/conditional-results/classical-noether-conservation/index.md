# Classical Noether conservation

## Statement

If the semiclassical reduction and boundary conditions preserve a symmetry of
the action, the corresponding Noether current passes to the classical limit.

## Examples

- Time homogeneity implies energy conservation.
- Spatial homogeneity implies momentum conservation.
- Isotropy implies angular momentum conservation.
- Global phase shift implies conservation of the transported Madelung charge.

## Limitation

Failure of a conserved quantity in a reduced model indicates boundary flux,
external time dependence, discarded degrees of freedom, apparatus-induced
symmetry breaking or an invalid reduction.
