# Electromagnetic normalization inheritance

## Statement

For a bound electromagnetic-like mode, if the GDQ symplectic current has no
lateral flux through the region connecting the global and local slices, then
the continuous normalization is inherited:

$$
Z_Q^{\rm lab}=Z_Q^E.
$$

For a massless extended channel, the same conclusion requires flux
normalization and convergence of DtN/scattering data, not Agmon localization.

## Meaning

$Z_Q$ is the coefficient of the physical quadratic/symplectic form. It is not
an integer topological class. The bridge may preserve it only after the
appropriate no-leakage or scattering condition is proved.

## Open part

The value of $Z_Q^E$ still has to be computed from the global background using
the official Hessian and its Schur complement. The bridge gives inheritance,
not numerical evaluation.
