# Macroscopic Einstein correspondence

## Statement

Under macroscopic torsion averaging, admissible metric background and
hydrodynamic closure of the stress contained in $f$, the weighted metric
equation reduces to the trace-reversed Einstein form

$$
R_{\mu\nu}
=\kappa_G
\left(
T_{\mu\nu}-\frac12g_{\mu\nu}T
\right)
+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Tracing gives the standard Einstein equation

$$
R_{\mu\nu}-\frac12g_{\mu\nu}R+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=\kappa_GT_{\mu\nu}.
$$

## Newtonian normalisation

Dimensional analysis gives $\kappa_G=C_GG/c^4$. Matching the weak-field
$00$ component to Poisson fixes

$$
\kappa_G=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}.
$$

## Limitations

The shape of the equation is a correspondence result. The absolute value of
$G$, the cosmological background $\Lambda$ and any residual torsion require
their own global/background calculations.
