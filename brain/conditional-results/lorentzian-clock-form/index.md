# Lorentzian clock form

## Statement

The pullback of the positive bulk metric cannot by itself produce a Lorentzian
metric. A nonzero clock one-form must be selected and used to reflect one
direction.

Given a positive metric `q` and a nonzero one-form `u`, define:

$$
s=q^{-1}(u,u)>0.
$$

Then:

$$
h=q-2\frac{u\otimes u}{s}
$$

has signature:

$$
(-,+,+,+).
$$

## Selection in the current GDQ background

In the adopted cosmological Einstein space:

$$
T^5\times S^3=T^4\times S_E^1\times S^3,
$$

the comoving foliation of the distinguished circle selects the local clock
form. Synchronization at the common event fixes its scale, and the causal
contour `gamma` fixes orientation.

## Status

Conditionally demonstrated within the adopted cosmological background. It is
not a proof for every possible cosmological class.
