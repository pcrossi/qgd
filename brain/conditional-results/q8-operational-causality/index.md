# Q8 operational causality

In an effective GDQ sector whose hyperbolic operator has principal cone $h$,

$$
O_A\perp_hO_B
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
[\mathcal A(O_A),\mathcal A(O_B)]=0.
$$

This implies no-signalling for nonselective local operations.

The Sudarshan propagator

$$
G_{\rm sym}=\frac12(G_{\rm ret}+G_{\rm adv})
$$

is global boundary/phase consistency. Operational response to a controllable
local source is retarded.
