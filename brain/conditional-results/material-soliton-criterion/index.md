# Material soliton criterion

## Criterion

A GDQ material soliton is not just a curved geometric solution. It must satisfy:

1. stationarity of the official action under declared constraints;
2. finite relative action and measure;
3. localization in the physical domain;
4. a conserved charge, flux, holonomy, or class;
5. stability of the projected physical Hessian;
6. a well-defined spectral response to probes.

## Hessian condition

For a candidate background `Phi_*`, after removing gauge, normalization, and
collective modes:

$$
\mathbb H_*^{\rm phys}
=P^{\rm phys}\mathbb H_*P^{\rm phys}.
$$

Linear stability requires:

$$
\langle\eta,
\mathbb H_*^{\rm phys}\eta\rangle\ge0.
$$

Strict stability asks for a gap:

$$
\langle\eta,
\mathbb H_*^{\rm phys}\eta\rangle
\ge\Delta_*\|\eta\|^2,
\qquad
\Delta_*>0.
$$

## Status

Criterion demonstrated as the correct chain of closure. Existence of concrete
families remains sector dependent.
