# Laurent stationarity principle

## Setup

The variation on the causal contour has the schematic form

$$
\delta S
=
\oint_\gamma
E(z_\tau)
\frac{dz_\tau}{z_\tau}.
$$

If

$$
E(z_\tau)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}E_kz_\tau^k,
$$

then the contour integral gives

$$
\oint_\gamma
E(z_\tau)
\frac{dz_\tau}{z_\tau}
=2\pi iE_0.
$$

Therefore the contour equation alone implies $E_0=0$.

## Principle

To recover local equations mode by mode, Q4 adopts the supplementary
stationarity condition

$$
E_k=0
\qquad
\forall k.
$$

## Status

This is a technical reconstruction principle. It must not be confused with
the weaker statement $E_0=0$ obtained from the contour integral alone.
