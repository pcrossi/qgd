# Q2 gauge and torsion separation

## Clock pullback is not electromagnetism

For the torus coordinates,

$$
X^*d\theta_a
$$

is closed. It can carry flat holonomy, but it does not produce an arbitrary
local electromagnetic field with $F\neq0$.

Local gauge fields require an Ehresmann connection:

$$
\Theta^a=d\theta^a+g_aA^a.
$$

Then

$$
F^a=dA^a.
$$

The torus translations initially give a $U(1)^4$ structure, with the physical
electromagnetic direction and normalization determined later by the spectral
background.

## Bismut 3-form is not Maxwell

In four physical dimensions, a 3-form is Hodge-dual to a 1-form/axial sector,
not to a Maxwell 2-form. Therefore the Bismut torsion $B$ or $*_hB$ must not
be identified tensorially with $F_{\mu\nu}$.

## Status

This is a structural distinction. It prevents mixing the clock, gauge and
torsion sectors.
