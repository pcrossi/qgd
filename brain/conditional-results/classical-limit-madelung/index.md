# Classical limit in the Madelung sector

## Statement

In the regular Madelung sector

$$
p_\rho=0,
\qquad
\Pi_{S_R}=\sqrt h\,\rho,
$$

with positive mass, $\rho>0$, controlled amplitude derivatives and no caustic
or uncontrolled boundary flux, the Hamilton--Jacobi--Bohm system reduces to
Hamilton--Jacobi when

$$
\varepsilon_{\rm cl}
=\frac{\hbar}{pL_\rho}\ll1.
$$

The relative Bohm correction satisfies

$$
\frac{|Q_B|}{T_{\rm cl}}
\le C_R\varepsilon_{\rm cl}^2.
$$

## Consequences

The limit yields:

- Hamilton--Jacobi;
- Hamilton equations;
- Newton's second law for $H=p^2/(2m)+V$;
- Liouville for the transported ensemble;
- WKB/stationary-phase correspondence.

## Limitations

The theorem depends on the Madelung polarisation supplied by prior chapters.
It fails or changes form at nodes, abrupt boundaries, microscopic cores and
caustics. A single classical trajectory requires additional localisation in
phase space.
