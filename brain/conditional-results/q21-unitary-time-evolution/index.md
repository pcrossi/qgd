# Q21 unitary time evolution

If OS reconstruction supplies $H=H^\dagger$ on $\mathcal H_{\rm phys}$, then

$$
U(t)=e^{-itH/\hbar}
$$

is unitary and preserves the physical inner product. Dissipation in $\tau$ is
geometric flow, not physical loss of norm in $t$.

The manuscript now contains the self-contained proof in
`manuscrito/08_hilbert_quantization_uncertainty/notes/unitariedade_tempo_fisico_e_setores_abertos.md`.
It also distinguishes projected/open-sector decay:

$$
H_{\rm eff}=H_{PP}+\Delta H-\frac{i}{2}\Gamma
$$

from fundamental non-unitarity. Such non-Hermitian generators are reductions
obtained after ignoring channels, while the closed total sector remains
unitary when its Hamiltonian is self-adjoint.
