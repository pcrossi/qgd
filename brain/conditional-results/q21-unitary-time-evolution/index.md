# Q21 unitary time evolution

If OS reconstruction supplies $H=H^\dagger$ on $\mathcal H_{\rm phys}$, then

$$
U(t)=e^{-itH/\hbar}
$$

is unitary and preserves the physical inner product. Dissipation in $\tau$ is
geometric flow, not physical loss of norm in $t$.
