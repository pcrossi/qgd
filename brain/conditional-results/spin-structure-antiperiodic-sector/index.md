# Spin structure and antiperiodic sector

## Demonstrated part

Since

$$
TM\simeq M\times\mathbb R^8,
$$

one has

$$
w_2(TM)=0.
$$

Therefore $M$ admits spin structures. For $T^4$,

$$
H^1(T^4,\mathbb Z_2)\simeq(\mathbb Z_2)^4,
$$

so there are

$$
2^4=16
$$

spin structures.

## Physical sector chosen

The current fermiionic minimal sector is

$$
\boldsymbol\varepsilon_F=(1,0,0,0).
$$

On the active cycle,

$$
\psi(\theta_1+2\pi)=-\psi(\theta_1).
$$

Thus allowed modes contain

$$
n+\frac12
$$

and the circulation is

$$
\oint p_{\theta_1}\,d\theta_1
=h\left(n+\frac12\right).
$$

## Status distinction

The half-integer circulation follows from the antiperiodic sector. The
selection of $\boldsymbol\varepsilon_F$ among the 16 spin structures remains
an axiom of the physical sector unless a later dynamic selection theorem is
proved.

Half-integer circulation alone does not prove fermionic anticommutation or
Pauli exclusion.
