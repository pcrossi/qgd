# Q16 variable diffusion

The GDQ stochastic reduction uses universal $\nu_0=\hbar/(2m_0)$ and
effective $\nu=\nu_0\Omega^{-1}=\hbar/(2m)$.

For variable $\Omega$, Itô calculus gives

$$
\partial_t\rho
=
-\nabla_i(b^i\rho)
+\nu_0\Delta_h(\Omega^{-1}\rho),
$$

and

$$
u^i=\nu(\nabla^i\ln\rho-\nabla^i\ln\Omega).
$$
