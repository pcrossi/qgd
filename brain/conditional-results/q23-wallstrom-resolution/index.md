# Q23 Wallstrom resolution

Wallstrom is resolved by treating the phase as a section of a $U(1)$ line
bundle over $M^\ast=M\setminus Z_\rho$. The integral class

$$
c_1(L)=\left[\frac{F_A}{2\pi}\right]\in H^2(M^\ast,\mathbb Z)
$$

forces integer circulation. Non-integer circulation is not a globally
admissible state in the chosen sector.
