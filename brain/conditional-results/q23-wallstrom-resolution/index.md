# Q23 Wallstrom resolution

Wallstrom is resolved by treating the phase as a section of a $U(1)$ line
bundle over $M^\ast=M\setminus Z_\rho$. The integral class

$$
c_1(L)=\left[\frac{F_A}{2\pi}\right]\in H^2(M^\ast,\mathbb Z)
$$

forces integer circulation. Non-integer circulation is not a globally
admissible state in the chosen sector.

Manuscript status: self-contained in Chapter 8, especially
`manuscrito/08_hilbert_quantization_uncertainty/notes/wallstrom_fibrado_linha_u1.md`.
The proof includes the regular domain `M*=M\\Z_rho`, local phases, U(1)
transition functions, cocycle condition, Chern class integrality, integer
holonomy around cycles, exclusion of non-integer circulations, and treatment of
nodes as removed defects/topological boundaries. Poisson summation is only a
consequence of S1 harmonic analysis, not the origin of quantization.
