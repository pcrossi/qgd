# Weighted metric equation

## Statement

Metric variation of the official GDQ action gives a weighted metric-dilatonic
bulk equation. It is not simply Einstein’s equation renamed.

## Equation

In the normalized problem:

$$
\begin{aligned}
0={}&\tau\mathcal U
\left(
\mathcal R_{AB}+P_{AB}^{(f)}
\right)
\\
&+\tau\left(
g_{AB}\Delta_g\mathcal U
-\nabla_A\nabla_B\mathcal U
\right)
\\
&-\frac12\mathcal U
\left(
\mathcal L_0-\lambda(\tau)
\right)g_{AB}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The derivative terms of `U` are structurally essential.

## Torsion status

Bismut torsion is not an arbitrary independent field in this variation. If
the complex structure or Bismut connection is varied independently, the
admissible Hermitian-Bismut class must be declared and the Hessian recomputed.
