# Q10 Madelung continuity

In the canonical Madelung reduction,

$$
I_{\rm Mad}
=
\int d\lambda\int_{\Sigma_\lambda}
\rho
\left[
\partial_\lambda S_R
+\frac12G^{AB}\partial_A S_R\partial_BS_R
+V_{\rm eff}
\right]
d\mu_g,
$$

variation with respect to $S_R$ yields

$$
\partial_\lambda\rho+\nabla_A(\rho v^A)=0,
\qquad
v^A=G^{AB}\partial_BS_R.
$$

With variable measure:

$$
\partial_\lambda(\rho\sqrt g)
+\partial_A(\rho v^A\sqrt g)
=0.
$$

This is a result of the selected physical polarization/reduction, not a
universal off-shell identity of the official action.
