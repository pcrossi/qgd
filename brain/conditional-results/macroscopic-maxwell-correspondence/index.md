# Macroscopic Maxwell correspondence

## Statement

Given a transported primitive $U(1)_Q$ mode, an effective connection $A$ and a
quadratic Hessian restriction with coefficient $Z_Q$, the macroscopic local
sector has

$$
F=dA,
$$

so

$$
dF=0.
$$

The quadratic action

$$
I_Q[A]
=-\frac{Z_Q}{4}\int F_{ab}F^{ab}\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x
+\int A_aJ_Q^a\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x
$$

gives the sourced Maxwell equation after variation.

## Status

The Maxwell form is conditionally established as a sectoral correspondence.
It is not a new fundamental action; it is the two-derivative physical Hessian
restriction of GDQ.

## Open normalisation

$Z_Q$, $\alpha$, $\varepsilon_0$ and $\mu_0$ require the global Hessian norm,
Schur complement and unit synchronisation. The chapter does not evaluate
them.
