# Q7 OS Lorentzian time reconstruction

If effective Euclidean GDQ Schwinger functions satisfy OS1--OS5, then

$$
\mathcal H=\overline{\mathcal D_+/\mathcal N},
\qquad
T_E(a)=e^{-aH/\hbar},
\qquad
H=H^\dagger\ge0,
\qquad
U(t)=e^{-itH/\hbar}.
$$

This proves Lorentzian quantum time evolution in that sector. It is not proved
by $t=-i\tau$, by $J^2=-1$, or by the Sudarshan contour alone.
