# Q18 soliton criterion

A particle soliton requires a validated sheet
$\mathfrak S_P=(g_P,B_P,f_P,\bar f_P)$ satisfying stationary equations,
finite energy, mass, charge, spin, stable Hessian, controlled zero modes,
asymptotics and interaction/scattering data.

The Gaussian soliton is the neutral minimal explicit solution, not proof of
charged physical particles.
