# Q19 monotonicity and stability

Monotonicity of $\mathcal F_T$ or $\mathcal W_T$ is a Lyapunov statement. It
implies stability only together with a true critical point, correct boundary
conditions, preserved topology and nonnegative physical Hessian/Jacobi
spectrum after zero-mode removal.
