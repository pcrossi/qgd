# Q11 Hamilton-Jacobi-Bohm

In the Madelung canonical reduction, the Fisher term

$$
\frac{\hbar^2}{8m}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^2}{\rho}
$$

has variational derivative

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{\Delta\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho}.
$$

Therefore variation in $\rho$ yields the Hamilton--Jacobi--Bohm equation.
This depends on the same reduction and boundary conditions as Q10.
