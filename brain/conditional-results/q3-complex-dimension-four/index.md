# Q3 complex dimension four

## Statement

Given the Q2 structural definition

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4,
$$

the real dimension is

$$
\dim_{\mathbb R}M=4+4=8.
$$

Equipping the eight real directions in four complex pairs gives

$$
\dim_{\mathbb C}M=4.
$$

## Logical status

The bulk choice is structural/axiomatic. The complex dimension follows from
that choice. There is no current derivation showing that all other dimensions
are dynamically impossible.

## Relation to Q2

Question 03 does not reopen the Q2 definition of the bulk. It records the
logical consequence of that definition.
