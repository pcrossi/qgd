# Q5 effective coupling programme

Q5 closes the dictionary of fields, but it does not derive all effective
physics from that dictionary.

The remaining programme is:

1. derive geometric beta-functions or their GDQ-native replacements;
2. prove perturbative regularity beyond the declared auxiliary sectors;
3. demonstrate explicitly how the Sudarshan contour and the physical Hessian
   project non-physical modes without making ghosts ontological;
4. calculate effective couplings and observable constants from geometry.

These are later-question tasks and do not reopen the field ontology.
