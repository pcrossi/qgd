# Canonical Madelung selection

## Problem

The official action directly gives

$$
\Pi_{S_R}
=n_\mu\widehat J_S^\mu
=\frac{2\tau}{\hbar\Lambda_C^2}
\mathcal U
n_\mu g^{\mu\bar\nu}\partial_{\bar\nu}S_R,
$$

not `Pi_{S_R}=rho` in general.

Therefore the canonical Madelung term

$$
\rho\,\partial_tS_R
$$

is not an automatic off-shell consequence of the official action.

## Required closure

One must show, in the physical bridge/measurement sector, that:

1. the pullback produces the correct temporal coefficient;
2. the relevant coefficient `A` is constant on the support or its variation is
   accounted for;
3. lapse, shift and internal modes do not introduce uncancelled cross terms;
4. no lateral flux leaks out of the support;
5. the primitive charge sector `Q_S=1` is fixed independently;
6. the physical dynamics selects the Routh minimizer or an equivalent
   canonical polarization.

## Status

Open/conditional. The state-space symplectic form and the conditional Routh
minimizer are established, but the general dynamical selection from the
official GDQ Cauchy space remains a later bridge/measurement task.
