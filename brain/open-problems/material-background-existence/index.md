# Material background existence

## Problem

Chapter 2 defines what a GDQ material background must satisfy, but does not
prove universal existence and stability of all such backgrounds.

For each concrete family, one must close:

$$
\text{official action}
\longrightarrow
\text{admissible background}
\longrightarrow
\text{physical Hessian}
\longrightarrow
\text{operator and domain}
\longrightarrow
\text{boundary conditions}
\longrightarrow
\text{stable spectrum}
\longrightarrow
\text{observable}.
$$

## What remains open

- existence of each solitonic family;
- uniqueness modulo symmetries;
- separation of gauge and collective zero modes;
- Hessian positivity in the physical sector;
- nonlinear stability when needed;
- quantitative observable normalization.

## Status

Open sector-by-sector problem. Chapter 2 gives the closure protocol, not a
universal existence theorem.
