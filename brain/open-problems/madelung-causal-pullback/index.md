# Madelung causal pullback

## Problem

The official action gives a phase momentum

$$
\Pi_{S_R}
=n_\mu\widehat J_S^\mu,
$$

not automatically

$$
\Pi_{S_R}=\rho.
$$

The laboratory Madelung action requires the canonical sector in which

$$
\Pi_{S_R}^{\rm lab}=\rho_{\rm lab}.
$$

## What is already derived

- The bulk phase current.
- The covariant continuity equation.
- The 3+1 laboratory balance law.
- The Kähler state-space canonical form
  $\Theta_{\rm state}=\int\rho\,\delta S_R$.
- The Routh minimizer condition under explicit convex-sector hypotheses.

## What remains open

One still needs to derive the physical causal pullback and/or measurement
polarization that selects the Madelung sector from the full GDQ Cauchy data.

The exponential clock law

$$
\tau_\gamma(t)=\tau_0e^{\kappa t}
$$

follows if the clock map is a continuous homomorphism from translations to
positive dilations. This is valid conditionally, but the physical derivation
of that homomorphism and the value of $\kappa$ remain separate tasks.
