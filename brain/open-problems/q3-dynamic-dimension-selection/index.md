# Q3 dynamic dimension selection

## Problem

The current GDQ has not proven a dynamic mechanism that selects

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4
$$

or excludes every alternative dimension.

## Not demonstrated

- cancellation of anomalies specifically in $n=4$;
- failure of $n\neq4$;
- a complete chiral spectrum for the anomaly calculation;
- a polynomial of anomaly for the final noncompact geometry;
- an index theorem application with domain and boundary conditions.

## Status

Open research programme. This does not reopen the Q3 definitional closure.
