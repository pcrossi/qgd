# Q2 Ricci-Bismut spectral programme

## Problem

Question 02 defines the mathematical arena but does not compute observed
masses, charges or coupling constants.

The remaining quantitative programme is to determine a stationary background

$$
(g_*,B_*,f_*,R_{a,*})
$$

and compute the relevant spectra and normalisations:

$$
\operatorname{Spec}(\slashed D_{B_*}),
\qquad
\operatorname{Spec}(\Delta_{B_*}),
\qquad
G_{ab,*},
\qquad
\kappa_{8,*}.
$$

Predictions then take the schematic form

$$
m_j^2c^2
=m_8^2c^2
+\hbar^2\lambda_j(\slashed D_{B_*}^2),
$$

with charges and $\alpha$ determined by the gauge direction and normalization.

## Important restriction

Experimental values must not be used as hidden inputs in a claim of first
principles derivation. Stabilisation of internal radii and gauge
normalisation must be calculated.
