# Perturbative all-orders finiteness

## Problem

Chapter 4 establishes ultraviolet damping and no new poles in declared
quadratic/heat-kernel sectors. It does not yet prove all-order finiteness of
the full GDQ perturbation theory.

## Missing chain

To close all-orders perturbative finiteness, one must derive from the
official action:

1. the physical Hessian on the chosen background;
2. the complete interaction vertices beyond quadratic order;
3. uniform high-frequency estimates for all vertices and propagators;
4. treatment of gauge quotient, zero modes, boundaries and measure anomalies;
5. multi-loop estimates showing that damping factors are not cancelled;
6. stability under admissible singular or interface backgrounds.

## Current status

Open. The quadratic sector is structurally promising and conditionally finite,
but a universal theorem requires the steps above.
