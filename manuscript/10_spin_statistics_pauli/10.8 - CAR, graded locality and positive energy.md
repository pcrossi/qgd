# 10.8. CAR, graded locality and positive energy

The spin-statistics theorem belongs to the local, Lorentzian, spinor, positive inner product, and positive energy effective sector. Under these hypotheses, half-integer spin fields obey anticommutation relations.

At equal times:

$$
\{\widehat\psi_\alpha(t,\mathbf x),
\widehat\psi_\beta^\dagger(t,\mathbf y)\}
=
\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf y),
$$

and:

$$
\{\widehat\psi_\alpha(t,\mathbf x),
\widehat\psi_\beta(t,\mathbf y)\}
=0.
$$

At space-like separation:

$$
\{\widehat\psi_\alpha(x),\widehat\psi_\beta(y)\}=0.
$$

Even physical observables commute at space-like separation:

$$
[A(O_1),B(O_2)]=0,
\qquad
O_1\perp_h O_2.
$$

This is graded locality. It preserves observable causality without treating odd fermionic fields as directly measurable observables.

In the positive energy sector:

$$
\mathcal F_-(\mathcal H_1^+)
=
\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty}\wedge^n\mathcal H_1^+.
$$

This construction recovers fermionic statistics without postulating the Standard Model as a fundamental ontology.

The complete proof, with hypotheses, causal cone, positive energy, graded locality, and relationship with holonomy, is in the note [[notes/conditional_spin_statistics_theorem|Conditional spin-statistics theorem in GDQ]].
