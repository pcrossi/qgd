# Spin structure in $\mathbb R^4\times T^4$

## Statement

The official local bulk of GDQ:

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4
$$

admits a spin structure.

## Proof

Both $\mathbb R^4$ and $T^4$ are parallelizable. Thus:

$$
T\mathbb R^4\simeq\mathbb R^4\times\mathbb R^4,
\qquad
TT^4\simeq T^4\times\mathbb R^4.
$$

For the product:

$$
TM\simeq M\times\mathbb R^8.
$$

A trivial vector bundle has all its higher Stiefel-Whitney classes equal to zero. In particular:

$$
w_2(TM)=0.
$$

The necessary and sufficient condition for the existence of a spin structure on an orientable manifold is:

$$
w_2(TM)=0.
$$

Therefore, $M$ admits a spin structure.

The inequivalent spin structures are classified by:

$$
H^1(M,\mathbb Z_2)
\simeq
H^1(T^4,\mathbb Z_2)
\simeq
(\mathbb Z_2)^4.
$$

Thus, there are:

$$
2^4=16
$$

inequivalent spin structures in the product local bulk.

## Scope

This proves existence in the official local bulk. For a more general physical Lorentzian leaf $(N,h)$, the condition $w_2(TN)=0$ must be verified or inherited.
