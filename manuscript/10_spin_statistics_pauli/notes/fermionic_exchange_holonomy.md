# Fermionic exchange holonomy

## Statement

The exchange of two identical fermionic solitons corresponds to a loop in the configuration space with holonomy $-1$:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma=-1.
$$

## Construction

In the sector of two identical objects, the physical configuration identifies:

$$
(x_1,x_2)\sim(x_2,x_1).
$$

An exchange is a closed path in the quotient space. If the accumulated phase is:

$$
\oint_\gamma dS_R=(2k+1)\pi\hbar,
$$

then:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma
=
\exp\left(
\frac{i}{\hbar}\oint_\gamma dS_R
\right)
=
e^{i(2k+1)\pi}
=
-1.
$$

Thus:

$$
\Psi(x_2,x_1)=-\Psi(x_1,x_2).
$$

## GDQ Interpretation

The real measure remains positive:

$$
\rho>0.
$$

The fermionic sign lives in the phase/holonomy:

$$
e^{iS_R/\hbar}.
$$

This is compatible with the treatment of the sign problem: the sign is not a negative weight of the positive measure, but a topological data of the phase sector.

## Scope

Holonomy $-1$ explains antisymmetry in the geometric sector. The complete proof of spin-statistics still requires locality, positive energy, and an effective spinor structure.
