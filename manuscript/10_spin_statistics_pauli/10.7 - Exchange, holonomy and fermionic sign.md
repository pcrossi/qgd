# 10.7. Exchange, holonomy and fermionic sign

When two identical solitons are exchanged, the system traces a loop in the configuration space. In the fermionic spinor sector, this loop has holonomy:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma=-1.
$$

In terms of phase:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma
=
\exp\left(
\frac{i}{\hbar}\oint_\gamma dS_R
\right).
$$

For fermionic exchange:

$$
\oint_\gamma dS_R=(2k+1)\pi\hbar.
$$

Thus:

$$
\Psi(r_2,r_1)
=
-\Psi(r_1,r_2).
$$

This is the geometric reading of antisymmetry. GDQ interprets the fermionic sign as phase/torsion holonomy, not as a negative weight of the real measure:

$$
\rho>0
\quad
\text{and}
\quad
\text{sign in }e^{iS_R/\hbar}.
$$
