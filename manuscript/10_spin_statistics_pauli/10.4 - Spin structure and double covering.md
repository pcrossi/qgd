# 10.4. Spin structure and double covering

In the official local bulk:

$$
M=\mathbb R^4\times T^4.
$$

Since $\mathbb R^4$ and $T^4$ are parallelizable, the tangent bundle of $M$ is trivial:

$$
TM\simeq M\times\mathbb R^8.
$$

Thus:

$$
w_2(TM)=0.
$$

Hence, the bulk admits a spin structure.

In the effective physical spacetime $(N,h)$, the fermionic sector requires a lift of the orthonormal bundle:

$$
P_{\rm Spin}(N)\to P_{\rm SO}(N).
$$

The relevant double covering is:

$$
\mathrm{Spin}^+(3,1)\simeq SL(2,\mathbb C)
\to
SO^+(3,1).
$$

In the spatial subgroup:

$$
\mathrm{Spin}(3)\simeq SU(2)\to SO(3).
$$

The kernel is:

$$
\ker\pi=\{\pm1\}.
$$

Therefore, a rotation that is the identity in $SO(3)$ can act as $-I$ on the spinor. This is the mathematical mechanism of spin $1/2$.
