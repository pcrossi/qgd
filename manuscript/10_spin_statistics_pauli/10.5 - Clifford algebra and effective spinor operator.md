# 10.5. Clifford algebra and effective spinor operator

The spin $1/2$ field in the physical sector is a section of a spinor bundle:

$$
\psi\in\Gamma(S\otimes E).
$$

Here $S$ is the spinor bundle and $E$ carries effective internal data. The Lorentzian local sector uses the Clifford algebra:

$$
\{\gamma^a,\gamma^b\}=2\eta^{ab}I.
$$

In curved coordinates:

$$
\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2h^{\mu\nu}I.
$$

The spinor generators are:

$$
\Sigma^{ab}=\frac14[\gamma^a,\gamma^b].
$$

The effective spinor operator with Bismut/Cartan torsion and internal coupling is:

$$
\slashed D_{B,A}
=
\gamma^\mu
\left(
\nabla_\mu^{\rm LC}
+
\frac18B_{\mu\nu\lambda}\gamma^{\nu\lambda}
-iq_aA_\mu^a
\right).
$$

This operator is not a new fundamental action. It is the effective/projected form of the reconstructed spinor sector of GDQ. Its principal symbol satisfies:

$$
(\gamma^\mu k_\mu)^2=h^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu.
$$

Thus, spinors propagate in the same physical causal cone determined by $h$.
