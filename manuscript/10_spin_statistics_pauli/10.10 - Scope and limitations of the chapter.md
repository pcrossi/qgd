# 10.10. Scope and limitations of the chapter

The chapter closes the structure of spin, fermionic statistics, and Pauli in the physical effective sector of GDQ.

What is closed:

1. spin as circulation/torsion is preserved as the proper interpretation of GDQ;
2. spin $1/2$ is realized by a spinor structure and double covering;
3. the half-monodromy is compatible with Hopf and Cauchy residue;
4. the $2\pi$ rotation produces $-1$ and $4\pi$ produces $+1$;
5. the fermionic exchange is a $-1$ holonomy;
6. CAR provide fermionic statistics;
7. Pauli follows from $(a_i^\dagger)^2=0$;
8. the Bohm barrier is the geometric manifestation of the node.

What remains conditional:

1. Lorentzian reconstruction and positive energy of the physical sector;
2. dynamical selection of the spin sector in general material backgrounds;
3. metrological calculation of torsion/spin couplings for composite objects.

These points do not reopen the structural theorem. They delimit where the chapter ends and where applications to detectors, composite matter, and metrological spectra begin.
