# 25.5 — Galactic acceleration and horizon scale

The galactic acceleration scale of GDQ is:

$$
a_0^{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{c^2}{2\pi R_H}
=
\frac{cH_0}{2\pi}.
$$

It is the circular projection of the horizon acceleration $a_H=c^2/R_H=cH_0$. The phenomenological value of MOND/RAR does not enter the deduction; it only enters the comparison.

For:

$$
H_0=67.4\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},
$$

we have:

$$
a_0^{\rm GDQ}
=
1.042197881145\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}.
$$

For:

$$
H_0=73\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},
$$

we have:

$$
a_0^{\rm GDQ}
=
1.128789989964\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}.
$$

Compared to the typical phenomenological scale of $1.20\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}$, the differences are:

| Boundary | $a_0$ | Relative error |
|---|---:|---:|
| $H_0=67.4$ | $1.042197881145\times10^{-10}$ | $-13.150177\%$ |
| $H_0=73$ | $1.128789989964\times10^{-10}$ | $-5.934168\%$ |

The auxiliary de Sitter scale is different:

$$
a_{\rm dS}^{(2\pi)}
=
\frac{cH_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}}{2\pi}
=
8.623833237863\times10^{-11}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}},
$$

for $H_0=67.4$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847$.

GDQ is not fundamental MOND. It contains a galactic limit of the MOND/Tully--Fisher type:

$$
v^4\sim GM_ba_0^{\rm GDQ}.
$$

But cluster lensing, growth of structure, and the CMB require the dark/torsional geometric sector:

$$
\Theta_{\mu\nu}^{(H)}.
$$

This sector must be calculated using the physical gravitational Hessian:

$$
K_{\rm grav}^{\rm phys}
=
P_{\rm grav}^{\rm phys}
\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
P_{\rm grav}^{\rm phys}.
$$

Therefore, the conservative astrophysical conclusion is: the critical galactic scale is structurally fixed by the global boundary, while the metrology of lensing, clusters, and the CMB requires solving the physical gravitational Hessian in the corresponding background.
