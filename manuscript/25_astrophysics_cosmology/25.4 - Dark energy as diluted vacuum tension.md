# 25.4 — Dark energy as diluted vacuum tension

Dark energy appears in GDQ as stable UV tension diluted by the global boundary. The preserved reduced expression is:

$$
\rho_\Lambda^{\rm GDQ}
=
\alpha^2
\cdot
28
\cdot
\frac{M_pc^2}{(4\pi/3)r_p^3}
\cdot
\frac{r_p}{R_H}
\cdot
\frac{1}{c^2}.
$$

Each factor has a function:

| Factor | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\frac{M_pc^2}{(4\pi/3)r_p^3}$ | stable baryonic UV tension |
| $\frac{r_p}{R_H}$ | linear dilution by the cosmological boundary |
| $28$ | number of curvature/tension channels in $\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^8)$ |
| $\alpha^2$ | global--local projection inherited from charge normalization |
| $1/c^2$ | conversion from energy density to mass density |

With:

$$
H_0=67.4\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},
\qquad
\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847,
$$

and:

$$
R_H=\frac{c}{H_0},
$$

we obtain:

$$
\rho_\Lambda^{\rm GDQ}
=
6.136532599384\times10^{-27}\,{\rm kg\,m^{-3}}.
$$

The reference density corresponding to the same cosmological boundary is:

$$
\rho_{\rm ref}
=
5.842445930612\times10^{-27}\,{\rm kg\,m^{-3}}.
$$

The relative error is:

$$
5.033622\%.
$$

This comparison must be read correctly. GDQ provides a strong structural scale, but the absolute value depends on the adopted cosmological boundary. No local theory calculates the observed universe without specifying the boundary of the problem.
