# Note — Black hole as soliton with horizon

The central regularity requirement is:

$$
m(r)=m_3r^3+O(r^5).
$$

Then:

$$
A(r)
=
1-\frac{2Gm_3}{c^2}r^2+O(r^4).
$$

Defining:

$$
\Lambda_{\rm core}
=
\frac{6Gm_3}{c^2},
$$

we obtain:

$$
A(r)
=
1-\frac{\Lambda_{\rm core}}{3}r^2+O(r^4).
$$

The invariants of the de Sitter core are finite:

$$
R(0)=4\Lambda_{\rm core},
$$

$$
R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}(0)=4\Lambda_{\rm core}^2,
$$

$$
K(0)=\frac83\Lambda_{\rm core}^2.
$$

In GDQ, the profile $m(r)$ must be obtained from the density, torsion, and Bohm pressure of the background $\Phi_{\rm BH,*}$. The reduced form only verifies that the regular class has the correct geometric structure.
