# Note — Dark energy and galactic acceleration

The baryonic UV tension used in the dark energy estimation is:

$$
\rho_{\rm UV}
=
\frac{M_pc^2}{(4\pi/3)r_p^3}.
$$

The global dilution is:

$$
\frac{r_p}{R_H}.
$$

With $N_C=28$ channels and projection $\alpha^2$:

$$
\rho_\Lambda^{\rm GDQ}
=
\alpha^2N_C\rho_{\rm UV}\frac{r_p}{R_H}\frac{1}{c^2}.
$$

The count is:

$$
N_C
=
\dim\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^8)
=
\binom82
=
28.
$$

The linear factor comes from the global weight:

$$
f(r)\sim\ln\left(\frac{r}{r_p}\right),
\qquad
e^{-f}=\frac{r_p}{r}.
$$

Thus:

$$
\rho_{\rm diluida}\propto\frac{r_p}{R_H}.
$$

At the boundary $H_0=67.4\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, $\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847$ and $r_p=0.840778765450\,{\rm fm}$:

$$
\rho_\Lambda^{\rm GDQ}
=
6.136532599384\times10^{-27}\,{\rm kg\,m^{-3}},
$$

against:

$$
\rho_\Lambda^{\rm obs}
=
5.842445930612\times10^{-27}\,{\rm kg\,m^{-3}},
$$

with error:

$$
5.033622\%.
$$

The GDQ galactic acceleration is the circular horizon scale:

$$
a_0^{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{c^2}{2\pi R_H}
=
\frac{cH_0}{2\pi}.
$$

The deduction is:

$$
R_H=\frac{c}{H_0},
\qquad
a_H=\frac{c^2}{R_H}=cH_0,
\qquad
a_0^{\rm GDQ}=\frac{a_H}{2\pi}.
$$

The de Sitter scale:

$$
\frac{cH_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}}{2\pi}
$$

is different and must not be confused with $a_0^{\rm GDQ}$.

With $H_0=67.4\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$:

$$
a_0^{\rm GDQ}
=
1.042197881145\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}.
$$

With $H_0=73\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$:

$$
a_0^{\rm GDQ}
=
1.128789989964\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}.
$$
