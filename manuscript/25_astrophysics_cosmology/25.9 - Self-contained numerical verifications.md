# 25.9 — Self-contained numerical verifications

The scripts of this chapter are located in [[scripts/README]]. They were written as self-contained verifiers, without depending on historical audits.

| Script | Verification |
|---|---|
| `reduced_black_hole.py` | regular core, horizons, reduced Hessian, and toy Page curve |
| `reduced_black_hole_pipeline.py` | radial saddle, effective TOV, virial, block-wise Hessian, and Schur consistency |
| `gdq_cosmology_scales.py` | $\rho_\Lambda$, $a_0$, and horizon scales |
| `electroweak_proton_radius.py` | $v_{\rm GDQ}$, reduced $W/Z$, and $r_p^{\rm surf}$ |

These scripts preserve the final/reduced versions, not unsuccessful attempts. Each Markdown output states its scientific classification.
