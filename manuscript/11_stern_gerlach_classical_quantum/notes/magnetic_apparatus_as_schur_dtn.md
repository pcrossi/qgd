# Magnetic apparatus as Schur/DtN

## Statement

The Stern--Gerlach apparatus enters as a classical magnetic source/boundary and induces an effective interface response:

$$
\mathsf R_{\rm SG}
=
K_{\partial\partial}
-
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

## Construction

Divide the fluctuations into boundary data and internal degrees of freedom of the apparatus:

$$
\delta\Phi=(\delta\Phi_\partial,\delta\Phi_I).
$$

The reduced Hessian has blocks:

$$
K=
\begin{pmatrix}
K_{\partial\partial} & K_{\partial I}\\
K_{I\partial} & K_{II}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Eliminating the stationary internal degrees of freedom yields the Schur complement:

$$
\mathsf R_{\rm SG}
=
K_{\partial\partial}
-
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

## Scope

For the chapter, the structural form is sufficient. The calculation of $\mathsf R_{\rm SG}$ for a real magnet is a metrological task dependent on material, geometry, and field profile.
