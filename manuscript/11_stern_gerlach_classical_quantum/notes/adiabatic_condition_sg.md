# Adiabatic condition

## Statement

The clean separation into two channels requires that non-adiabatic transitions be small:

$$
\left|
\frac{
\langle -;\mathbf n(t)|\dot H_{\rm int}|+;\mathbf n(t)\rangle
}{
\Delta E(t)^2
}
\right|
\ll1.
$$

## Interpretation

$\Delta E(t)$ is the gap between the two channels:

$$
\Delta E(t)=E_+(t)-E_-(t).
$$

If the direction $\mathbf n(t)$ changes slowly, the state remains in the instantaneous eigen-bundle. If it changes rapidly, transitions occur.

In GDQ language, the variation of the magnetic boundary must be slow compared to the internal stiffness that separates the two sectors of circulation.

## Scope

This condition defines the domain of validity of the classical deflection per channel. A real apparatus outside this regime requires solving the complete interface dynamics.
