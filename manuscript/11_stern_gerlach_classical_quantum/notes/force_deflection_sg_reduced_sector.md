# Force and deflection in the reduced sector

## Statement

In a fixed adiabatic channel, the reduced interface energy:

$$
E_\pm(\mathbf x)=\mp\mu|\mathbf B(\mathbf x)|
$$

generates the force:

$$
\mathbf F_\pm=\pm\mu\nabla|\mathbf B|.
$$

## Proof

By definition:

$$
\mathbf F_\pm=-\nabla E_\pm.
$$

Substituting:

$$
\mathbf F_\pm
=
-\nabla(\mp\mu|\mathbf B|)
=
\pm\mu\nabla|\mathbf B|.
$$

If the field is aligned with $z$ and the dominant gradient is vertical:

$$
|\mathbf B|\simeq B_z,
$$

then:

$$
m\ddot z
=
\kappa\mu\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial z}.
$$

For longitudinal velocity $v_y$ and magnet length $L$:

$$
t=\frac{L}{v_y}.
$$

With approximately constant acceleration:

$$
\Delta z
=
\frac12\ddot z\,t^2.
$$

Thus:

$$
\Delta z
=
\kappa
\frac{\mu L^2}{2mv_y^2}
\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial z}.
$$

## Scope

This is a classical center-of-mass reduction in a fixed channel. It does not calculate the weights of the channels.
