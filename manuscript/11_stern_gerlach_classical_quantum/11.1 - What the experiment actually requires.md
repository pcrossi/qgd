# 11.1. What the experiment actually requires

The Stern--Gerlach experiment requires explaining four facts:

1. the beam splits into two branches;
2. each branch has a well-defined mechanical trajectory;
3. the fraction of particles in each branch depends on the axis of the apparatus;
4. sequential measurements along different axes do not reveal simultaneous pre-existing values.

An incomplete explanation would merely say:

$$
\kappa=\pm1.
$$

This explains two branches for a fixed axis, but does not explain the complete experiment. The axis is chosen by the apparatus:

$$
\mathbf n=\frac{\mathbf B}{|\mathbf B|}.
$$

Thus, the correct label is:

$$
\kappa_{\mathbf n}=\pm1.
$$

The chapter must, therefore, explain the response of the object to the magnetic boundary and the statistical distribution between the two channels.
