# 11.6. Angular probabilities and operational Born

A preparation oriented by $\mathbf a$ is represented locally by:

$$
\varrho_{\mathbf a}
=
\frac12
\left(I+\mathbf a\cdot\sigma\right).
$$

The apparatus measures along the axis $\mathbf n$, with projectors:

$$
P_{\mathbf n}^{\pm}
=
\frac12
\left(I\pm\mathbf n\cdot\sigma\right).
$$

By the operational Born rule:

$$
p_\pm(\mathbf n|\mathbf a)
=
\operatorname{Tr}
\left(
\varrho_{\mathbf a}P_{\mathbf n}^{\pm}
\right).
$$

Using:

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_i\sigma_j)=2\delta_{ij},
$$

we obtain:

$$
p_\pm(\mathbf n|\mathbf a)
=
\frac{1\pm\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n}{2}.
$$

If $\theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf n$:

$$
p_+=\cos^2\frac\theta2,
\qquad
p_-=\sin^2\frac\theta2.
$$

Thus:

$$
\text{two channels do not imply equal weights.}
$$

The weights depend on the preparation and on the axis of the apparatus.
