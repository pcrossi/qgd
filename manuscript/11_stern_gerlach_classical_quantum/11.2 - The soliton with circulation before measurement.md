The apparatus does not inject this structure into the object. It merely selects a decomposition relative to the external field.

$$
u\in S^3\subset\mathbb C^2.
$$

The common phase does not change the physical direction:

$$
u\sim e^{i\varphi}u.
$$

Therefore:

$$
S^3/U(1)\simeq\mathbb{CP}^1\simeq S^2.
$$

This $S^2$ is the space of orientations. The field of the apparatus chooses a point $\mathbf n$ in this space and decomposes the state into the two associated eigen-bundles.
