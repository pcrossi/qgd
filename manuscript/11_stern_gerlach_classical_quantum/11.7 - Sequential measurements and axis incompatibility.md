# 11.7. Sequential measurements and axis incompatibility

After selecting result $s=\pm1$ along the axis $\mathbf a$, the sub-beam is in the sector:

$$
\varrho_{\mathbf a,s}=P_{\mathbf a}^{s}.
$$

If it passes through a new apparatus along the axis $\mathbf b$, the probability of obtaining $s'=\pm1$ is:

$$
p(s'|s;\mathbf b,\mathbf a)
=
\operatorname{Tr}
\left(
P_{\mathbf a}^{s}P_{\mathbf b}^{s'}
\right).
$$

The result is:

$$
p(s'|s;\mathbf b,\mathbf a)
=
\frac{1+ss'\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{2}.
$$

Examples:

1. $z+$ followed by $z$ gives $z+$ with probability $1$;
2. $z+$ followed by $x$ gives $x+$ and $x-$ with probability $1/2$;
3. if we then select $x+$ and measure $z$, $z+$ and $z-$ reappear with probability $1/2$.

This shows that the intermediate apparatus changes the decomposition into eigen-bundles. It does not reveal a pre-existing list of simultaneous values for all axes.
