# 11.4. Hopf, apparatus axis and two projectors

For the local direction of the apparatus:

$$
\mathbf n=\frac{\mathbf B}{|\mathbf B|},
$$

the two channels are:

$$
P_{\mathbf n}^{\pm}
=
\frac12
\left(I\pm\mathbf n\cdot\sigma\right).
$$

They satisfy:

$$
(P_{\mathbf n}^{\pm})^2=P_{\mathbf n}^{\pm},
\qquad
P_{\mathbf n}^{+}P_{\mathbf n}^{-}=0,
\qquad
P_{\mathbf n}^{+}+P_{\mathbf n}^{-}=I.
$$

The spin observable along the axis of the apparatus is:

$$
S_{\mathbf n}
=
\frac\hbar2\mathbf n\cdot\sigma.
$$

Since:

$$
(\mathbf n\cdot\sigma)^2=I,
$$

the eigenvalues are:

$$
\operatorname{spec}(S_{\mathbf n})
=
\left\{-\frac\hbar2,+\frac\hbar2\right\}.
$$

The chapter uses matrices $\sigma_i$ as a local basis of the Hopf/Clifford bundle of the effective sector, not as an ontology external to the model.
