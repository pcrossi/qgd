# 11.3. The apparatus as source and magnetic boundary

The magnetic field of the apparatus is a classical external given. It enters as a source, constraint, or interface boundary. It is not a new fundamental term of the official action.

The chain is:

$$
J_{\rm app}^{\rm magnetic}
\to
\delta\Phi_{\rm app}
\to
\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\mathsf R_{\rm SG}
\to
\text{channels}.
$$

In a linear reduction, the effective impedance of the apparatus can be written as:

$$
\mathsf R_{\rm SG}
=
K_{\partial\partial}
-
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

In the limit where the reduced response is equivalent to a magnetic dipole:

$$
V_{\rm SG}
=
-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B.
$$

This expression is an interface reduction. It does not replace the GDQ action.

The complete construction of the source, the physical Hessian, the Schur complement, and the DtN operator is recorded in [[notes/background_hessian_and_dtn_sg|Background, Hessian and DtN in Stern-Gerlach]]. The essential point is that $\mathsf R_{\rm SG}$ is not a free parameter of the object: it is the effective response obtained by eliminating the unmonitored internal degrees of freedom after the apparatus provides the classical boundary.
