# 11.5. Force and deflection of center of mass

In the adiabatic regime, the internal orientation tracks the local axis of the field. The two channels have energy:

$$
E_\pm(\mathbf x)=\mp\mu|\mathbf B(\mathbf x)|.
$$

The center-of-mass force is:

$$
\mathbf F_\pm
=
-\nabla E_\pm
=
\pm\mu\nabla|\mathbf B|.
$$

For a field approximately aligned with $z$:

$$
m\frac{d^2z}{dt^2}
=
\kappa\,\mu\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial z}.
$$

With transit time:

$$
t=\frac{L}{v_y},
$$

the deflection inside the magnet is:

$$
\Delta z
=
\kappa
\frac{\mu L^2}{2mv_y^2}
\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial z}.
$$

This equation describes the trajectory in a fixed channel. It does not state how many particles enter each channel. The weights come from the operational Born rule.
