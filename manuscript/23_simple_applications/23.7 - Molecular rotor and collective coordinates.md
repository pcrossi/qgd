# 23.7. Molecular rotor and collective coordinates

A diatomic molecule is treated as two geometric centers linked by a flux bridge. At low energy, if the fast internal modes are gapped, there remain:

$$
R(t)\in\mathbb R_+,
\qquad
\Omega(t)\in S^2.
$$

The effective quadratic functional is:

$$
L_{\rm eff}
=
\frac{\mu_{\rm GDQ}}{2}\dot R^2
+
\frac{\mu_{\rm GDQ}R^2}{2}|\dot\Omega|^2
-
V_{\rm GDQ}(R)
+\cdots .
$$

At the equilibrium radius $R_0$:

$$
I_0=\mu_{\rm GDQ}R_0^2.
$$

The angular sector is:

$$
K_{\rm ang}
=
-
\frac{\hbar^2}{2I_0}
\Delta_{S^2}.
$$

Since:

$$
-\Delta_{S^2}Y_{Jm}
=
J(J+1)Y_{Jm},
$$

it follows that:

$$
E_J^{(0)}
=
B_{\rm GDQ}J(J+1),
\qquad
B_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar^2}{2I_0}.
$$

The centrifugal distortion comes from radial minimization:

$$
V_{\rm GDQ}(R)
=
V_0
+
\frac12\mu_{\rm GDQ}\omega_e^2(R-R_0)^2
+\cdots .
$$

The reduced result is:

$$
E_J
=
B_{\rm GDQ}J(J+1)
-
D_{\rm GDQ}[J(J+1)]^2
+\cdots,
$$

with:

$$
D_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar^4}
{2\mu_{\rm GDQ}^3\omega_e^2R_0^6}
=
\frac{4B_{\rm GDQ}^3}{\hbar^2\omega_e^2}.
$$

In spectroscopic units, when $B$ and $\omega_e$ are given in ${\rm cm}^{-1}$:

$$
D_{\rm GDQ}
\simeq
\frac{4B_{\rm GDQ}^3}{\omega_e^2}.
$$

The script [[scripts/rotor_distortion_symbolic.py|rotor_distortion_symbolic.py]] symbolically verifies the radial minimization and confirms:

$$
B_L=\frac{1}{2\mu R_0^2},
\qquad
D_L=\frac{1}{2\mu^3\omega_e^2R_0^6}.
$$

Since $L^2=\hbar^2J(J+1)$:

$$
D_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar^4}{2\mu_{\rm GDQ}^3\omega_e^2R_0^6}.
$$

The script [[scripts/reduced_molecular_rotor.py|reduced_molecular_rotor.py]] makes a phenomenological comparison for CO using external $B$ and $\omega_e$:

$$
B=1.93128087\,{\rm cm}^{-1},
\qquad
\omega_e=2169.81358\,{\rm cm}^{-1}.
$$

With this:

$$
D_{\rm GDQ}
=
6.120000554143\times10^{-6}\,{\rm cm}^{-1}.
$$

Compared with the typical reference:

$$
D_{\rm ref}
=
6.121000000000\times10^{-6}\,{\rm cm}^{-1},
$$

the relative error is:

$$
-1.632814665640\times10^{-4}.
$$

This comparison is not an absolute prediction of the molecule: $B$ and $\omega_e$ were external inputs. It shows that the derived formula has the correct scale when the collective data of the molecular bridge are known.
