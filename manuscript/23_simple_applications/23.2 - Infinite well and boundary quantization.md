# 23.2. Infinite well and boundary quantization

In the one-dimensional stationary sector, we take:

$$
S_R=-Et,
\qquad
\nabla S_R=0,
\qquad
R=\sqrt\rho.
$$

The Hamilton--Jacobi--Bohm equation reduces to:

$$
E
=
-
\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{R''}{R}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
R''+k^2R=0,
\qquad
k^2=\frac{2mE}{\hbar^2}.
$$

For ideal walls at $x=0$ and $x=L$:

$$
R(0)=R(L)=0.
$$

The regular solutions are:

$$
R_n(x)
=
A\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right),
\qquad
n=1,2,\ldots
$$

and:

$$
E_n
=
\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2n^2}{2mL^2}.
$$

This is a retrieval of the ideal boundary limit. GDQ does not postulate this operator as a fundamental ontology: it is the projection of the physical Hessian onto a flat, stationary sector with an ideal wall.
