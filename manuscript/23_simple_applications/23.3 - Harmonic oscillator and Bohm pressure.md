# 23.3. Harmonic oscillator and Bohm pressure

In the harmonic oscillator:

$$
V(x)=\frac12m\omega^2x^2.
$$

The stationary ground state satisfies:

$$
E
=
\frac12m\omega^2x^2
-
\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{R''}{R}.
$$

We test the profile:

$$
R(x)=Ae^{-\alpha x^2/2}.
$$

Then:

$$
\frac{R''}{R}
=
\alpha^2x^2-\alpha.
$$

Substituting:

$$
E
=
\left(
\frac12m\omega^2
-
\frac{\hbar^2\alpha^2}{2m}
\right)x^2
+
\frac{\hbar^2\alpha}{2m}.
$$

For $E$ to be independent of $x$:

$$
\alpha=\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
E_0=\frac12\hbar\omega.
$$

The full ladder appears through the reduced harmonic operator or by the WKB/Maslov rule:

$$
\oint p\,dx
=
h\left(n+\frac12\right),
$$

and:

$$
E_n
=
\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right).
$$

The term $1/2$ is the Maslov index of the two turning points. In the GDQ reading, it expresses the closure condition of the phase channel against the turning boundaries of the motion.
