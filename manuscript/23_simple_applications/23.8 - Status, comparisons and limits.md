# 23.8. Status, comparisons and limits

## 2. What remains closed

The following are structurally closed:

1. well and oscillator as stationary reductions;
2. wall impedance as Schur/DtN;
3. Hartman effect as proper length saturation in the evanescent channel;
4. ideal Casimir as the Hessian determinant with an ideal boundary;
5. ideal rotor as Laplace--Beltrami on $S^2$;
6. centrifugal distortion as radial harmonic response.

## 3. What does not remain closed

The following are not metrologically closed:

1. real material walls without derived $\mathsf R_{\rm wall}$;
2. real barriers without wavepacket, detector, and band;
3. real material Casimir without $\mathsf R_{\rm plate}(\omega,k,T)$;
4. absolute molecular rotor without calculating $\Phi_{\rm mol,*}$, $\mu_{\rm GDQ}$, $R_0$, and $\omega_e$;
5. specific torsional/curved effects without physical background.

These pending points do not reopen the ideal results. They merely state that material applications require boundary data and real Hessians.
