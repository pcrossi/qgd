# 23. Simple Applications and Reduction Tests

This chapter gathers elementary problems where GDQ must recover known operators when the background is flat, stationary, and has simple boundaries.

The objective is not to use these examples as independent proof of the theory. They serve to verify the chain:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_\ast
\to
K_{\rm phys}
\to
P_{\rm phys}K_{\rm phys}P_{\rm phys}
\to
\text{reduced operator}
\to
\text{observable}.
$$
