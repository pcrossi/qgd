# 14.10. What remains for masses, mixings, and metrology

This chapter closes the structural architecture:

1. effective internal bundle;
2. effective group;
3. hypercharges;
4. one chiral generation;
5. selection of three stomata;
6. collective stability of the $C_3$ junction;
7. geometric coupling ratios at the common point.

The chapter does not intend to close:

1. particle masses;
2. leptonic hierarchy;
3. CKM/PMNS matrices;
4. values of couplings at experimental scales;
5. complete warped/mixed backgrounds;
6. hadronic composite spectrum.

These items require:

$$
\Phi_\ast^{\rm real},
\qquad
K_{\rm phys}^{\rm real},
\qquad
\mathsf R_{\rm app},
\qquad
\text{domain and metrological boundaries}.
$$

Therefore, masses and mixings do not reopen the geometric taxonomy. They are subsequent applications of the already obtained structure.
