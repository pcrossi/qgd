# 14.2. Solitons, stomata, and geometric sectors

A stoma is an internal topological interface of the GDQ soliton. It is not a point particle; it is a gluing region where flux, phase, torsion, and metric organize a stable excitation.

A particle sector is defined by:

1. topological class;
2. stoma type;
3. localized internal modes;
4. response of the physical Hessian;
5. effective representation after projection in the laboratory.

Thus:

$$
\text{operational particle}
=
\text{physical mode of an admissible geometric sector.}
$$

To avoid ambiguity, "soliton" does not mean any localized profile. It means an admissible stationary configuration of GDQ, with finite energy, normalizable density, topological invariants, and a controlled physical Hessian. The technical note [[notes/gdq_soliton_criterion|GDQ criterion for a material soliton]] registers the complete record: stationary equations, energy, mass, charge, spin, zero modes, asymptotics, and interaction.

The minimum explicit example is the neutral Gaussian soliton in $\mathbb R^d$:

$$
g_{ij}=\delta_{ij},
\qquad
H=0,
\qquad
\phi=\frac{|x|^2}{4\sigma}.
$$

It satisfies

$$
R_{ij}+\nabla_i\nabla_j\phi
=
\frac1{2\sigma}g_{ij},
$$

and has normalized density

$$
\rho_N
=
\frac{1}{(4\pi\sigma)^{d/2}}
\exp\left(-\frac{|x|^2}{4\sigma}\right).
$$

Its reduced free energy is

$$
\mathcal W_{\rm gauss}
=
\left\langle
\sigma|\nabla\phi|^2+\phi-d
\right\rangle
=0.
$$

This example proves that the structure admits an explicit neutral solitonic solution. It does not replace the required record for electron, proton, neutron, or any charged particle.

The number of stomata organizes sectors, but must not be used as a free table:

| Number of centers | Safe reading |
|---|---|
| 1 | isolated primitive defect, useful for leptonic or elementary sectors |
| 2 | composite/mesonic sector, generally not isolated without external boundary |
| 3 | first closed horizontal junction, non-colinear and isolated |
| $N>3$ | composite states or additional internal modes in the reduced model |

The strong point is that $N=3$ will not be postulated. It will be selected by flux conservation and collective stability.
