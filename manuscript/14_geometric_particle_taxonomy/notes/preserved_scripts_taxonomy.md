# Preserved scripts of geometric taxonomy

## 1. Criterion

The construction of the geometric taxonomy has many historical tests. For the chapter, only the final or reduced scripts necessary to confirm the central construction were preserved, without carrying intermediate attempts.

Incorporated scripts:

| Script in the chapter | Preserved conceptual origin | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `hypercharges_z6.py` | Diophantine search for integer hypercharges | Verifies hypercharges, primitivity, and anomaly cancellation. |
| `aps_index_hopf_bismut.py` | local APS index on the Hopf--Bismut link | Verifies primitive flux, reduced eta, torsional kernel, and local unitary index. |
| `index_lifting_representations.py` | lifting of the index to internal representations | Verifies $15$ Weyl components per generation and $45$ for three stomata. |
| `global_product_three_stomata.py` | audit of the global product and local selection | Confirms that the flat product does not generate three and that three stomata generate $N_G=3$. |
| `hessian_three_centers.py` | constrained Hessian of the three-center background | Verifies reduced stability of the $C_3$ sector. |
| `physical_hessian_c3_gap.py` | projected physical Hessian of the $C_3$ junction | Verifies physical projector, Schur, and positive reduced gap. |
| `couplings_norms.py` | geometric norms of the effective subbundles | Calculates coupling ratios and reduced $\sin^2\theta_W$. |
| `junction_n_selection.py` | selection by torsional equilibrium of stomata | Tests the selection $N=3$ and null modes for $N>3$. |

## 2. Unincorporated material

Tests of orbifold, K3, anisotropic backreaction, moduli, and alternative routes do not enter the main body because they are exploratory or belong to subsequent chapters. The flat Berry test was preserved only in the reduced form needed to show that the trivial global product does not generate three generations.

## 3. Status

No final script necessary for the structural closing of Chapter 14 was left behind. Exploratory scripts remain outside the body of the manuscript to avoid mixing historical evolution with the positive construction.
