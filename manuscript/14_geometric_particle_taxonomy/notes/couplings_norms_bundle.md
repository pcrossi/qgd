# Couplings as bundle norms

At the common geometric point:

$$
\frac1{g_a^2}
=
C_{\rm GDQ}I_a.
$$

For one generation:

$$
I_3=2,
\qquad
I_2=2,
\qquad
I_Y=\frac{10}{3}.
$$

Hence:

$$
g_s=g,
$$

$$
\frac{g'^2}{g^2}=\frac35,
$$

and:

$$
\sin^2\theta_W=\frac38.
$$

The absolute normalization can be performed by:

$$
e^2=4\pi\alpha.
$$

But couplings measured at different scales require different backgrounds. One should not directly compare the common geometric point with hadronic or electroweak scales without background transport.
