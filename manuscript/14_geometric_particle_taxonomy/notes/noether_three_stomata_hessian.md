# Noether, three stomata and Hessian

## 1. Noether constraint

With:

$$
f=u+iv,
$$

the symmetry $v\mapsto v+\varepsilon$ generates current conservation. At the junction:

$$
\sum_{a=1}^{N}\mathbf T_a=0.
$$

The tensions belong to the Hopf horizontal distribution:

$$
\mathcal H=\ker\eta_H,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathcal H=2.
$$

## 2. Selection of $N=3$

An elementary junction needs to be closed, non-colinear, and isolated. In horizontal dimension two:

$$
N\ge3.
$$

For $N>3$, $N-3$ internal null modes appear. Isolation requires:

$$
N-3=0.
$$

Hence:

$$
N=3.
$$
