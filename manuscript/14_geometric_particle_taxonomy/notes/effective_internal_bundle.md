# Effective internal bundle

The internal bundle used in the chapter is:

$$
E_{\rm int}
=
E_C\oplus E_W\oplus L_Y.
$$

With:

$$
E_C\simeq\mathbb C^3,
\qquad
E_W\simeq\mathbb C^2,
\qquad
L_Y\simeq\mathbb C.
$$

The effective group is:

$$
G_{\rm eff}
=
\operatorname{Aut}_{\rm GDQ}(E_{\rm int}).
$$

Preservation of complex volume in $E_C$ yields $SU(3)_C$. Separation of the abelian phase in $E_W$ yields $SU(2)_L$. The line $L_Y$ yields $U(1)_Y$.

Globally:

$$
G_{\rm eff}^{\rm global}
=
\frac{
SU(3)_C\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y
}{
\mathbb Z_6
}.
$$

Status: structural definition of the local effective sector. It is not a new axiom of the official action.
