# Killing potentials and generators

A geometric route to the local gauge generators uses Killing potentials. In a compatible internal Kähler or Hermitian geometry in the effective sector, holomorphic Killing vectors $\xi_A$ admit potentials $P_A$:

$$
\partial_aP_A
=
i\,g_{a\bar b}\xi_A^{\bar b}.
$$

With the local symplectic form, the Poisson bracket satisfies:

$$
\{P_A,P_B\}_{\rm Poisson}
=
f_{ABC}P_C.
$$

Thus, the local generator matrices are representations of internal Hamiltonians.

Status: local geometric route preserved. The global lifting depends on the effective internal bundle and the gluing.
