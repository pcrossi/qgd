# GDQ construction of the geometric taxonomy

## 1. Statement

The taxonomy must arise from the action, the backgrounds, and the physical modes. The correct chain is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_\ast^{\rm stoma}
\to
K_{\rm GDQ}
\to
P_{\rm phys}^{\dagger}K_{\rm GDQ}P_{\rm phys}
\to
\mathcal V_{\rm int}
\to
E_{\rm int}
\to
G_{\rm eff}
\to
\text{charges and index}.
$$

## 2. Background

The material background is a soliton/stoma:

$$
\Phi_\ast^{\rm stoma}
=
(g_\ast,J_\ast,H_\ast,f_\ast).
$$

It is admissible when it satisfies the first variation of the official action in the domain with interface:

$$
\left.
\frac{\delta\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}}{\delta\Phi}
\right|_{\Phi_\ast^{\rm stoma}}
=
0.
$$

## 3. Hessian and internal modes

The second variation is:

$$
K_{\rm GDQ}
=
\left.
\frac{\delta^2\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}}
{\delta\Phi\,\delta\Phi}
\right|_{\Phi_\ast^{\rm stoma}}.
$$

After removing constraints and gauge:

$$
K_{\rm phys}
=
P_{\rm phys}^{\dagger}
K_{\rm GDQ}
P_{\rm phys}.
$$

The localized internal modes are the subspace:

$$
\mathcal V_{\rm int}
=
\ker_{\rm loc}K_{\rm phys}
\oplus
\text{stable quasi-zero modes}.
$$

These modes generate the effective internal bundle:

$$
E_{\rm int}\to N.
$$

## 4. Automorphisms

Base changes in $E_{\rm int}$ that preserve the GDQ invariants define:

$$
G_{\rm eff}
=
\operatorname{Aut}_{\rm GDQ}(E_{\rm int}).
$$

This definition prevents two confusions:

1. the Standard Model group is not postulated;
2. gauge is not confused with a new ontology.

## 5. Observables

The taxonomy observables are:

1. effective representation;
2. hypercharge;
3. chiral index;
4. generational multiplicity;
5. collective stability.

They are extracted from $E_{\rm int}$, the global quotient, and the constrained Hessian of the stomata.
