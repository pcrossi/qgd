# Hypercharges by Z6 and anomalies

Define:

$$
y=6Y.
$$

The global quotient imposes:

$$
2t+3p+y\equiv0\pmod6.
$$

For the five multiplets:

$$
q\equiv1\pmod6,
$$

$$
u\equiv d\equiv2\pmod6,
$$

$$
\ell\equiv3\pmod6,
$$

$$
e\equiv0\pmod6.
$$

The anomalies impose:

$$
2q+u+d=0,
$$

$$
3q+\ell=0,
$$

$$
6q+3u+3d+2\ell+e=0,
$$

$$
6q^3+3u^3+3d^3+2\ell^3+e^3=0.
$$

With primitivity:

$$
\gcd(|q|,|u|,|d|,|\ell|,|e|)=1.
$$

The minimal oriented solution is:

$$
(q,u,d,\ell,e)
=
(1,-4,2,-3,6).
$$

Therefore:

$$
\left(
Y_Q,
Y_{u^c},
Y_{d^c},
Y_L,
Y_{e^c}
\right)
=
\left(
\frac16,
-\frac23,
\frac13,
-\frac12,
1
\right).
$$

Status: closed as a Diophantine theorem, conditioned on the internal representations.
