# 14.5. Global Z6 quotient and hypercharges

Fractional charges are not literal fractional Chern classes. They are global weights of a $U(1)$ line normalized by a global gluing.

Define:

$$
y=6Y.
$$

The global effective group is:

$$
G
=
\frac{SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)_y}{\mathbb Z_6}.
$$

Choose the generator:

$$
z_6
=
\left(
e^{2\pi i/3}I_3,
-I_2,
e^{i\pi/3}
\right).
$$

If $t$ is color triality and $p$ is isospin parity, the representation descends to the quotient when:

$$
2t+3p+y\equiv0\pmod6.
$$

The quotient fixes modulo-six classes. Anomalies and primitivity fix the integer solution:

$$
(q,u,d,\ell,e)
=
(1,-4,2,-3,6).
$$

Dividing by six:

$$
\left(
Y_Q,
Y_{u^c},
Y_{d^c},
Y_L,
Y_{e^c}
\right)
=
\left(
\frac16,
-\frac23,
\frac13,
-\frac12,
1
\right).
$$

The logical meaning is restricted and precise: given the types of internal representation, the hypercharge is derived by global congruence, anomalies, and primitivity.
