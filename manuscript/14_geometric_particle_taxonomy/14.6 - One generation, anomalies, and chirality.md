# 14.6. One generation, anomalies, and chirality

The effective chiral spectrum of one generation is:

$$
\mathcal E_{\rm gen}
=
(3,2)_{1/6}
\oplus
(\bar3,1)_{-2/3}
\oplus
(\bar3,1)_{1/3}
\oplus
(1,2)_{-1/2}
\oplus
(1,1)_1.
$$

Optionally, there can be:

$$
(1,1)_0
$$

for a conjugate right-handed neutrino, without affecting the hypercharge anomalies.

The chiral decomposition is given by:

$$
\slashed D_{B,A}^{+}
:
\Gamma(S^+\otimes E_{\rm int})
\to
\Gamma(S^-\otimes E_{\rm int}).
$$

The net index is:

$$
\operatorname{Ind}(\slashed D_{B,A}^{+})
=
\ker\slashed D_{B,A}^{+}
-
\ker\slashed D_{B,A}^{-}.
$$

For the spectrum above, the anomalies cancel:

$$
2q+u+d=0,
$$

$$
3q+\ell=0,
$$

$$
6q+3u+3d+2\ell+e=0,
$$

$$
6q^3+3u^3+3d^3+2\ell^3+e^3=0.
$$

In addition, the number of $SU(2)$ doublets per generation is:

$$
3+1=4,
$$

hence there is no Witten global anomaly.
