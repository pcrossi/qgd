# 14.9. Couplings as geometric norms

Couplings are not introduced as three free numbers. At the common geometric matching point, they appear as norms of generators in the effective bundle:

$$
\frac1{g_a^2}
=
C_{\rm GDQ}I_a.
$$

The prefactor $C_{\rm GDQ}$ is common to the sectors being compared. The ratios are determined by the quadratic indices.

For one generation:

$$
I_3=2,
\qquad
I_2=2.
$$

Hence:

$$
g_s=g.
$$

For the hypercharge:

$$
I_Y
=
6\left(\frac16\right)^2
+3\left(-\frac23\right)^2
+3\left(\frac13\right)^2
+2\left(-\frac12\right)^2
+1^2
=
\frac{10}{3}.
$$

Thus:

$$
\frac{g'^2}{g^2}
=
\frac{I_2}{I_Y}
=
\frac35.
$$

Consequently:

$$
\sin^2\theta_W
=
\frac{g'^2}{g^2+g'^2}
=
\frac38.
$$

This result belongs to the common geometric point. Couplings measured at specific physical scales require background response and do not belong to the structural taxonomy.
