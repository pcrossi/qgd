# 26.4 — Derivations and conditional theorems

GDQ recovers quantum mechanics, effective fields, and usual operational sectors
as reductions. This does not mean that quantum mechanics is the fundamental
ontology of the theory.

Central variational results:

| Result | Classification |
|---|---|
| Madelung continuity | derivation in the canonical sector |
| Hamilton--Jacobi--Bohm | variational derivation of the density |
| weighted metric equation | metric variation of the action |
| phase current | Noether of phase symmetry |
| positivity of $\rho$ | consequence of the definition of $f$ |
| physical operator | projected Hessian |

The general form of the physical operator is:

$$
K^{\rm phys}
=
P_{\rm phys}
\operatorname{Hess}_{\Phi_*}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
P_{\rm phys}.
$$

When there are unobserved internal degrees, the boundary response is:

$$
\mathsf R
=
K_{\partial\partial}
-
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

This form appears in walls, detectors, protons, nuclei, plates, slits,
cosmological interfaces, and horizons.

Central conditional theorems:

| Result | Relevant condition |
|---|---|
| Wick rotation | causal boundary and class of fields |
| reality of the action | $\gamma$ and compatible boundaries |
| Lorentzian reconstruction | clock-form and local simultaneity |
| canonical Madelung sector | dissipative/Routh reduction and macroscopic apparatus |
| global--local bridge | six lemmas and declared gluing class |
| inheritance of $\alpha$ | global normalization and admissible transport |
| effective gauge group | topological class and APS/Bismut gluing |
| three generations | three stomata and index additivity |
| confinement/effective Yang--Mills | sectorial isomorphism in the color domain |
| leptonic masses | product domain; future warped/mixed |

These results are conditional because their domains have been declared. This
is the correct form of a controlled mathematical statement.
