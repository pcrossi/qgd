# 26.6 — Structural backlog, future programs, and metrology

The main structural backlog has been sorted. This does not mean the research
program has ended.

$$
\boxed{
\text{zero structural backlog}
\quad\ne\quad
\text{research program completed.}
}
$$

What remains are extensions, fine metrology, complete covariant proofs, and
real apparatuses.

Main future programs:

1. real warped/mixed backgrounds for $\alpha$, masses, and hierarchy;
2. complete 8D covariant Hessians for black holes;
3. physical Page curve by real spectral channels;
4. integrated cosmological solver;
5. joint comparison with SPARC/RAR, lensing, CMB, BAO, SN, and BBN;
6. real apparatuses in Stern--Gerlach, double slit, delayed choice,
   Aharonov--Bohm, and Sagnac;
7. complete computational algorithm for the sign problem;
8. differential baryonic phenomenology;
9. PMNS, CP phase, and MSW by neutral Hessian;
10. fine metrology of hydrogen by local protonic Hessian.

These items must be read as upcoming calculations, not as failures of the action.
