# Note — Conditional theorems

A conditional theorem has the form:

$$
\text{declared hypotheses}
\Longrightarrow
\text{result}.
$$

In GDQ, this appears whenever the result depends on:

1. geometric class;
2. causal boundary;
3. functional domain;
4. regularity;
5. physical projection;
6. stationary background.

Example of correct form:

$$
\left(
\Phi_* \text{ stationary},
P_{\rm phys} \text{ well-defined},
K^{\rm phys}\ge0
\right)
\Longrightarrow
\text{stable physical spectrum}.
$$

This is not weakness. It is the normal form of a controlled mathematical statement.
