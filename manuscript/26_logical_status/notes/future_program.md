# Note — Future program

Future program means real work not yet executed at the fine metrological or
complete covariant level. It is not automatically a structural failure.

The criterion is:

$$
\text{if the logical objection already has a classified answer,}
\quad
\text{the rest is refinement or extension.}
$$

If a new derivation shows contradiction with the official action, then the item
returns to being a structural problem. Without that, it remains as a research
agenda.
