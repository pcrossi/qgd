# Note — Axioms and definitions

An internal definition is not adjustable case by case. In GDQ, the central
definitions are:

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2},
$$

$$
S_R=\frac{\hbar}{2i}(f-\bar f),
$$

$$
\mathcal U=\frac{\rho}{(4\pi z_\tau)^n}.
$$

Redefining $\rho$ to force Born, or redefining $S_R$ to force phase, breaks
the Madelung variation, continuity, and operational reconstruction.

The official action remains the dynamical axiom. Subsequent results may add
sources, probes, or interface terms, but they do not alter the bulk action.
