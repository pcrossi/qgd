# 26.7 — How to read the current status of GDQ

The correct statement of the current status is:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{GDQ has an official action and a coherent variational chain.}\\
\text{Quantum mechanics, effective fields, and usual interactions arise as reductions.}\\
\text{The main applications have been classified by domain and boundary.}\\
\text{The complete metrology depends on real Hessians and backgrounds.}
\end{gathered}
}
$$

The incorrect statement would be:

$$
\boxed{
\text{all physical problems are closed without conditions.}
}
$$

What is organized:

1. axioms and definitions;
2. variational chain;
3. role of boundaries and apparatuses;
4. relationship between GDQ and known operational reductions;
5. status of the main calculations;
6. list of future programs.

This organization is the goal of this chapter. It allows the manuscript to
continue without reopening loops that have already been classified.

## Quantum mechanics as a projective sector

A central reading of the current status is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{usual quantum mechanics is the particular projective-operational case of GDQ.}
}
$$

The reduction chain is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\text{Madelung regular polarization}
\to
\mathcal H_{\rm phys}
\to
\text{projective observables}
\to
\text{usual QM}.
$$

This formulation simultaneously preserves two things:

1. quantum mechanics is not discarded; it is recovered in the domain in which
   apparatuses and boundaries select projective alternatives;
2. GDQ is broader than operational QM, because it keeps geometry, the Hessian,
   boundaries, classical sources, and variable domains as part of the primary
   dynamics.

Therefore, when comparing GDQ with quantum mechanics, the correct question is
not whether GDQ "replaces" QM, but whether it recovers QM in the projective
sector and explains geometrically when this sector is selected.
