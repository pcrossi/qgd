# 26 — Logical Status of the Theory

This chapter does not introduce new physics. It organizes the logical accounting
of GDQ: what is an axiom, definition, derivation, conditional theorem, effective
reduction, numerical evidence, phenomenological comparison, and future program.

The goal is to prevent two symmetric errors:

$$
\boxed{
\text{do not call a metrological refinement a structural failure;}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{do not call numerical agreement a variational proof.}
}
$$
