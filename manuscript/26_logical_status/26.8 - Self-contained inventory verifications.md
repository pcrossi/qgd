# 26.8 — Self-contained inventory verifications

This chapter contains only inventory scripts. They do not prove GDQ; they
verify whether the manuscript is correctly classifying its own content.

See [[scripts/README]].

| Script | Function |
|---|---|
| `logical_inventory.py` | generates a table of axioms, definitions, conditional theorems, reductions, and future programs |
| `preserved_comparisons.py` | consolidates numerical comparisons already preserved in the chapters |

These scripts do not replace the proofs and calculations of the previous chapters.
They serve to keep the accounting explicit.
