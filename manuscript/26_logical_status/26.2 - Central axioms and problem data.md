# 26.2 — Central axioms and problem data

The central axioms of GDQ must remain few.

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| official action | dynamical axiom |
| Hermitian/Bismut class | geometric axiom |
| causal boundary $\gamma$ | fundamental causal data of the problem |
| admissible topological class | structural data |
| global and boundary conditions | physical data of the problem |
| non-derived metrological scales | calibration, not new dynamics |

The official action is:

$$
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{GDQ}}
=
\int_{\gamma}
\left[
\int_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathbb C}}
\frac{\hbar}{\Lambda_C^2}
\left[
\tau\left(
\mathcal R
+
g^{\mu\bar\nu}
\partial_\mu f
\partial_{\bar\nu}\bar f
\right)
+
\frac{f+\bar f}{2}
-n
\right]
\mathcal U
\sqrt{\det g}
\,d^{2n}z
\right]
\frac{d\tau}{\tau}.
$$

It must not be replaced by Einstein--Hilbert, Yang--Mills, Dirac, Pauli,
or the Standard Model. These structures may appear as effective reductions,
comparisons, or auxiliary languages.

In the restructured manuscript, $\Lambda_C$ is treated as a cutoff number in
coordinates normalized by the Cartan scale. Dimensional symbols like
$k_C$, $\ell_C$, and $E_C$ are used when it is necessary to restore units.

Sources, probes, and boundary terms do not alter the fundamental action. They
enter as external data of the problem or as interface terms derived by the
variational principle.
