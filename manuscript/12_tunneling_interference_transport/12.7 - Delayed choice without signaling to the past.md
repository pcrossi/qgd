# 12.7. Delayed choice without signaling to the past

In delayed choice, the final arrangement of the apparatus is defined late. The GDQ interpretation is not signaling to the past. It is a change of the boundary realized before final registration.

We write:

$$
\mathsf R_{\rm app}(t)
=
\mathsf R_{\rm off}
+
s(t-t_c)
\left(
\mathsf R_{\rm on}
-
\mathsf R_{\rm off}
\right),
$$

where $s$ is a causal switching function.

The accumulated cost in the final registration can be represented by:

$$
\Gamma_{\rm det}(t_f)
=
\frac12
\int
\left\langle
\Delta\Phi_\partial(t),
\mathsf R_{\rm app}(t)
\Delta\Phi_\partial(t)
\right\rangle
w(t_f,t)\,dt.
$$

The kernel $w(t_f,t)$ represents causal transport and apparatus memory. It is neither superluminal propagation nor retroactive physical influence.

What changes is:

$$
(\Omega,\partial\Omega_{\rm old})
\to
(\Omega,\partial\Omega_{\rm new}).
$$

The final registration reflects the boundary value problem effectively realized.

As a reduced material example, an electro-optic Mach--Zehnder interferometer with finite switching is treated in the note:

- [[notes/electro_optic_mzi_delayed_choice|EO-MZI interferometer and delayed choice]]

In this interpretation, a power crosstalk of $-30\,{\rm dB}$ corresponds to:

$$
p_{\rm leak}=10^{-3},
\qquad
\mathcal C_{\rm app}=\sqrt{p_{\rm leak}}
=
3{,}162277660168\times10^{-2}.
$$

The reduced calculation yields:

$$
\Gamma_\infty=3{,}453877639491,
\qquad
e^{-\Gamma_\infty}
=
3{,}162277660168\times10^{-2}.
$$

This is a comparison with external apparatus data, not a first-principles prediction. The reduced material layer shows that, in an ideal MZI, $p_{\rm dark}\simeq0$; the finite crosstalk belongs to $\delta K_{\rm app}$: phase, couplers, losses, manufacturing, and electro-optic response.
