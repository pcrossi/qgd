# 12.4. Density, phase and Bohm pressure in the fringes

In the reduced paraxial regime, the double slit can be represented by two coherent contributions:

$$
\psi_{\rm tot}=\psi_1+\psi_2.
$$

The density is:

$$
\rho
=
I_1+I_2
+
2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\Delta\phi.
$$

In GDQ, this formula does not describe an abstract wave. It describes the geometric density $\rho$ and the phase difference $S_R/\hbar$ between two flow channels.

The interference nodes have:

$$
\rho\to0.
$$

The Bohm potential:

$$
Q[\rho]
=
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{\Delta\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho}
$$

can become singular at ideal nodes. Thus, destructive fringes appear as barriers of Bohm pressure, and constructive fringes as flow channels.

This is the GDQ interpretation of the pattern: there is no point particle choosing a slit without structure; there is density/phase guided by boundaries and geometric nodes.
