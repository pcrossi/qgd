# Bohm pressure at interference nodes

## Statement

Density zeros in the interference pattern appear, in the Madelung reduction, as barriers of geometric pressure.

## Construction

Define:

$$
R=\sqrt\rho.
$$

The Bohm potential is:

$$
Q
=
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{\Delta R}{R}.
$$

At an ideal node:

$$
R\to0.
$$

If $\Delta R$ does not vanish to the same order, the ratio becomes singular:

$$
\frac{\Delta R}{R}\to\infty.
$$

Thus:

$$
|Q|\to\infty.
$$

## Scope

This conclusion belongs to the Madelung reduction. It does not require a new action and does not replace the complete calculation of the GDQ Hessian in real geometries.
