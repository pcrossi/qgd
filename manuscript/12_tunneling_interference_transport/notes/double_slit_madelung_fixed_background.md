# Double slit in Madelung on fixed background

## Statement

In the reduced fixed background sector, the double slit is a boundary value problem for $\rho$ and $S_R$.

## Equations

$$
\partial_t\rho
+
\nabla\cdot
\left(
\rho\frac{\nabla S_R}{m}
\right)
=0.
$$

$$
\partial_tS_R
+
\frac{|\nabla S_R|^2}{2m}
+
V_{\rm app}
-
\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{\Delta\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho}
=0.
$$

$V_{\rm app}$ represents the barrier and slits as classical apparatus data.

## Reduced density

For two coherent contributions:

$$
\psi_{\rm tot}=\psi_1+\psi_2.
$$

Then:

$$
\rho
=
|\psi_1|^2+|\psi_2|^2
+
2\operatorname{Re}(\psi_1\bar\psi_2).
$$

Writing $I_j=|\psi_j|^2$:

$$
\rho
=
I_1+I_2
+
2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\Delta\phi.
$$

## Scope

This reduction recovers the known interference. The GDQ differential appears in the geometric interpretation and in the treatment of the detector via impedance.
