# 12.3. Double slit as a boundary value problem

The double slit is a domain and boundary problem. In the reduced laboratory sector, we use:

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2},
\qquad
S_R=\frac{\hbar}{2i}(f-\bar f).
$$

In a flat/reconstructed background, the Madelung dynamics is:

$$
\partial_t\rho
+
\nabla\cdot
\left(
\rho\frac{\nabla S_R}{m}
\right)
=0,
$$

and:

$$
\partial_tS_R
+
\frac{|\nabla S_R|^2}{2m}
+
V_{\rm app}
-
\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{\Delta\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho}
=0.
$$

Here $V_{\rm app}$ represents the barrier, slits, and experimental support. It is an external boundary datum, not a new term of the official action.

If the slits are $A_1$ and $A_2$, the flux condition is:

$$
\int_{A_1}\rho v^n\,dA
+
\int_{A_2}\rho v^n\,dA
=
\mathcal J_{\rm in}.
$$

For identical slits:

$$
\int_{A_1}\rho v^n\,dA
=
\int_{A_2}\rho v^n\,dA
=
\frac12\mathcal J_{\rm in}.
$$
