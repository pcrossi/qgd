# Dimensional analysis of gravitational coupling

## Statement

The dimensional gravitational constant cannot be extracted solely from an dimensionless integral. GDQ must separate the dimensionless geometric ratio, metrological calibration, and cosmological boundary.

## Dimensionless action and restoration of units

In the normalized form of the official action, the weighted integrand is a pure number. The physical dimension of action comes from the factor $\hbar$. Therefore, dimensional quantities such as $G$ cannot arise as absolute numbers without reference to a physical scale.

$$

Here, $M_p$ is the mass used as a metrological reference and is not "created" by the chosen unit; it enters as the physical scale of the compared sector.

$$

G=\Pi_G\frac{\hbar c}{M_p^2}.

If the only dimensional constants used to convert a geometric ratio into gravitational coupling are $\hbar$, $c$, and $M_p$, then the dimensional form of $G$ is forced.

Since:

$$

\qquad

$$

$$

$$

Thus, any reduced gravitational prediction must have the form:

$$
G_{\rm GDQ}
