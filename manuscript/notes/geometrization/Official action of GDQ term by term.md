# Official action of GDQ term by term

Define

$$
\mathcal L_0
=
\tau\left(
\mathcal R
+g^{\mu\bar\nu}\partial_\mu f\partial_{\bar\nu}\bar f
\right)
+\frac{f+\bar f}{2}
-n.
$$

Since $[\tau]=L^2$, $[\mathcal R]=L^{-2}$ and $f$ is dimensionless, $\mathcal L_0$ is dimensionless.

The fundamental variations are

$$
\delta g_{\mu\bar\nu},
\qquad
\delta f,
\qquad
\delta\bar f.
$$

$\mathcal U$ accompanies the variation of $f$ and $\bar f$. $H$ is also not varied independently when defined by $H=d_J^c\omega_H$.

Integrations by parts generate boundary terms only after the domain is specified. There is no universal boundary term that can be added without declaring the boundary geometry and the allowed variations.

Since $z_\tau$ and $\gamma$ are complex, the integral can be complex. Physical stationarity is applied to

$$
S_{\rm phys}=\operatorname{Re}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}.
$$
