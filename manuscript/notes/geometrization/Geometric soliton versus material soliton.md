# Geometric soliton versus material soliton

A Ricci gradient soliton satisfies

$$
\operatorname{Ric}+\nabla^2f=\lambda g.
$$

This expresses self-similarity of the flow, not charge, spin, or mass.

A candidate material background must satisfy

$$
\delta\operatorname{Re}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}=0
$$

in the domain and under the declared constraints. After removing gauge, normalization, and symmetry modes, its physical Hessian must satisfy

$$
\langle\delta\Phi,
\mathbb H_*^{\rm phys}\delta\Phi\rangle\geq0.
$$

For strict stability in the sector orthogonal to collective modes, we look for a gap $\Delta_*>0$.

We will call the configuration a material soliton only when there is a stationary solution, finite relative action, localization, conserved class, stable physical Hessian, and well-defined spectral response.
