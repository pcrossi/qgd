# Complex causal variable: dimension, symmetries and conditional uniqueness

Consider a minimal affine variable

$$
z=a\tau+ibt,
$$

with real $a$ and $b$. Since $[\tau]=L^2$, homogeneity requires

$$
[b]=L^2T^{-1}.
$$

A real reparametrization absorbs $a\ne0$. Writing $\nu_0=b/a$, we obtain

$$
z_\tau=\tau+i\nu_0t.
$$

Under time reversal,

$$
t\mapsto-t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
z_\tau\mapsto\bar z_\tau.
$$

Therefore, within the affine class, without a new scale and with this conjugation law, the form is unique up to orientation and real reparametrization. The conclusion does not exclude non-linear functions of $\tau$ and $t$; therefore, uniqueness is conditional on the minimal affine class.
