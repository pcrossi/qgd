# Sudarshan as contour language

## Statement

In GDQ, the advanced-retarded language associated with Sudarshan is used as a vocabulary for decomposing solutions and endpoint conditions. It is not postulated as physical retrocausality.

## Causal structure of GDQ

The official action integrates a form along a causal contour $\gamma$. This contour selects the branch, orientation, poles, and conjugate pairing. The flow parameter $\tau$, the physical time $t$, and the complex causal variable $z_\tau$ have distinct roles.

When a solution is decomposed into retarded and advanced sectors, one writes formally:

$$
\Phi
=
\Phi_{\rm ret}
+\Phi_{\rm adv}.
$$

This decomposition is useful because hyperbolic operators and Green's functions can be solved using past and formal future data. However, in GDQ, the fundamental object is not a physical influence coming from the future; it is the admissible contour of the action.

## Pairing and reality

If the contour has conjugate branches, the complex contributions appear in pairs:

$$
\mathcal I[\gamma_+]
+\overline{\mathcal I[\gamma_+]}
=
2\,{\rm Re}\,\mathcal I[\gamma_+].
$$

Thus, the reality of the action in the paired sector stems from the orientation and conjugation of the contour, not from a physical message propagating backward in time.

## What is demonstrated

It is demonstrated that:

1. advanced and retarded sectors can be treated as branches of the same complex causal construction;
2. conjugate pairing can make the action real;
3. poles and residues can select phases and quantization;
4. operational causality must be tested later in the measurement theory.

## What is not demonstrated

It is not demonstrated:

- signaling to the past;
- operational violation of causality;
- automatic advanced-retarded cancellation for every domain;
- unrestricted equivalence between Wick, Feynman, and Wiener.

Thus, Sudarshan enters as contour language, not as a new physical axiom.
