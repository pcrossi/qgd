# Quantization by monodromy and integral class

In local charts $U_i$, write a section of a line bundle as

$$
\Psi_i=\sqrt\rho\,e^{iS_i/\hbar}.
$$

In intersections,

$$
\Psi_i=e^{i\chi_{ij}}\Psi_j,
$$

so that

$$
S_i-S_j=\hbar\chi_{ij}
\quad\text{modulo }2\pi\hbar.
$$

Consistency at triple intersections imposes the integer cocycle. For a connection $A$, this is equivalent to the prequantization condition

$$
\left[\frac{F_A}{2\pi}\right]
\in H^2(M,\mathbb Z).
$$

When transporting the section around a cycle $\gamma$, its holonomy is

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma(A)
=\exp\left(i\oint_\gamma A\right).
$$

Trivial holonomy yields integer periods. Spin $-1$ holonomy shifts the period by half a unit. The physical unit depends on the normalization relating $A$ to $p/\hbar$.
