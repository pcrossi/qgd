# Reality of an integrated action on a complex contour

Let

$$
I_+=\int_{\gamma_+}\omega(z).
$$

Define $\gamma_-$ as the conjugate branch with the chosen orientation such that

$$
\int_{\gamma_-}\omega(z)
=\overline{I_+}.
$$

This occurs when the 1-form satisfies the reflection

$$
\overline{\omega(z)}=\omega(\bar z)
$$

after properly including the differential and the orientation. Then

$$
I=I_++\overline{I_+}
=2\operatorname{Re}I_+
\in\mathbb R.
$$

If the closed contour contains small cycles around poles, their contributions must also occur in conjugate pairs or produce a real sum. The geometric invariance of the contour design, without the condition on the 1-form, is not enough.
