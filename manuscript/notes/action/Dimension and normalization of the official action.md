# Dimension and normalization of the official action

Choose a Cartan length $\ell_C$ and introduce dimensionless variables:

$$
z^a=\ell_C\widehat z^a,
\qquad
\tau=\ell_C^2\widehat\tau,
\qquad
z_\tau=\ell_C^2\widehat z_\tau.
$$

The area dimension of $\tau$ is natural: it is the dimension of the Fermi age, which measures accumulated dispersion and not chronological time.

The curvature, volume element, and kernel transform as:

$$
\mathcal R=\ell_C^{-2}\widehat{\mathcal R},
\qquad
dV_g=\ell_C^8dV_{\widehat g},
\qquad
\mathcal U=\ell_C^{-8}\widehat{\mathcal U}.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\tau\mathcal R
= \widehat\tau\widehat{\mathcal R},
$$

$$
\int_M\mathcal U\,dV_g
=\int_{\widehat M}\widehat{\mathcal U}\,dV_{\widehat g},
$$

and

$$
\frac{d\tau}{\tau}
= \frac{d\widehat\tau}{\widehat\tau}
$$

are dimensionless. The same holds for

$$
\tau g^{\mu\bar\nu}
\partial_\mu f\partial_{\bar\nu}\bar f.
$$

In the normalized action, the cutoff appears as the number

$$
\Lambda_C
=\ell_C k_C
=1.
$$

Here, $k_C$ is the physical momentum with dimension $L^{-1}$, while $\Lambda_C$ is dimensionless. This is the convention used in the prefactor of the official action. Therefore,

$$
\left[\frac{\hbar}{\Lambda_C^2}\right]=[\hbar].
$$

Thus,

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
= \hbar\,\widehat{\mathcal I}_{\rm GDQ},
$$

where $\widehat{\mathcal I}_{\rm GDQ}$ is dimensionless, and therefore

$$
[\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}]=[\hbar].
$$

When restoring units, the same symbol must not be used for two distinct quantities. $\ell_C$ is the geometric length; the corresponding energy is:

$$
E_C=\frac{\hbar c}{\ell_C}
=\hbar c\,k_C.
$$

Using $\Lambda_C$ simultaneously for $k_C$, $\ell_C$, or $E_C$ would produce the dimensional contradiction pointed out in the audit. This reuse is prohibited by the convention above.
