# 24.9 — Self-contained numerical verifications

The scripts for this chapter are located in [[scripts/README]]. They were written to be self-contained, commented, and executable without depending on historical audits.

They do not preserve failed attempts. They preserve only:

1. the final reduced alpha chain;
2. the spin--torsion counting of the shells;
3. the Klein--Nishina formula, its Thomson limit, its total cross section, and flux normalization;
4. the reduced neutral torsional candidate;
5. the reduced reconstruction of neutral sheet--mode oscillations.

The main outputs are:

| Script | Output | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| `reduced_alpha_decay.py` | RMS $0.067894$ decades | reduced proof of concept |
| `alpha_pipeline_reduced_schur_riesz.py` | Schur/Riesz, $E_\partial$, $\nu_{\rm GDQ}$, RMS $0.067894$ | final reduced construction |
| `spin_torsion_shells.py` | $2,8,20,28,50,82,126$ | reduced derivation |
| `reduced_klein_nishina.py` | Thomson limit and angular table | consistency test |
| `total_and_flux_klein_nishina.py` | $r_e$, $\sigma_T$, angular integration, and total cross section | consistency test |
| `reduced_torsional_neutrinos.py` | $\Delta m^2$ and candidate masses | reduced candidate |
| `neutrino_oscillations_sheet_mode.py` | $K^\nu$, mixing, $\Delta m^2$, and probabilities | reduced candidate |

Each comparison must be read with the status indicated in the script itself. The fact that a number agrees well does not automatically transform the calculation into a blind prediction. The classification depends on whether the chain used the experimental target and whether the official Hessian has already been evaluated on the complete background.
