# Note — Neutral torsional sector

The neutrino is represented by the projected neutral sector:

$$
D_\nu^{\rm tors}
=
P_{\ker Q,\chi_L}
K_{\rm neutral}^{\rm phys}
P_{\ker Q,\chi_L}.
$$

For three leptonic sheets, the operational form is a generalized problem:

$$
K^\nu c_i
=
\lambda_i G^\nu c_i.
$$

Here $G^\nu$ is the weighted Gram matrix of the neutral modes and $K^\nu$ is the Hessian block in the neutral sector. The mixing matrix is the change of basis between the sheets and the eigenmodes.

The preserved reduced candidate uses:

$$
S_\nu
=
\alpha^7Q_\beta^2,
$$

and:

$$
\lambda
=
\left(
0,
\frac{\chi_\nu^2}{2},
\frac{6\pi}{5}
\right),
\qquad
\chi_\nu=\frac{12}{25}e^{-\alpha/4}.
$$

Hence:

$$
m_i^2
=
S_\nu\lambda_i.
$$

The result is close to the oscillation data, but it is still a reduced candidate. The final derivation requires obtaining $G^\nu$ and $K^\nu$ via weighted integrals of the official Hessian.
