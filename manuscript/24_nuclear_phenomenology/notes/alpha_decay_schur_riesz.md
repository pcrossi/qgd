# Note — Alpha decay by Schur and Riesz

Consider the second physical variation of the official action in the nuclear background:

$$
\delta^2\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac12
\left\langle
\begin{pmatrix}
\delta\Phi_I\\
\delta\Phi_\partial
\end{pmatrix},
\begin{pmatrix}
K_{II} & K_{I\partial}\\
K_{\partial I} & K_{\partial\partial}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
\delta\Phi_I\\
\delta\Phi_\partial
\end{pmatrix}
\right\rangle.
$$

In the channel calculation, the internal modes are variationally eliminated:

$$
\frac{\delta}{\delta\Phi_I}
\delta^2\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
=
0.
$$

Hence:

$$
K_{II}\delta\Phi_I
+
K_{I\partial}\delta\Phi_\partial
=
0,
$$

and:

$$
\delta\Phi_I
=
-
K_{II}^{-1}
K_{I\partial}\delta\Phi_\partial.
$$

Substituting into the quadratic form:

$$
\delta^2\mathcal S_{\partial}
=
\frac12
\left\langle
\delta\Phi_\partial,
K_{\partial}^{\rm phys}
\delta\Phi_\partial
\right\rangle,
$$

with:

$$
K_{\partial}^{\rm phys}
=
K_{\partial\partial}
-
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

The channel projector is:

$$
P_\alpha
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\oint_{\mathcal C_\alpha}
\left(z-K_{\partial}^{\rm phys}\right)^{-1}
\,dz.
$$

The surface energy is:

$$
E_{\partial}^{\rm GDQ}
=
\langle
P_\alpha(1-P_{\rm daughter})\Phi_\alpha,
K_{\partial}^{\rm phys}
P_\alpha(1-P_{\rm daughter})\Phi_\alpha
\rangle_\partial.
$$

This is the preserved construction. The reduced calculation uses small matrices to evaluate this scheme; the future metrological calculation must replace these blocks with operators obtained directly from the complete nuclear Hessian.

## Reduced construction executed

To prevent the alpha comparison from looking like just an adjusted table, the preserved reduced version constructs the blocks from channel data:

$$
A,\quad Z,\quad Q_\alpha,
$$

and geometric quantities:

$$
R_{\rm touch}
=
r_0\left((A-4)^{1/3}+4^{1/3}\right),
$$

$$
x_{\rm barrier}
=
\frac{2(Z-2)\alpha\hbar c}{R_{\rm touch}Q_\alpha}
-1,
$$

$$
\delta_{\rm touch}
=
\frac{R_{\rm touch}-r_0A^{1/3}}{r_0A^{1/3}},
$$

and:

$$
\chi_{\rm curv}
=
\frac{\delta_{\rm touch}^2}{x_{\rm barrier}}.
$$

The positive surface impedance base is:

$$
\mathcal I_\Sigma(x)
=
j_0^2\frac{x^2}{1+x}
+j_1^2\frac{x^2}{(1+x)^2}
+j_2^2\frac{x^3}{(1+x)^2}.
$$

In the reduced normalization:

$$
K_{\partial\partial}
=
\operatorname{diag}
\left(
\frac{4\mathcal I_\Sigma(\chi_{\rm curv})}{\alpha},
1+s_{\rm shell},
1+x_{\rm barrier}
\right).
$$

The internal block is taken positive:

$$
K_{II}
=
\operatorname{diag}
\left(
1+x_{\rm barrier},
1+\frac{4\chi_{\rm curv}}{\alpha},
1+s_{\rm shell}
\right).
$$

The cross block $K_{I\partial}$ uses the same reduced currents $j_0,j_1,j_2$ that enter $\mathcal I_\Sigma$. Thus, the Schur feedback is not a free factor, but the matrix contraction:

$$
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

The reduced shell rigidity is:

$$
s_{\rm shell}
=
\frac{(A-4)^{2/3}}{D_{\rm shell}+(A-4)^{2/3}},
$$

where:

$$
D_{\rm shell}
=
d_Z^2+d_N^2,
$$

and $d_Z,d_N$ are distances to closures generated by the spin-torsion angular operator, not by a manual list.

After forming $K_\partial^{\rm phys}$, the band with the largest overlap with the primitive alpha vector after removing the daughter subspace is selected:

$$
P_\perp
=
P_\alpha(1-P_{\rm daughter}).
$$

The reduced bounce frequency is:

$$
\nu_{\rm bounce}
=
\frac{c}{2R_{\rm touch}}
\sqrt{\frac{2Q_\alpha}{\mu}}.
$$

With determinant mobility:

$$
\nu_{\rm GDQ}
=
\nu_{\rm bounce}
\left(1+\lambda_\alpha\right)^{n_{\rm cl}/2},
$$

where $n_{\rm cl}$ is the number of exact closures of the daughter nucleus in protons and neutrons. For $\mathrm{Po}$-$212\to\mathrm{Pb}$-$208+\alpha$, $n_{\rm cl}=2$.

Finally:

$$
\Gamma_{\rm GDQ}
=
\nu_{\rm GDQ}
\exp(-E_\partial^{\rm GDQ})
\exp(-W_{\rm rad}^{\rm GDQ}).
$$
