# Note — Macroscopic interface and effective gravity

When a nuclear calculation involves a medium, gravity, or cosmological scale, GDQ uses the projected weighted metric equation:

$$
G_{\mu\nu}
+
\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=
\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}
T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm GDQ}
+
\Delta_{\mu\nu}^{\rm tor}.
$$

In the unpolarized macroscopic regime:

$$
\Delta_{\mu\nu}^{\rm tor}
\to
0.
$$

This recovers the leading limit of general relativity as an effective description, without replacing the official action with Einstein--Hilbert.

The fine corrections depend on:

1. residual torsion;
2. global boundary;
3. material medium;
4. global--local normalization.

Therefore, in this chapter, effective gravity enters only as a leading interface and normalization. It is not used to recalibrate the nuclear results.
