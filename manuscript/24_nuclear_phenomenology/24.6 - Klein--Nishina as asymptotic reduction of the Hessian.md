# 24.6 — Klein--Nishina as asymptotic reduction of the Hessian

Compton scattering is:

$$
\gamma(k,\epsilon)+e(p,s)
\longrightarrow
\gamma(k',\epsilon')+e(p',s').
$$

The kinematics originate from Noether conservations in the asymptotic sector:

$$
p+k=p'+k'.
$$

Hence:

$$
\frac{E'}{E}
=
\frac{1}
{1+x(1-\cos\theta)},
\qquad
x=\frac{E}{m_ec^2}.
$$

In GDQ, the amplitude is not postulated from external spinors. It must come from the expansion of the official action around the electronic background:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}[\Phi_e^*+\delta\Phi]
=
\mathcal S_*
+
\frac12
\langle
\delta\Phi,
K_e^{\rm phys}\delta\Phi
\rangle
+
\frac{1}{3!}
\mathcal V_e^{(3)}[\delta\Phi^3]
+
\cdots.
$$

With:

$$
K_e^{\rm phys}
=
P_{\rm phys}
\operatorname{Hess}_{\Phi_e^*}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
P_{\rm phys}.
$$

The photon channel is selected by a Riesz projector onto the massless transverse subspace:

$$
P_\gamma
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\oint_{\mathcal C_\gamma}
(z-K_e^{\rm phys})^{-1}\,dz.
$$

The effective vertex that produces Compton is:

$$
\mathcal V_{\gamma e\gamma}^{\rm eff}
=
P_\gamma
\mathcal V_e^{(3)}
G_e^{\rm phys}
\mathcal V_e^{(3)}
P_\gamma
+
P_\gamma\mathcal V_e^{(4)}P_\gamma.
$$

In the regular asymptotic sector, the two branches of the physical propagator reduce to the standard channels:

$$
\mathcal M_s\propto\frac{1}{2p\cdot k},
\qquad
\mathcal M_u\propto\frac{1}{-2p\cdot k'}.
$$

The averaging over polarizations and circulation states is not inserted as a fundamental shortcut. The photon projector reduces to the transverse projector, the longitudinal terms are annihilated by the Noether/Ward identity of the $U(1)_Q$ channel, and the average of the two Hopf states of the electron reduces to the asymptotic spinorial projector.

The angular factor obtained is:

$$
T_{\rm KN}
=
\frac{E'}{E}
+
\frac{E}{E'}
-
\sin^2\theta.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}
=
\frac{r_e^2}{2}
\left(\frac{E'}{E}\right)^2
\left(
\frac{E'}{E}
+
\frac{E}{E'}
-
\sin^2\theta
\right).
$$

The Thomson limit is recovered when $x\to0$:

$$
\frac{1}{r_e^2}
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}
\longrightarrow
\frac{1}{2}(1+\cos^2\theta).
$$

In the numerical test, at $\theta=90^\circ$, the relative error against Thomson falls from $-1.996007\times10^{-3}$ for $x=10^{-3}$ to $-1.999996\times10^{-6}$ for $x=10^{-6}$.

The final metrological closure still requires obtaining $P_\gamma$, the electronic circulation projector $P_s$, the vertex $\mathcal V_{\gamma e\gamma}^{\rm eff}$ and the prefactor $r_e^2$ directly from the 8D Hessian. The asymptotic reduction, however, already shows that the Klein--Nishina operational structure is inherited from GDQ without changing the fundamental action.
