# 15.4. Rosen-Morse as an auxiliary benchmark

The Rosen--Morse radial route is numerically coherent and must be preserved as a benchmark. Its reduced spectrum is:

$$
\lambda_n
=
(s+n)^2
-
\frac{b^2}{(s+n)^2}.
$$

Historically, the following was used:

$$
e\leftrightarrow n=0,
\qquad
\mu\leftrightarrow n=1,
\qquad
\tau\leftrightarrow n=17.
$$

This identification produces good numerical ratios, but review H-01 showed that:

$$
\text{auxiliary radial index}
\ne
\text{physical GDQ generation index}.
$$

Therefore, in the manuscript:

1. Rosen--Morse is an auxiliary spectral test;
2. the Reg--Reg domain serves as a global comparison limit;
3. finite boundaries measure local stoma response;
4. $n_\tau=17$ is not the ontology of the hierarchy.

The current physical derivation uses tension/topology and the Hessian, not the choice of a radial level.
