# 15.6. Reduced derivation of the muon ratio

$$
\nu_k=\frac{k}{3}.
$$

$$

\frac{1}{\nu_k\alpha}

\frac{3}{k}\alpha^{-1}.
$$

$$
k=2,
$$

$$

$$

$$

2\alpha.
$$

$$

$$
