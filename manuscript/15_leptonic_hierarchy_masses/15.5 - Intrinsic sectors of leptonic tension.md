# 15.5. Intrinsic sectors of leptonic tension

The current GDQ route uses three sectors of tension/topology:

$$
e:
\text{primary torsion},
$$

$$
\mu:
\text{bispatial transverse torsion},
$$

$$
\tau:
\text{three-dimensional saturation}.
$$

The reduced space of charged tensions is:

$$
V\simeq\mathbb R^3.
$$

Each sector occupies orthogonal supports represented by projectors:

$$
P_iP_j=\delta_{ij}P_i.
$$

The construction is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_\ell
\to
K_{\rm phys}^{\ell}
\to
P_k
\to
R_\ell.
$$

The electron is the primitive sector:

$$
R_e=1.
$$

The muon occupies the first bispatial transverse support. The tau corresponds to the three-dimensional saturation of the charged triplet.
