# 15.9. Product background and warped-mixed criterion

On the stationary product background:

$$
g_8=g_B\oplus g_K,
\qquad
K_5=T^5.
$$

And:

$$
\nabla_K f=0,
\qquad
H_{BK}=0,
\qquad
\mathcal C_{BK}=0.
$$

Then:

$$
J=0,
\qquad
\Sigma=JH_\perp^{-1}J^\dagger=0.
$$

For warped/mixed backgrounds:

$$
\nabla_K f\ne0,
\qquad
H_{BK}\ne0,
\qquad
\text{or non-product metric},
$$

the correction is controlled by:

$$
\|\Sigma\|
\le
\frac{j_{\rm mix}^2}{m_\perp^2}.
$$

The stability criterion is:

$$
\frac{j_{\rm mix}^2}{m_\perp^2}
<
\lambda_B^{\rm gap}.
$$

If the inequality holds, the hierarchy is preserved. If it fails, resonances, boundary states, or excitations may appear, but not a new primitive generation without proof of charge, domain, and stability.
