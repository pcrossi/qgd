# Preserved scripts of the leptonic hierarchy

## 1. Criterion

The leptonic hierarchy has historical and exploratory scripts. For this chapter, only final or reduced versions that confirm the current construction are included, without turning boundary attempts into a foundation.

| Script in the chapter | Preserved conceptual origin | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `intrinsic_tension_mu_tau.py` | intrinsic tensions in the leptonic background | Calculates muon and tau ratios by the intrinsic GDQ path. |
| `symbolic_derivation_leptonic_hierarchy.py` | algebraic deduction of the intrinsic path | Shows the muon formula and the branches of the third ratio without using target masses. |
| `koide_saturation.py` | Koide geometric saturation | Verifies the two branches of the geometric condition. |
| `perelman_reduction_3d_bulk8.py` | conditional reduction of the 8D singular sector to the 3D curved factor | Verifies the product/block hypotheses under which Perelman acts only on $B_3$. |
| `stationary_8d_background.py` | leptonic product background | Evaluates $a_W,a_f,a_H,\varepsilon,\lambda_B^{\rm gap}$ and shows $\Delta_{\rm Schur}=0$. |
| `hessian_8d_schur.py` | 8D product inheritance by Schur complement | Verifies product and warped/mixed criterion. |
| `warped_mixed_criterion.py` | control of non-product backgrounds | Calculates the subcritical Schur threshold for future mixtures. |
| `hierarchy_8d_schur_response.py` | 8D response of the ratios | Calculates $R_\ell^{(8)}=R_\ell^{(0)}-\sigma_\ell$ and shift bounds. |
| `rosen_morse_benchmark.py` | auxiliary spectral benchmark | Preserves classified auxiliary route, without using it as ontology. |
| `verify_metrological_calibration.py` | scale calibration | Verifies that dimensionless ratios are independent of the dimensional ruler. |
