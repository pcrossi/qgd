# Koide as geometric saturation

Define:

$$
A_i=\sqrt{R_i}.
$$

The GDQ condition is:

$$
\|A_\perp\|^2=\|A_\parallel\|^2.
$$

It is equivalent to:

$$
Q
=
\frac{R_1+R_2+R_3}
{(\sqrt{R_1}+\sqrt{R_2}+\sqrt{R_3})^2}
=
\frac23.
$$

Given $R_1$ and $R_2$, the third ratio is:

$$
R_{3,\pm}
=
\left[
2(\sqrt{R_1}+\sqrt{R_2})
\pm
\sqrt{
3R_1+12\sqrt{R_1R_2}+3R_2
}
\right]^2.
$$

The heavy branch is used for the charged tau. The light branch requires a proper Hessian before any physical interpretation.
