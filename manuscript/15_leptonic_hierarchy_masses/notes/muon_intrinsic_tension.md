# Muon ratio by intrinsic tension

For bispatial support:

$$
\nu_2=\frac23.
$$

Thus:

$$
R_\mu^{(0)}
=
\frac{1}{\nu_2\alpha}
=
\frac32\alpha^{-1}.
$$

The interface impedance is:

$$
\Delta_\partial=\frac{6}{5}.
$$

The self-energy of two orthogonal circulations is:

$$
\Delta_{\rm self}=2\alpha.
$$

Therefore:

$$
R_\mu
=
\frac32\alpha^{-1}
+
\frac65
+
2\alpha.
$$
