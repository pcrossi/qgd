# GDQ construction of the leptonic hierarchy

## 1. Chain

The construction used in this chapter is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_\ell
\to
K_{\rm GDQ}
\to
P_{\rm phys}^{\dagger}K_{\rm GDQ}P_{\rm phys}
\to
V\simeq R^3
\to
P_k
\to
R_\ell.
$$

Here $\Phi_\ell$ is the charged leptonic background and $P_k$ projects the tension support occupied by the physical sector.

## 2. Constraints

The constraints used are:

1. conservation of circulation by Cauchy;
2. conservation of flux by Noether;
3. orthogonality of tension supports;
4. normalization by $R_e=1$.

## 3. Observables

The observables of the chapter are ratios:

$$
R_\ell=\frac{M_\ell}{M_e}.
$$

Absolute masses require a subsequent metrological scale.
