# 8D Hessian and Schur

The physical 8D Hessian is organized as:

$$
H_8
=
\begin{pmatrix}
H_B & J\\
J^\dagger & H_\perp
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The effective sector is:

$$
H_B^{\rm eff}
=
H_B-JH_\perp^{-1}J^\dagger.
$$

On the product background:

$$
\nabla_Kf=0,
\qquad
H_{BK}=0,
\qquad
J=0.
$$

Thus:

$$
H_B^{\rm eff}=H_B.
$$

On a warped/mixed background:

$$
\Sigma = JH_\perp^{-1}J^\dagger,
\qquad
\|\Sigma\|
\le
\frac{j_{\rm mix}^2}{m_\perp^2}.
$$

The hierarchy is preserved if:

$$
\frac{j_{\rm mix}^2}{m_\perp^2}
<
\lambda_B^{\rm gap}.
$$
