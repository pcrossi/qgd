# 15.7. Koide as geometric saturation and tau ratio

Koide is not used here as an empirical formula. It appears as the scalar form of a geometric condition.

Define tension amplitudes:

$$
A_i=\sqrt{R_i}.
$$

With:

$$
A=(A_1,A_2,A_3),
\qquad
u=\frac1{\sqrt3}(1,1,1).
$$

Decompose:

$$
A=A_\parallel+A_\perp,
\qquad
A_\parallel\parallel u,
\qquad
A_\perp\perp u.
$$

The three-dimensional saturation of GDQ is:

$$
\|A_\perp\|^2
=
\|A_\parallel\|^2.
$$

This is equivalent to:

$$
Q
=
\frac{R_1+R_2+R_3}
{(\sqrt{R_1}+\sqrt{R_2}+\sqrt{R_3})^2}
=
\frac{2}{3}.
$$

With $R_e=1$ and $R_\mu$ derived in the bispatial sector, the heavy branch yields:

$$
R_\tau
\simeq
3477.446405098.
$$

The light branch of the same equation is mathematical, but it is not promoted to a particle without its own domain, boundary, and Hessian.
