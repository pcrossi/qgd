# 15.3. Evolution of construction and conceptual debugging

The central geometric intuition is:

$$
\text{mass is the geometric cost of sustaining a soliton.}
$$

But some preliminary ways of writing this were too strong. In particular:

1. using $M_n-M_p$ as the origin of the electron mass;
2. treating Rosen--Morse as a physical generation index;
3. treating Koide as an external formula;
4. speaking of renormalization/QED as the ontology of GDQ;
5. promising absolute mass without calibration.

In the current version, we preserve the positive evolution:

$$
\text{geometric intuition}
\to
\text{dimensionless ratios}
\to
\text{intrinsic tension}
\to
\text{three-dimensional saturation}
\to
\text{8D Hessian by Schur}.
$$

Attempts that are not the current route do not enter as the main narrative. When useful, they appear only as a benchmark or delimitation note.
