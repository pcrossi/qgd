# 15.1. What mass means in GDQ

In GDQ, mass is not a number pasted onto a point particle. Mass is the inertial cost of maintaining a localized geometric configuration.

A material sector possesses:

1. charge/circulation;
2. torsion;
3. stoma boundary;
4. response of the physical Hessian;
5. stable mode in the projected domain.

Therefore:

$$
M_\ell c^2
=
\mathcal E_{\rm GDQ}[\Phi_\ell]
-
\mathcal E_{\rm vac}.
$$

For leptonic ratios, we use:

$$
R_\ell
=
\frac{M_\ell}{M_e}.
$$

The electron is taken as the primitive normalization sector:

$$
R_e=1.
$$

This does not claim that GDQ produced the MeV unit from nothing. It states that, once a metrological standard is fixed, the internal ratios can be tested.
