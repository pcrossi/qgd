# Output — positivity and quotient

Classification: algebraic toy model.

Eigenvalues of the form: `[0. 1. 2.]`.

Physical rank after quotient by the kernel: `2`.

| vector | squared norm |
|---|---:|
| `[1.0, 0.0, 0.0]` | 2.00000000 |
| `[0.0, 1.0, 0.0]` | 1.00000000 |
| `[0.0, 0.0, 1.0]` | 0.00000000 |
| `[1.0, 1.0, 3.0]` | 3.00000000 |

Conclusion: zero-norm vectors must be quotiented out before Hilbertian completion, as in $\mathcal D_+/(\mathcal N+\mathcal G)$.
