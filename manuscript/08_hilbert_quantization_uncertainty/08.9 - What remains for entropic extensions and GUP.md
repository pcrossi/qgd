# What remains for entropic extensions and GUP

In addition to the operational inequalities demonstrated in this chapter, there are important geometric ideas that must remain as future extensions:

- BBM entropic uncertainty;
- Perelman functional;
- minimum length;
- GUP;
- Fubini--Study geometry;
- torsional and rotational corrections.

These ideas must not be erased. But they must also not be used as closed theorems in this chapter.

What is closed here is:

1. physical Hilbert space as an operational reconstruction;
2. Wallstrom resolved by the circular phase/U(1) line bundle;
3. Heisenberg by Cauchy-Schwarz in the regular sector;
4. Robertson--Schrödinger by Hermitian positivity.

What remains as a future program:

- deriving a BBM entropic uncertainty directly from the GDQ measure;
- connecting Perelman collapse to entropic limits without postulating the result;
- obtaining a GUP from horizon backgrounds, gravitational and horizon backgrounds, or cosmological gluing;
- calculating metrological torsional corrections in real apparatuses;
- treating effective non-Hermitian sectors via boundaries, dissipation, and extension of measurement theory.

Thus the chapter ends with a clean separation:

$$
\boxed{
\text{operational uncertainty closed in the regular sector;}
\qquad
\text{deep geometric extensions remain a future program.}
}
$$
