# Reconstruction by reflection positivity

The operational reconstruction starts with an effective Euclidean sector of GDQ. The goal is not to replace the official action with another functional. The goal is to ask when the geometric functionals of the theory induce a positive norm structure.

We choose a domain of positive temporal functionals:

$$
\mathcal D_+.
$$

These functionals depend on admissible geometric configurations and have support on the positive temporal half-space of the reconstructed sector.

If $\Theta$ is the Euclidean temporal reflection of the sector, we define the form:

$$
(F,G)_E
=
\langle \Theta F\,G\rangle_E.
$$

The central condition is reflection positivity:

$$
\langle \Theta F\,F\rangle_E\ge0
\qquad
\text{for all }F\in\mathcal D_+.
$$

When this condition holds, the zero-norm functionals form:

$$
\mathcal N
=
\{F\in\mathcal D_+:\langle \Theta F\,F\rangle_E=0\}.
$$

The quotient by $\mathcal N$ removes operationally indistinguishable states. Completion yields the Hilbert space.

High-energy geometric redundancies:

$$
\mathcal G.
$$

They include diffeomorphisms, choices of section, longitudinal directions, and modes that do not correspond to physical variations of the sector.

Thus, the physical definition is:

$$
\mathcal H_{\rm phys}
=
\overline{
\mathcal D_+/
(\mathcal N+\mathcal G)
}.
$$

This construction is conditional. It holds for sectors where positivity, domains, and reflection are established.

> [!note]- OS criterion for operational Lorentzian time
> [[notes/os_criterion_lorentzian_time|OS criterion for operational Lorentzian time]]

The important point is that the condition does not state:

$$
t=-i\tau.
$$

Physical time in GDQ was treated in previous chapters via complex causality and the global-local bridge. Reflection positivity is a sectorial operational reconstruction, not the isolated origin of physical time.
