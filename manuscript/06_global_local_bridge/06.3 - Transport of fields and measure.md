# Transport of fields and measure

The convergence of metrics does not automatically transport the other fields. We need to construct backgrounds in $M_\varepsilon$ that, when viewed in the pointed charts, reproduce the local background.

## The local background

Denote by

$$
X_0=(g_0,J_0,f_0)
$$

an admissible local background containing the stoma $Y_{\rm st}$. The derived quantities are

$$
\omega_0(\cdot,\cdot)=g_0(J_0\cdot,\cdot),
$$

$$
H_0=d_{J_0}^c\omega_0,
$$

and

$$
\mathcal U_0
=\frac{e^{-(f_0+\bar f_0)/2}}{(4\pi z_\tau)^4}.
$$

The adjective "admissible" means that $X_0$ satisfies the variational, normalization, regularity, and boundary conditions of the studied sector. The bridge does not create this background; it transports it.

## Localized insertion

If $X_\varepsilon^{\rm vac}$ is the cosmological background without the defect, choose a cutoff $\chi_\varepsilon$ equal to one in the material region and vanishing before the chart limit. Define, schematically,

$$
X_\varepsilon
=X_\varepsilon^{\rm vac}
+\chi_\varepsilon
(\Phi_{\varepsilon,L_\varepsilon})_*
(X_0-X_0^{\rm vac}).
$$

This formula must be read in coordinates or parametrizations that preserve the geometric constraints. Complex structures are not summed arbitrarily; one uses a chart in the space of compatible structures.

On the compact set where $\chi_\varepsilon=1$,

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*g_\varepsilon\to g_0,
\qquad
\Phi_\varepsilon^*J_\varepsilon\to J_0,
\qquad
\Phi_\varepsilon^*f_\varepsilon\to f_0
$$

in $C^{k,\alpha}_{\rm loc}$.

## Naturalness of torsion

Torsion is not transported as an independent field. Since

$$
H=d_J^c\omega_g
$$

is a natural differential operation,

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*H_\varepsilon
=d_{\Phi_\varepsilon^*J_\varepsilon}^c
(\Phi_\varepsilon^*\omega_\varepsilon).
$$

From the convergence of $g_\varepsilon$ and $J_\varepsilon$ with one additional derivative, it follows that

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*H_\varepsilon
\longrightarrow H_0
$$

in $C^{k-1,\alpha}_{\rm loc}$. This preserves the Bismut relation and avoids introducing an $H$ independent of the official action.

## Transport of density and measure

From the continuity of the exponential,

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*\rho_\varepsilon
=\exp\left[-\frac12
\Phi_\varepsilon^*(f_\varepsilon+\bar f_\varepsilon)
\right]
\longrightarrow\rho_0.
$$

Keeping the same causal branch of $z_\tau$,

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*\mathcal U_\varepsilon
\longrightarrow\mathcal U_0.
$$

To integrate, we also need the Jacobian. The ratio of the measures defines the local unitary identification

$$
I_\varepsilon\eta
=\left(
\frac{\mathcal U_0dV_{g_0}}
{\Phi_\varepsilon^*(\mathcal U_\varepsilon dV_{g_\varepsilon})}
\right)^{1/2}
(\Phi_\varepsilon)_*\eta.
$$

With this choice,

$$
\|I_\varepsilon\eta\|_{\mathcal H_\varepsilon}
=\|\eta\|_{\mathcal H_0}
$$

for perturbations supported in the chart. It is not enough to pull back a function; we must transport its weighted norm.

## The residue produced by the cutoff

The region where $d\chi_\varepsilon\neq0$ produces an error

$$
E_\varepsilon
=O(\varepsilon^2)+o_{L_\varepsilon}(1).
$$

If the local physical Hessian is invertible on the complement of the zero modes, the implicit function theorem provides the correction

$$
\delta X_\varepsilon
=-(K_0^{\rm phys})^{-1}
P^{\rm phys}E_\varepsilon
+O(\|E_\varepsilon\|^2).
$$

Therefore, the approximate background can be corrected to a close exact background when there is a gap. This step is conditional on the spectral result, and not on a saddle between the two spaces.

## Transport lemma

**Lemma 2.** Under local regularity, uniform domination at the causal boundary, and physical gap of the local background, the fields, the derived torsion, the density, and the measure are transported to the global family, preserving the relative charge of the stoma.
