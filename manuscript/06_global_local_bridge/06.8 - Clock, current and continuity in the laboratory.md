# Clock, current and continuity in the laboratory

## The transported clock

In Einstein's space, we write

$$
T^5\times S^3
=T^4\times S_E^1\times S^3.
$$

If $\Theta_E$ is the coordinate of the distinguished cosmological cycle, the clock-form is

$$
\omega_E=R_Ed\Theta_E.
$$

In the pointed chart, with

$$
x^0=R_E\Theta_E,
$$

we have

$$
\Phi_\varepsilon^*\omega_E\longrightarrow dx^0.
$$

For the physical immersion $X:N^4\to M_0$, we define

$$
u=X^*dx^0.
$$

The synchronization at the basepoint fixes the scale of $u$; the causal branch of $\gamma$ fixes its orientation. This prevents an arbitrary rescaling of time when comparing global energy and frequency with local ones.

## Decomposition of a conserved current

Chapter 5 derived the bulk phase current,

$$
\widehat J_S^\mu
=\frac{2\tau}{\hbar\Lambda_C^2}
\mathcal U g^{\mu\bar\nu}
\partial_{\bar\nu}S_R,
$$

with

$$
\nabla_\mu\widehat J_S^\mu=0.
$$

After Lorentzian reconstruction, let $n^a$ be the unit timelike vector dual to the clock-form and $h^a{}_b$ the spatial projector. Any local current can be decomposed as

$$
J^a=\varrho n^a+j^a,
\qquad
n_aj^a=0,
$$

where

$$
\varrho=-n_aJ^a
$$

in the signature convention $(-,+,+,+)$.

Using the $3+1$ decomposition, conservation yields

$$
\mathcal L_n\varrho
+\theta\varrho
+D_aj^a
+a_aj^a=0,
$$

where $\theta=\nabla_an^a$ is the expansion of the leaves, $D_a$ is the spatial derivative, and $a^a=n^b\nabla_bn^a$ is the observer's acceleration. In adapted comoving coordinates, this equation is equivalent to

$$
\partial_t(\sqrt h\,\varrho)
+\partial_i(\sqrt h\,j^i)=0.
$$

In the approximately inertial and flat laboratory,

$$
\sqrt h\simeq1,
$$

and we obtain

$$
\partial_t\varrho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0.
$$

## When this is the Madelung continuity

To identify

$$
\varrho=\rho,
\qquad
\mathbf j=\rho\mathbf v,
$$

it is necessary that the causal pullback of the action yields the canonical symplectic form

$$
\int dt\int_{\Sigma_t}
\rho\,\partial_tS_R\,d\Sigma_t
$$

with the correct kinetic normalization. Varying $S_R$ in this reduced action,

$$
\delta S_{\rm red}
=\int dt\int_{\Sigma_t}
\left[
\rho\,\partial_t\delta S_R
+\frac{\rho}{m_{\rm eff}}
\nabla S_R\cdot\nabla\delta S_R
\right]d\Sigma_t.
$$

Integrating by parts,

$$
\delta S_{\rm red}
=-\int dt\int_{\Sigma_t}
\left[
\partial_t\rho
+\nabla\cdot
\left(\rho\frac{\nabla S_R}{m_{\rm eff}}\right)
\right]
\delta S_R\,d\Sigma_t.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\partial_t\rho
+\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf v)=0,
\qquad
\mathbf v=\frac{\nabla S_R}{m_{\rm eff}}.
$$

## Status of the conclusion

The bridge already demonstrates the transport of the clock-form, the measure, and the conserved current. The decomposition of a covariantly conserved current yields the time balance law above. The identification of its temporal density with the constitutive $\rho$, with a unit coefficient in the term $\rho\partial_tS_R$, remains **conditional on the evaluation of the causal pullback of the official action's symplectic form**.

Direct evaluation locates the condition with greater precision. The obtained momentum is

$$
\Pi_{S_R}=n_\mu\widehat J_S^\mu,
$$

therefore it is proportional to $\rho\,n\cdot dS_R$, and not just to $\rho$. The closure requires demonstrating in the physical sector

$$
\Pi_{S_R}^{\rm lab}=\rho_{\rm lab}.
$$

The transverse normalization, by itself, does not prove this equality. See [[../notes/equations/Audit of the canonical term rho d_t S_R|Audit of the canonical term rho d_t S_R]].

The ADM calculation recorded in that note shows that equality is preserved in the stationary comoving sector when imposed on the initial data, but does not constitute a constraint of the general dynamics. It also remains necessary to construct the causal map relating $d\tau/\tau$ to $dt$ to evaluate the temporal coefficient.

## Physical significance of the Madelung polarization

The bridge does not claim that every mathematical state of GDQ is already a Madelung fluid. It transports the complete space and allows us to distinguish within it the sector in which

$$
p_\rho=0,
\qquad
\Pi_{S_R}=\sqrt h\rho.
$$

In this sector, the amplitude does not have an independent kinetic oscillation and the phase momentum is exactly the transported density. Usual quantum mechanics appears, therefore, as the effective dynamics of this stable polarization, not as the entire off-shell dynamics of GDQ.

Solutions external to the Madelung sector still belong to the official action. They represent out-of-equilibrium geometric possibilities until their stability, production, and observability are calculated. It is not necessary to eliminate them from the theory or to add terms to the action; it is necessary to classify them by the physical Hessian and the causal and boundary conditions.

This locates exactly the pending task: it is not the conservation law that is missing, but the canonical normalization that identifies the bulk current with the Madelung density measured in the laboratory.
