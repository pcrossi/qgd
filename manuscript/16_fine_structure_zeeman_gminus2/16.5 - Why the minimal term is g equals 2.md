# 16.5. Why the minimal term is $g$ equals 2

The minimal normalization follows from a simple comparison between two ways of writing the same magnetic flux.

By QGD circulation:

$$
\mu
=
\frac{q}{mc}C.
$$

By the operational convention of the magnetic moment:

$$
\mu
=
g\frac{q}{2mc}C.
$$

Since both expressions describe the same Noether channel in the minimal sector, it follows:

$$
g_0=2.
$$

The physical content is important: $g=2$ is not an algebraic coincidence. It expresses that charge and circulation belong to the same conserved flux. If the elementary circulation is $\hbar/2$, the minimal magnetic normalization must appear with a factor of two in the operational convention.
