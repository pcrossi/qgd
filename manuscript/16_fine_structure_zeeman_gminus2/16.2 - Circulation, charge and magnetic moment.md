# 16.2. Circulation, charge and magnetic moment

In QGD, spin and magnetic moment do not start as abstract matrices. The material sector possesses a conserved circulation. For a unit axis $n$, the two elementary channels are:

$$
C_\pm
=
\pm
\frac{\hbar}{2}n.
$$

The charge couples this circulation to the external field. The minimal magnetic moment is:

$$
\mu
=
\gamma_0 C,
\qquad
\gamma_0=\frac{q}{mc}.
$$

The usual operational definition writes:

$$
\mu
=
g\frac{q}{2mc}C.
$$

Comparing the two expressions, the minimal response requires:

$$
g_0=2.
$$

This result does not depend on importing the Dirac equation. It expresses a geometric compatibility: the same circulation that carries the Noether flux also carries the minimal magnetic response.
