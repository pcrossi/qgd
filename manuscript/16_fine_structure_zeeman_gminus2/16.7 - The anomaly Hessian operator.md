# 16.7. The anomaly Hessian operator

To calculate the anomaly, one fixes the lepton circulation and applies a weak magnetic source. The constrained functional is:

$$
\mathscr I[\Phi,\lambda;B]
=
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}[\Phi]
-B\,M[\Phi]
-\lambda
\left(
\mathcal C[\Phi]-C_\ell
\right).
$$

In the leptonic background:

$$
\Phi=\Phi_\ell+\eta.
$$

Linearizing the circulation:

$$
\mathcal C[\Phi_\ell+\eta]
=
C_\ell+\langle c_\ell,\eta\rangle+O(\eta^2).
$$

Linearizing the magnetic moment:

$$
M[\Phi_\ell+\eta]
=
M[\Phi_\ell]+\langle m_\ell,\eta\rangle+O(\eta^2).
$$

The constrained Hessian is:

$$
H_{C,\ell}
=
P_C^\dagger
\left.
\delta^2\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\right|_{\Phi_\ell}
P_C.
$$

The projector $P_C$ restricts fluctuations to the subspace that preserves the fixed circulation and removes redundant directions.

The magnetic source separates into a minimal part and a transverse part:

$$
m_\ell
=
\gamma_{0,\ell}c_\ell+m_{\perp,\ell}.
$$

The minimal part generates $g=2$. The anomaly is the transverse response:

$$
a_\ell
=
\frac{1}{\gamma_{0,\ell}}
\frac{
\langle c_\ell,H_{C,\ell}^{+}m_{\perp,\ell}\rangle
}{
\langle c_\ell,H_{C,\ell}^{+}c_\ell\rangle
}.
$$

This is the object that must be calculated for complete metrology. It transforms the question "what is the loop?" into the correct QGD question: what is the contraction of the physical Hessian with the transverse magnetic source?

The chapter also preserves the analysis of the upper channels. The important result is twofold:

1. a new universal direct linear source in a uniform magnetic field is forbidden by Hodge orthogonality;
2. the allowed upper route is the mixture of the physical Hessian, in particular the channel mediated by the density.

In symbols, for the phase cycle:

$$
h=\frac{d\vartheta}{2\pi},
\qquad
e_k\propto d\sin(k\vartheta),
$$

we have:

$$
\langle h,e_k\rangle=0
\qquad
(k\ge1).
$$

Therefore:

$$
\mu_{2,\ell}^{\rm direct}=0.
$$

The robust upper coupling found in the reduced truncation appears as:

$$
T_{123}\simeq -2\pi,
$$

that is, the leading mode and the upper mode communicate through the density $\operatorname{Re}f$. Thus, the missing metrological datum is not an adjustable $\mu_2$, but the density profile of the physical leptonic saddle.
