# Factor $1/(2\pi)$

In the phase cycle $S^1$, the normalized harmonic 1-form is:

$$
h
=
\frac{d\vartheta}{2\pi}.
$$

It satisfies:

$$
\int_{S^1}h=1.
$$

Its norm on the unit cycle is:

$$
\langle h,h\rangle
=
\int_0^{2\pi}
\frac{d\vartheta}{(2\pi)^2}
=
\frac{1}{2\pi}.
$$

The first transverse magnetic channel weighs this norm by the electrogeometric intensity $\alpha$:

$$
a^{(1)}
=
\alpha\langle h,h\rangle
=
\frac{\alpha}{2\pi}.
$$

This reproduces the leading scale known operationally as the Schwinger term, but here it is presented as a geometric response of the phase cycle.
