# Coupling by 1-form and 2-form in Zeeman

The usual coupling by potential can be written by current:

$$
S_A
=
\frac{q}{c}
\int J^\mu A_\mu\,dV.
$$

When the current comes from a localized circulation, one can write:

$$
J^\mu
=
\nabla_\alpha \mathcal T^{\alpha\mu}.
$$

Integrating by parts:

$$
S_A
=
-\frac{q}{c}
\int \mathcal T^{\alpha\mu}\nabla_\alpha A_\mu\,dV.
$$

The antisymmetric part yields:

$$
S_{\rm int}
=
\frac{q}{2c}
\int
\mathcal T^{\alpha\mu}F_{\alpha\mu}\,dV.
$$

In the magnetic case $B=B_z\hat z$:

$$
S^{\alpha\beta}F_{\alpha\beta}
=
2S_zB_z.
$$

This is an effective reduction of the external source coupling to the QGD background, not a new fundamental action.
