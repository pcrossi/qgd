# Zeeman by Noether and isotropy

In the linear regime:

$$
E(C,B)
=
E_0(C^2)-\gamma_{\rm eff}C\cdot B.
$$

Varying the orientation of $C$ with fixed magnitude, the stationary condition is:

$$
\delta E
=
-\gamma_{\rm eff}\delta C\cdot B
=0.
$$

Since $\delta C$ is orthogonal to $C$, this implies:

$$
C\times B=0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
C_\pm
=
\pm
\frac{\hbar}{2}
\frac{B}{|B|}.
$$

The Zeeman splitting is:

$$
\Delta E
=
E_+-E_-
=
-\gamma_{\rm eff}\hbar |B|.
$$

Physically, the apparatus selects the axis; Noether preserves the circulation; the Hessian determines the stiffness of the response.
