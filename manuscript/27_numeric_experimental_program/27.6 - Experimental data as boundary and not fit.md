# 27.6 — Experimental data as boundary and not fit

Experimental data is necessary. The key point is when it enters the calculation.

Data can enter as an independent boundary condition:

$$
B_{\rm app},\quad
L_{\rm cavity},\quad
T_{\rm detector},\quad
R_H,\quad
H_0.
$$

These are data from the apparatus, the material, or the observed universe. They define the problem.

What is not permitted is choosing an internal coefficient after the comparison and calling it derived.

$$
\boxed{
\text{experimental data as boundary/apparatus}
\ne
\text{experimental data used to fit the theory.}
}
$$

Data priorities:

1. CODATA for reference constants;
2. PDG for masses, widths, moments, and mixing parameters;
3. NUBASE/AME/ENSDF for nuclear data;
4. SPARC/RAR for galactic curves;
5. Planck/BAO/SNe for cosmology;
6. spectroscopy of electronic and muonic hydrogen;
7. real specifications of Stern--Gerlach, detectors, solenoids, fibers, rings, and cavities.
