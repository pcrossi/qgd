# 27.5 — Methodology preserved in the manuscript

The manuscript must not depend on external work directories. The operational methodology has therefore been preserved here as part of Chapter 27 itself. Operational files outside the manuscript can continue to exist as a work layer, but they are not necessary to understand or reproduce the positive line presented.

## Numerical Layer

The historical numerical layer preserves scripts, outputs, no-gos, and corrected routes. It is important for traceability, but it is not a superior authority to the consolidated chapters, the technical memory, and the proof notes.

The manuscript must migrate only:

1. final scripts;
2. canonical reduced versions;
3. methodology verifiers;
4. outputs that sustain a preserved comparison.
