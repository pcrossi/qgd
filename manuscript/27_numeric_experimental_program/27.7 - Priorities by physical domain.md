# 27.7 — Priorities by physical domain

## Matter and particles

Priority:

1. warped/mixed backgrounds for masses;
2. differential baryonic form factors;
3. upper magnetic channel of $g-2$;
4. local protonic Hessian for hydrogen;
5. global neutral operator for neutrinos.

## Field, confinement, and color

Priority:

1. self-contained Wilson loops;
2. tension per declared radius/boundary;
3. gap by physical Hessian;
4. comparison with lattice/QCD only as an operational benchmark.

Yang--Mills appears as a sectorial effective reduction, not as the fundamental action of GDQ.

## Cosmology and gravitation

Priority:

1. single cosmological solver;
2. $K_{\rm cos}^{\rm phys}$;
3. joint comparison with $H(z)$, BAO, SNe, and CMB;
4. lensing, growth, and Bullet Cluster;
5. 8D covariant black holes.

## Apparatuses and measurement

Priority:

1. Stern--Gerlach with real $\mathsf R_{\rm app}$;
2. double slit with material detector;
3. Wheeler with real interferometer;
4. Aharonov--Bohm with real solenoid;
5. Sagnac with real fiber or ring.

The pattern is:

$$
J_{\rm app}^{\rm classical}
\to
\delta\Phi_{\rm app}
\to
\mathsf R_{\rm app}
\to
\Gamma_{\rm det}
\to
\text{readout}.
$$
