# 18.7. Transverse gap and geometric operator

The reduced gap of the tube is:

$$
\Delta_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp}
>
0.
$$

The general spectral path uses the operator:

$$
\mathcal H_{\rm LM}\phi
=
-
\Delta_{\rm LB}\phi
+
\left(
R+|\nabla f|^2-\frac{1}{12}|H|^2
\right)\phi.
$$

If the effective geometry satisfies the Ricci--Bismut bound:

$$
R_{ij}
+
\nabla_i\nabla_j f
-
\frac14H_{ikm}H_j{}^{km}
\ge
\Lambda_0g_{ij},
\qquad
\Lambda_0>0,
$$

then:

$$
\lambda_1
\ge
c_D\Lambda_0
>
0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\Delta
=
\hbar\sqrt{\lambda_1}
>
0.
$$

This is the effective geometric gap. In the isomorphic GDQ--Yang--Mills sector, it is transported as the operational mass gap of the color sector.

The homogeneous radial mode of the throat has an independent verification when the torsional charge is conserved. For:

$$
\mathcal W_Q(R)
=
\tau
\left(
\frac6{R^2}
-
\frac{Q_T^2}{2\pi^2R^6}
\right)
+3\log R,
$$

the saddle satisfies:

$$
R^6-4\tau R^4+\frac{\tau Q_T^2}{\pi^2}=0.
$$

The constrained second variation is:

$$
K_R
=
\frac{6(3R^2-8\tau)}{R^4}.
$$

Thus:

$$
K_R>0
\quad
\Longleftrightarrow
\quad
R^2>\frac83\tau.
$$

In the reduced solution:

$$
R=1.03707435228632,
\qquad
\tau=0.274900522513626,
$$

and:

$$
K_R=5.32888850629080>0.
$$
