# 18.6. Linear law tension and area law

The correct variational form does not assume constant area only. It uses:

$$
\delta E[q]=0,
\qquad
\partial_z\mathcal L_\perp=0.
$$

For a tube between sources separated by geodesic coordinate $z\in[0,r]$:

$$
E[q]
=
\int_0^r
\mathcal L_\perp(q,q')\,dz.
$$

Since $\partial_z\mathcal L_\perp=0$, the Beltrami identity holds:

$$
\sum_a
q_a'
\frac{\partial\mathcal L_\perp}{\partial q_a'}
-
\mathcal L_\perp
=
{\rm constant}.
$$

In the translationally invariant minimizer, $q'(z)=0$.

So:

$$
\mathcal L_\perp(q_0,0)
=
\sigma
=
\text{constant}.
$$

Hence:

$$
V(r)
=
\sigma r+O(1).
$$

In the language of the effective Wilson loop:

$$
\langle W(C)\rangle
\sim
\exp[-\sigma A_{\min}(C)].
$$

This is the area law of the effective geometric sector of GDQ.

In the formulation by tubular saddles, the action difference in the holonomy sector $C$ takes the form:

$$
{\rm Re}\,\mathcal S[q_C^\ast]
-
{\rm Re}\,\mathcal S[q_0]
=
\sigma_{\rm cl}A_{\min}(C)
+\mu_{\rm cl}P(C)
+O(1).
$$

After Laplace expansion on the physical thimble, the determinant of the Hessian renormalizes the tension:

$$
\sigma_{\rm eff}
=
\sigma_{\rm cl}+\delta\sigma+O(\hbar^2).
$$

The area limit exists by subadditivity when there is a transverse gap and locality:

$$
\sigma_{\rm eff}
=
\lim_{A\to\infty}
-\frac{\hbar}{A}
\log
\left|
\langle\mathcal H(C)\rangle
\right|.
$$
