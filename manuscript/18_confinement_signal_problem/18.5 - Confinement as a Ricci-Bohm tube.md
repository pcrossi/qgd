# 18.5. Confinement as a Ricci--Bohm tube

Confinement in GDQ begins at the Ricci--Bohm transverse neck. The central objects are:

$$
\mathcal A_0
=
\pi r_\perp^2,
$$

$$
\Delta_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp},
$$

and:

$$
\sigma_{\rm GDQ}
=
\mathcal S_\perp[q_\ast]
-
\mathcal S_\perp[q_{\rm vac}]
>
0.
$$

In the homogeneous reduced model:

$$
\sigma_{\rm GDQ}
=
\int_0^{r_\perp}
2\pi s\,ds\,
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^4}
=
\pi
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^2}.
$$

The factor $\pi$ is the area integral of the circular cross-section. It is not an adjustment.

Equivalently, the same factor appears as the curvature index of the transverse Ricci--Bohm cap. In the hemispherical cap with a geodesic boundary:

$$
\int_{\rm cap}R_2\,dA=4\pi,
$$

and the reduced on-shell coefficient is:

$$
C_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac14
\int_{\rm cap}R_2\,dA
=
\pi.
$$

With $r_\perp=0.86\,{\rm fm}$:

$$
\sigma_{\rm GDQ}
=
0.838184142752\,{\rm GeV/fm}.
$$

Using the canonical surface radius:

$$
r_p=0.840778765450\,{\rm fm},
$$

we have:

$$
\sigma_{\rm GDQ}
=
0.876946044304\,{\rm GeV/fm}.
$$

The effective compressed probe radius $r_{\rm eff}=0.8354\,{\rm fm}$ provides:

$$
\sigma_{\rm eff}
=
0.888274921594\,{\rm GeV/fm}.
$$

These values are compared later with $\sigma_{\rm had}\simeq0.89\,{\rm GeV/fm}$; the reference value does not enter the derivation.
