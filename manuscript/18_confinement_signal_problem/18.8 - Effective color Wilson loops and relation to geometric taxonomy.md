# 18.8. Effective color, Wilson loops, and relation to geometric taxonomy

Color enters through the internal geometry:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
E_{\rm int}
\to
G_{\rm eff}
=
\operatorname{Aut}_{\rm GDQ}(E_{\rm int})
\to
SU(3)_C
\to
A_C
\to
F_C.
$$

In the effective classical limit:

$$
A_C
\in
\Omega^1(N,\mathfrak{su}(3)),
$$

and:

$$
F_C
=
dA_C+A_C\wedge A_C.
$$

Effective Wilson loop:

$$
W_R(C)
=
\operatorname{Tr}_R
\mathcal P
\exp
\left(
i\oint_C A_C
\right).
$$

The sectorial status is a $*$-isomorphism of observables:

$$
\mathfrak H_\Theta:
\mathcal A_{\rm YM}^{\rm red}
\to
\mathcal A_{\rm GDQ}^{\rm red}.
$$

In the reduced physical sector:

$$
O_{\rm YM}
\text{ holds}
\quad
\Longleftrightarrow
\quad
\mathfrak H_\Theta(O_{\rm YM})
\text{ holds in GDQ}.
$$

Thus, GDQ recovers classical Yang--Mills and proves its operational sector of color/quarks in the isomorphic domain. This does not alter the official action.

The content of the isomorphism is operational. The fundamental action of GDQ is not identified with the Yang--Mills action. Instead, the reduced observables are identified:

$$
W_{\rm YM}(C)
\longleftrightarrow
\mathcal H_{\rm GDQ}(\Theta^{-1}C),
$$

$$
V_{\rm YM}(r)
\longleftrightarrow
V_{\rm GDQ}(r),
$$

$$
\sigma_{\rm YM}
\longleftrightarrow
\sigma_{\rm GDQ},
$$

and:

$$
\Delta_{\rm YM}
\longleftrightarrow
\Delta_{\rm GDQ}.
$$

A useful symbolic verification is the confining transfer function:

$$
F_\mu(k^2)
=
-\frac{8\pi\sigma}{(k^2+\mu^2)^2},
$$

whose subtracted potential satisfies:

$$
\left.\lim_{\mu\to0^+}
\sigma\frac{1-e^{-\mu r}}{\mu}
=
\sigma r.
$$
