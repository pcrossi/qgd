# 18. Confinement, color, and the signal problem

This chapter separates two topics that usually appear mixed:

1. the fermionic signal problem, treated by GDQ as phase, holonomy, interfaces, and positive measure;
2. confinement, treated as a Ricci--Bohm tube with positive tension, area law, and transverse gap.

GDQ does not postulate QCD, Yang--Mills, gluons, or point quarks as a fundamental ontology. What occurs is different:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Classical Yang--Mills emerges as an effective language of the internal connection of GDQ.}
}
$$

In the reduced physical sector where the isomorphism of observables was constructed, this statement is strong. Outside this sector, it should not be sold as a universal proof.
