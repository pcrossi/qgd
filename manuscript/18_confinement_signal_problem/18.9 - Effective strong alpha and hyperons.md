# 18.9. Effective strong alpha and hyperons

The reduced construction contains an analytical proposal for the effective coupling:

$$
\psi(\theta)
=
\phi_0(\theta)
+
\lambda
\int_0^{2\pi}
K(\theta,\theta')\psi(\theta')\,d\theta'.
$$

With:

$$
\phi_0=\frac{3}{4\pi},
\qquad
K=I,
\qquad
\lambda=-\frac1{2\pi},
$$

one has transmission:

$$
T_{\rm transm}
=
\frac12.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\alpha_s^{\rm eff}
=
\frac12\frac{3}{4\pi}
=
\frac{3}{8\pi}
\approx
0.119366.
$$

This is a sectorial effective coupling, not the full running of QCD.

The same reduced layer provides a phenomenological estimate of global hyperon polarization:

$$
P_\Lambda
\approx
0.85\%.
$$

This block tests the coupling of torsion/vorticity in an effective quark-gluon plasma. It is not an independent proof of confinement.
