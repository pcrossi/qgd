# Signal as phase and holonomy

The GDQ decomposition is:

$$
f=-\ln\rho+i\frac{S_R}{\hbar}.
$$

Hence:

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2}>0.
$$

For fermionic exchange:

$$
S_R(P_{ij}Z)=S_R(Z)+\pi\hbar.
$$

Then:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}(P_{ij})
=
e^{i\pi}
=
-1.
$$

The signal is phase monodromy, not negativity of the measure.
