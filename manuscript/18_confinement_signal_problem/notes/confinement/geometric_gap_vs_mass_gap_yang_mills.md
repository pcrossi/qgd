# Geometric gap and effective mass gap

The effective geometric operator is:

$$
\mathcal H_{\rm LM}
=
-\Delta_{\rm LB}
+
R+|\nabla f|^2-\frac{1}{12}|H|^2.
$$

With the bound:

$$
R_{ij}
+
\nabla_i\nabla_j f
-
\frac14H_{ikm}H_j{}^{km}
\ge
\Lambda_0g_{ij},
$$

and $\Lambda_0>0$, we obtain:

$$
\lambda_1\ge c_D\Lambda_0>0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\Delta=\hbar\sqrt{\lambda_1}>0.
$$

In the isomorphic GDQ--Yang--Mills sector, this geometric gap is translated as the operational mass gap of the color sector.
