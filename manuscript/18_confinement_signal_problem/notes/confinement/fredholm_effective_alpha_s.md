# Fredholm and $\alpha_s^{\rm eff}$

The reduced equation is:

$$
\psi
=
\phi_0
+
\lambda K\psi.
$$

With:

$$
\phi_0=\frac{3}{4\pi},
\qquad
K=I,
\qquad
\lambda=-\frac1{2\pi},
$$

in the effective periodic discretization there occurs:

$$
T_{\rm transm}=\frac12.
$$

Then:

$$
\alpha_s^{\rm eff}
=
T_{\rm transm}\frac{3}{4\pi}
=
\frac{3}{8\pi}.
$$

Status: sectorial effective coupling, not a complete running.
