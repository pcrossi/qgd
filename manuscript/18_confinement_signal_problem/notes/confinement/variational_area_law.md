# Variational area law

If the stationary transverse profile satisfies:

$$
\delta E[q]=0,
\qquad
\partial_z\mathcal L_\perp=0,
$$

then:

$$
\mathcal L_\perp(q_0,0)=\sigma.
$$

Thus:

$$
V(r)=\sigma r+O(1).
$$

For a contour $C$:

$$
\langle W(C)\rangle
\sim
\exp[-\sigma A_{\min}(C)].
$$

This is the area law in the effective geometric sector.
