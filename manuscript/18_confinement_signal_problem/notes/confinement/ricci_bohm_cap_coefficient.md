# Coefficient of the Ricci--Bohm cap

This note demonstrates why the factor $\pi$ in the reduced tension is not an adjustment. It is the geometric index of the primitive cross-section.

## 1. Transverse Sector Used

The primitive transverse cap is a compact 2-section with a geodesic boundary:

$$
ds_\perp^2
=
r_\perp^2
\left(
d\chi^2+\sin^2\chi\,d\theta^2
\right),
\qquad
0\le\chi\le\frac\pi2.
$$

The boundary at $\chi=\pi/2$ is geodesic. By the Gauss--Bonnet theorem:

$$
\int_{\rm cap}K\,dA=2\pi.
$$

In two dimensions:

$$
R_2=2K.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\int_{\rm cap}R_2\,dA=4\pi.
$$

## 2. Reduced On-Shell Coefficient

In the transverse Ricci--Bohm reduction of GDQ, the on-shell balance between curvature and Bohm pressure leaves the index:

$$
C_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac14
\int_{\rm cap}R_2\,dA.
$$

Therefore:

$$
C_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac14(4\pi)
=
\pi.
$$

This coefficient belongs to the transverse cap, not to the Yang--Mills action.

## 3. Reduced Tension

The reduced formula is:

$$
\sigma_{\rm GDQ}
=
C_{\rm GDQ}
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^2}
=
\pi\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^2}.
$$

With:

$$
r_\perp=0.86\,{\rm fm},
\qquad
\hbar c=0.1973269804\,{\rm GeV\,fm},
$$

we obtain:

$$
\Delta_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp}
=
0.229449977209\,{\rm GeV},
$$

and:

$$
\sigma_{\rm GDQ}
=
0.838184142752\,{\rm GeV/fm}
=
0.165396345908\,{\rm GeV}^2.
$$

Comparing later with the usual hadronic scale $\sigma_{\rm had}\simeq0.89\,{\rm GeV/fm}$, the deviation of the primitive cap is:

$$
-5.822006\%.
$$

## 4. Relation to the Direct Disc Integral

The reduced density:

$$
\varepsilon_\sigma
=
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^4}
$$

produces:

$$
\int_0^{r_\perp}
2\pi s\,ds\,
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^4}
=
\pi\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^2}.
$$

Thus, the integral on the projected disc and the index of the cap give the same coefficient.

## 5. Classification

This result is a direct evaluation of a quantity already derived in the reduced transverse sector. It is not the complete 8D solution of the profile $q_\ast$, but it closes the coefficient of the primitive cap.
