# Ricci--Bohm tube and tension

The transverse tube has radius $r_\perp$. The transverse scale is:

$$
\Delta_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp}.
$$

In the reduced homogeneous model:

$$
\varepsilon_\sigma
=
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^4}.
$$

Integrating over the circular cross-section:

$$
\sigma
=
\int_0^{r_\perp}
2\pi s\,ds\,
\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^4}
=
\pi\frac{\hbar c}{r_\perp^2}.
$$

The factor $\pi$ is geometric.
