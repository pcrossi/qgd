# Hyperon polarization and torsion

The preserved phenomenological form is:

$$
P_\Lambda
=
\frac{\hbar\omega_{\rm fluid}}{2k_BT}
\left(
\frac{\chi_{{\rm Fano},n}}{\delta^2}
\right).
$$

In the reduced estimate:

$$
P_\Lambda\approx0.85\%.
$$

This result is a test of the coupling of torsion/vorticity in an effective QGP. It must not be used as a proof of confinement.
