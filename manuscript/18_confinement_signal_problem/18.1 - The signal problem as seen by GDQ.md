# 18.1. The signal problem as seen by GDQ

In the usual formulation, the signal problem appears when oscillatory or negative weights prevent direct probabilistic interpretation. In GDQ, the geometric density is positive:

$$
\rho
=
e^{-(f+\bar f)/2}
>
0.
$$

The phase remains separate:

$$
\chi
=
\frac{S_R}{\hbar}
=
\operatorname{Im}f.
$$

For fermionic exchange:

$$
\rho(P_{ij}Z)=\rho(Z),
$$

but:

$$
S_R(P_{ij}Z)
=
S_R(Z)+\pi\hbar
\quad
(\mathrm{mod}\ 2\pi\hbar).
$$

Therefore:

$$
e^{iS_R/\hbar}
\mapsto
-
e^{iS_R/\hbar}.
$$

The fermionic signal does not live in the positive measure. It lives in the phase/holonomy.
