# 18.10. Comparison with QCD and limits

The chapter closes three strong blocks:

1. the geometric origin of the fermionic signal;
2. confinement by Ricci--Bohm tension and transverse gap;
3. classical Yang--Mills as a sectorial effective reduction.

But it retains three limits:

1. the signal benchmark does not prove a general polynomial algorithm;
2. the confinement calculation does not provide a complete metrology of every experimental channel;
3. the GDQ--Yang--Mills isomorphism holds in the declared reduced physical sector.

The safe statement is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{phase, color, and confinement are responses of geometry, holonomy, and torsion.}
}
$$

And the necessary reservation is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{GDQ is not a universal equivalence outside the isomorphic physical sector.}
}
$$
