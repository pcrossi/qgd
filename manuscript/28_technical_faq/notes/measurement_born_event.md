# Operational Born and individual event

Born's operational rule answers a statistical question:

$$
P(i)=\operatorname{Tr}(\varrho P_i).
$$

It does not by itself describe the physical dynamics of a detector click.

In GDQ, the individual event requires coupling to the apparatus:

$$
J_{\rm app}^{\rm classical}
\to
\delta\Phi_{\rm app}
\to
\mathsf R_{\rm app}
\to
\Gamma_{\rm det}
\to
\text{registration}.
$$

The role of Born is to provide the weights of the channels in the reconstructed physical sector. The role of measure theory is to explain how a macroscopic apparatus selects and stabilizes one of these channels.

This distinction avoids two confusions:

1. transforming operational probability into a complete physical mechanism;
2. inserting collapse as a separate axiom.
