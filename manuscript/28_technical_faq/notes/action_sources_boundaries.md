# Action, sources, and boundaries

A classical source is not a new axiom. It is external data used to ask how the background responds.

If $\Phi$ collectively represents $g$, $J$, $H$, $f$ and the measurement data, the local expansion of the official action around an admissible saddle $\Phi_*$ is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}[\Phi_*+\delta\Phi]
=
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}[\Phi_*]
+\frac12
\langle\delta\Phi,K\delta\Phi\rangle
+O(\delta\Phi^3).
$$

After applying constraints and removing redundancies:

$$
K_{\rm phys}
=
P_{\rm phys}^\dagger K P_{\rm phys}.
$$

An external probe enters as a linear functional:

$$
\delta\mathcal S_{\rm app}
=
\langle J_{\rm app},\delta\Phi\rangle.
$$

The linear response is:

$$
\delta\Phi_{\rm app}
=
-K_{\rm phys}^{-1}J_{\rm app}.
$$

Therefore, the source modifies the solution, not the fundamental law.
