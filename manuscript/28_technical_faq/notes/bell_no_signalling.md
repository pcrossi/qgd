# Bell and absence of signalling

Geometric entanglement means that the prepared state is not factorable by a simple product of local geometries.

For operational absence of signalling (no-signalling), however, it is necessary to prove:

$$
\sum_bP(a,b|x,y)
=
\sum_bP(a,b|x,y')
$$

for any remote choices $y$ and $y'$.

In GDQ, this must be done by:

1. construction of the two classical apparatuses;
2. calculation of the responses $\mathsf R_A$ and $\mathsf R_B$;
3. conditioned evolution;
4. marginalization of unobserved channels;
5. proof that the local marginal is invariant.

Until this chain is executed for a real family of apparatuses, the correct status is operational extension, not a fatal ontological gap.
