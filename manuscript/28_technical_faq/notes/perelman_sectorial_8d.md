# Sectorial Perelman in dimension eight

The correct use of Perelman in GDQ depends on factorization.

In the product background:

$$
M=M_{\rm curved}\times K_{\rm flat},
$$

if:

$$
\operatorname{Ric}_{K}=0,
\qquad
\nabla_K f=0,
\qquad
H_{BK}=0,
$$

then the Hessian separates into blocks:

$$
K_{\rm GDQ}
=
K_{\rm curved}
\oplus
K_{\rm flat}.
$$

Surgeries and singularities controlled by curvature belong to the curved block. The flat block provides degeneracy, holonomy, phase, and internal structure, but does not drive the neckpinch by itself.

If any of the above conditions fail, a mixed coupling arises:

$$
K_{\rm GDQ}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
K_{\rm curved} & K_{\rm mix}\\
K_{\rm mix}^\dagger & K_{\rm flat}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

In this case, the sectorial reduction is not enough. The problem must be solved in the full warped/mixed background.
