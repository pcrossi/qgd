# Effective reductions and the Standard Model

An effective reduction preserves observables in a domain. It does not identify the ontologies.

The admissible pattern is:

$$
\text{GDQ in sector }D
\longrightarrow
\text{effective operator }L_D
\longrightarrow
\text{known formalism}.
$$

Examples:

| Recovered formalism | Status in GDQ |
|---|---|
| Dirac | effective spinorial limit of the Hessian/boundary |
| Pauli | non-relativistic limit with magnetic coupling |
| Yang--Mills | effective internal connection in the color sector |
| Born | operational probability in the physical Hilbert space |
| Lindblad | effective language for open apparatus, not ontology |

The logical error to avoid is:

$$
\text{known formalism}
\Rightarrow
\text{GDQ foundation}.
$$

This inversion would erase exactly what the theory tries to explain.
