# Loops, ghosts, and renormalization

The physical second-order determinant in GDQ must be calculated in the physical space:

$$
\Gamma^{(1)}_{\rm GDQ}
=
\frac12
\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm phys}
\log K_{\rm phys}.
$$

The physical projector incorporates:

1. constraints;
2. boundary conditions;
3. gauge removal;
4. exclusion of non-physical null modes;
5. measure normalization.

If an external language introduces ghosts to represent the Jacobian of the quotient, this is auditing. GDQ does not need to transform these fields into physical entities.

Likewise, "renormalization" must be read as:

$$
\text{spectral projection, boundary response, or effective scale},
$$

not as the free addition of fundamental counterterms.
