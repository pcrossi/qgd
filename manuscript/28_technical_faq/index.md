# 28 — Technical FAQ and Recurrent Objections

This chapter gathers short answers to objections that repeatedly appear when QDQ is read from external languages: operational quantum mechanics, the Standard Model, Perelman geometrization, field theory, experimental metrology, and numerical computation.

The FAQ does not replace the derivations in the manuscript. It functions as a layer of guidance:

$$
\boxed{
\text{the body of the manuscript demonstrates; this chapter classifies objections.}
}
$$
