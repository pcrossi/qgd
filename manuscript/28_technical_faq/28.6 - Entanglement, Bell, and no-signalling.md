# 28.6 — Entanglement, Bell, and no-signalling

In GDQ, entanglement is geometric non-factorization in the multiparticle configuration space. Correlation is not interpreted as a hidden force traveling between detectors. It is a global property of holonomy, boundary, and geometric class of the composite system.

The structural statement is:

$$
\mathcal H_{AB}
\ne
\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B
\quad
\text{as a trivial geometric factorization of the prepared state}.
$$

This formulation resolves the geometric intuition of entanglement, but it does not yet replace the operational Bell calculation for real apparatuses. To prove no-signalling in the experimental sense, it is necessary to show:

$$
P(a|x,y)
=
\sum_bP(a,b|x,y)
$$

independent of the remote choice $y$.

In GDQ language, this requires:

1. two classical apparatuses;
2. two interface impedances, $\mathsf R_A$ and $\mathsf R_B$;
3. multiparticle Hessian;
4. conditioned measurement;
5. marginalization of unobserved channels;
6. proof that the local marginal does not depend on the remote boundary.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{geometric entanglement is formulated; real Bell/no-signalling is an operational extension.}
}
$$
