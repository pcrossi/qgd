# 28.7 — Ghosts, BRST, loops, and renormalization

Ghosts, BRST, and renormalization are not fundamental ontology of GDQ.

They can appear as:

1. external audit;
2. comparative language;
3. way to verify gauge quotients;
4. representation of effective determinants;
5. operational reduction in known sectors.

The intrinsic object of GDQ is:

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm phys}\log
\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}.
$$

The subscript $\rm phys$ is essential. It indicates that gauge modes, redundancies, non-physical null modes, and variations incompatible with constraints have been removed by a physical projector.

If an external audit rewrites this quotient using Jacobians, auxiliary determinants, or BRST notation, this does not change the theory. It is just a control language.

Likewise, the word "loop" must be used with care. In GDQ, higher-order corrections mean expansion of the physical Hessian, boundary response, or spectral determinants over admissible backgrounds. They do not mean the free addition of fundamental counterterms to save a divergence.
