# 28.10 — Operational conclusion

This chapter fixes the technical reading of the manuscript.

GDQ must not be defended by analogy, by isolated good numbers, or by silent substitution with known theories. It must be defended by its own chain:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\text{background}
\to
\text{physical Hessian}
\to
\text{projector}
\to
\text{boundary}
\to
\text{observable}.
$$

The recurrent objections have been classified:

1. sources and boundaries do not change the official action;
2. the Standard Model appears as an operational reduction, not as ontology;
3. Perelman is an auxiliary sectorial structure;
4. operational Born does not exhaust the dynamics of the event;
5. Bell/no-signalling requires real apparatuses;
6. ghosts, BRST, and renormalization are external language when used;
7. good numbers reinforce derivation, but do not replace derivation;
8. conditional results must preserve their hypotheses.

Thus, the FAQ fulfills its function:

$$
\boxed{
\text{it prevents conceptual confusion without replacing the proofs of the manuscript.}
}
$$
