# 22.8. Lamb shift and near field

In pure Coulomb--Dirac:

$$
E(2s_{1/2})
=
E(2p_{1/2}).
$$

Hence, the Lamb shift requires an additional near-field operator:

$$
\Delta E_{\rm Lamb}
=
\langle 2s_{1/2}|
\delta H_{\rm near}
|2s_{1/2}\rangle
-
\langle 2p_{1/2}|
\delta H_{\rm near}
|2p_{1/2}\rangle.
$$

In GDQ:

$$
\delta\mathcal D_{\rm near}
=
\Pi_{\rm spin}
\left(
\mathsf R_p-\mathsf R_{\rm point}
\right)
\Pi_{\rm spin}.
$$

## 1. Diagnosed Scale

Using the metrological value only to diagnose the scale of the missing operator:

$$
\Delta E_{\rm near}^{\rm req}
=
4.374323887281\times10^{-6}\,{\rm eV}.
$$

Equivalent to:

$$
1.057706810320\times10^9\,{\rm Hz}.
$$

This is not a prediction. It is the scale that the near-field Hessian needs to produce.

## 2. Interpretation

The Heun/Hill language remains useful as a radial technique to solve the near field. The GDQ origin of the operator, however, is the DtN/Schur of the protonic surface.
