# 22. The Hydrogen Atom

This chapter is the first complete atomic application of GDQ.

The thesis is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{the hydrogen atom is a spinor probe problem in a protonic background.}
}
$$

GDQ does not postulate the Dirac equation as a fundamental action. It retrieves it as an external spinor reduction of the physical Hessian on the proton background.

The correct chain is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_{p,\ast}
\to
\text{Hess }\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}
\to
\text{atomic spectrum}.
$$

Here $\Phi_{p,\ast}$ is the stationary protonic background and $\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}$ is the effective Dirac--Bismut operator seen by the electron.
