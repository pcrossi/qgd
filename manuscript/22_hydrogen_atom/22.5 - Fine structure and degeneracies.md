# 22.5. Fine structure and degeneracies

The Coulomb--Dirac spectrum depends on $n$ and $j$, but not on $m_j$.

Hence:

$$
\deg(n,j)
=
2j+1.
$$

In pure Coulomb:

$$
E(2s_{1/2})
=
E(2p_{1/2}).
$$

This degeneracy is important: the Lamb shift cannot come from the pure Coulomb--Dirac operator.

## 1. Fine Structure Expansion

The leading expansion is:

$$
E_{nj}
=
m_ec^2
-\frac{m_ec^2\alpha^2}{2n^2}
-\frac{m_ec^2\alpha^4}{2n^4}
\left(
\frac{n}{j+1/2}
-\frac34
\right)
+O(\alpha^6).
$$

In the GDQ text, this means that spin--orbit and relativistic corrections are retrieved as part of the external spinor reduction.

## 2. Physical Meaning

Spin--orbit was not inserted manually. It appears because the probe operator is spinorial and carries a connection.

This is the difference between:

$$
\text{useful radial scalar equation}
$$

and:

$$
\text{complete effective spinor operator}.
$$
