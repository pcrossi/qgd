# Sommerfeld--Dirac spectrum

For the Coulomb--Dirac Hamiltonian:

$$
H_D
=
c\boldsymbol\alpha\cdot\mathbf p
+\beta m_ec^2
-\frac{Z\alpha\hbar c}{r},
$$

the spectrum is:

$$
E_{n\kappa}
=
m_ec^2
\left[
1+
\frac{(Z\alpha)^2}
{
\left(
n-|\kappa|+\sqrt{\kappa^2-(Z\alpha)^2}
\right)^2
}
\right]^{-1/2}.
$$

The binding energy is:

$$
E_{\rm bind}
=
E_{n\kappa}-m_ec^2.
$$

For atomic comparison, the reduced mass is used:

$$
\mu_{ep}
=
\frac{m_em_p}{m_e+m_p}.
$$
