# Protonic domain and boundary

The domain with a finite proton is:

$$
H^1_{\rm loc}
(\mathbb R^3\setminus\mathcal N_p,S\otimes L_Q)
\cap
\mathcal B_p.
$$

The boundary condition is:

$$
\left(
n^a\nabla_a^B+\mathsf R_p
\right)\psi
\big|_{\partial\mathcal N_p}
=
0.
$$

The impedance is the Schur complement:

$$
\mathsf R_p
=
K_{YY}
-K_{YI}K_{II}^{-1}K_{IY}.
$$

This makes the problem self-contained: the proton surface provides the DtN operator that corrects the Lamb shift, hyperfine structure, and form factors.
