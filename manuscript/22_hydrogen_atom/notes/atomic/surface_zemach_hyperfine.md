# Hyperfine and Zemach

The leading Fermi frequency is:

$$
\nu_F
=
\frac{16}{3}
\alpha^2cR_\infty
\left(
\frac{\mu}{m_e}
\right)^3
\frac{\mu_p}{\mu_B}.
$$

The Zemach factor corrects for the finite distribution of charge and magnetization:

$$
\delta_Z
=
-2\alpha
\frac{\mu c}{\hbar}
r_Z.
$$

For coincident shells:

$$
r_Z
=
\frac43r_p.
$$

In the finer layer, the surface response enters the magnetic factor:

$$
\frac{G_M(q)}{\mu_p}
=
j_0(qr_p)
+\beta_{\rm GDQ}\mathcal I_\Sigma(q),
$$

with:

$$
\beta_{\rm GDQ}
=
3(1+\kappa_p).
$$

This insertion shifts the problem from the $10^{-5}$ level to the tens of hertz level in the 21 cm line.

The residual value after these corrections must be associated with the higher magnetic Hessian, the complete hyperfine recoil, and the fine polarizability, rather than being absorbed by a fit.
