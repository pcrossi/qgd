# Leptonic backreaction on proton radius

The linear response of the surface is:

$$
\delta r_p[\ell]
=
-
(H_p^{\rm surf})^{-1}J_\ell.
$$

For $s$ states:

$$
J_\ell
\propto
|\psi_{ns}(0)|^2
\propto
\mu_{\ell p}^3.
$$

Hence:

$$
\frac{\delta r_p[e]}{\delta r_p[\mu]}
=
\left(
\frac{\mu_{ep}}{\mu_{\mu p}}
\right)^3.
$$

Since this number is of the order of $10^{-7}$, the electron backreacts very little on the proton radius compared to the muon.

Using the reduced masses of the electronic and muonic systems,

$$
\mu_{ep}
=
9.104425288917\times10^{-31}\,{\rm kg},
\qquad
\mu_{\mu p}
=
1.692895470098\times10^{-28}\,{\rm kg},
$$

we obtain:

$$
\frac{\mu_{ep}}{\mu_{\mu p}}
=
5.378019759478\times10^{-3},
$$

and therefore:

$$
\left(
\frac{\mu_{ep}}{\mu_{\mu p}}
\right)^3
=
1.555489846616\times10^{-7}.
$$

The table below does not fix the absolute contraction. It only shows the direct consequence of the contact scale if the muonic probe produces an effective contraction at one of the indicated diagnostic scales:

| Assumed muonic contraction | Estimated electronic contraction |
|---:|---:|
| $-0.010000\,{\rm fm}$ | $-1.555489846616\times10^{-9}\,{\rm fm}$ |
| $-0.030000\,{\rm fm}$ | $-4.666469539847\times10^{-9}\,{\rm fm}$ |
| $-0.034000\,{\rm fm}$ | $-5.288665478493\times10^{-9}\,{\rm fm}$ |

The absolute value of $\delta r_p[\mu]$ must not be chosen by the experimental target. In GDQ, it must come from the surface Hessian of the proton:

$$
H_p^{\rm surf}
=
P_{\rm surf}^{\rm phys}
\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
P_{\rm surf}^{\rm phys},
$$

with leptonic source $J_\ell$ obtained by the contact coupling of the probe to the protonic background. Thus, the closed result here is the scale ratio between probes; absolute metrology requires calculating $H_p^{\rm surf}$ and $J_\mu$ directly.
