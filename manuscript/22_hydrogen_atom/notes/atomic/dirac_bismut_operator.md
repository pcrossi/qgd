# Dirac--Bismut operator

The physical Hessian of the protonic background defines the probe operator. In the external spinor sector:

$$
\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}
=
i\hbar c\,\gamma^a e_a{}^\mu
\left(
\nabla_\mu^B+\frac{iQ}{\hbar c}A_\mu^{(p)}
\right)
-m_ec^2.
$$

The Bismut connection is:

$$
\omega_{\mu ab}^{B}
=
\omega_{\mu ab}^{\rm LC}
+K_{\mu ab}^{B}.
$$

In the external limit:

$$
\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}
=
\mathcal D_{\rm Coul}
+\delta\mathcal D_{\rm tor}
+\delta\mathcal D_{\rm near}.
$$

This is the form that retrieves the usual Dirac operator as a reduction, without replacing the official GDQ action.
