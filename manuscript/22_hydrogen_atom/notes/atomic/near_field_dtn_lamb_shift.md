# Lamb shift as near-field DtN

In pure Coulomb--Dirac:

$$
E(2s_{1/2})
=
E(2p_{1/2}).
$$

The Lamb shift is:

$$
\Delta E_{\rm Lamb}
=
\langle 2s|
\delta H_{\rm near}
|2s\rangle
-
\langle 2p|
\delta H_{\rm near}
|2p\rangle.
$$

In GDQ:

$$
\delta\mathcal D_{\rm near}
=
\Pi_{\rm spin}
\left(
\mathsf R_p-\mathsf R_{\rm point}
\right)
\Pi_{\rm spin}.
$$

The Heun/Hill technique resolves the radial part. The physical origin of the operator is the DtN/Schur of the protonic surface.
