# 22.1. The atomic problem and the role of the proton

Hydrogen is not just a Coulombic potential. In GDQ, it is a spinor probe bound to the geometric background of the proton.

The background is:

$$
\Phi_{p,\ast}
=
(g_p,J_p,H_p,f_p,\mathcal U_p)_\ast.
$$

The electron is a charged mode living on this background:

$$
\psi
\in
\Gamma(S\otimes L_Q),
\qquad
Q=-e.
$$

The radial scalar equation is preserved as a useful auxiliary technique, but not as a foundation. The correct atomic foundation is spinorial.

## 1. Physical Chain

The path is:

$$
\text{proton as soliton}
\to
\text{physical Hessian}
\to
\text{probe operator}
\to
\text{surface boundary}
\to
\text{spectrum}.
$$

This avoids two confusions:

1. replacing spin with a fitted scalar equation;
2. importing QED corrections as fundamental ontology.

In GDQ, fine corrections come from the near field, surface, impedance, and Hessian.
