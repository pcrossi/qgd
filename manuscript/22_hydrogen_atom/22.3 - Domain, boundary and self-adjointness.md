# 22.3. Domain, boundary and self-adjointness

The domain of the probe is:

$$
\mathcal D(\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B})
=
H^1_{\rm loc}
(\mathbb R^3\setminus\mathcal N_p,S\otimes L_Q)
\cap
\mathcal B_p.
$$

In the point-like limit:

$$
\psi\in L^2(\mathbb R^3),
\qquad
\psi\text{ regular at }r=0.
$$

With a finite-radius proton, the proton surface imposes:

$$
\left(
n^a\nabla_a^B+\mathsf R_p
\right)
\psi\big|_{\partial\mathcal N_p}
=
0.
$$

## 1. Origin of the Impedance

The surface impedance is:

$$
\left. \mathsf R_p \right|_{\partial\mathcal N_p}
=
K_{YY}
-K_{YI}K_{II}^{-1}K_{IY}.
$$

It is the Schur/DtN complement of the protonic Hessian. This is important: $\mathsf R_p$ is not adjusted level by level.

## 2. What Remains Metrological

Fine closure requires evaluating:

$$
K_{YY},
\qquad
K_{YI},
\qquad
K_{II},
\qquad
\Delta\mathsf R_p.
$$

These blocks control the Lamb shift, hyperfine residue, and the difference between the electric and magnetic radii.
