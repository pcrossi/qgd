# 22.4. Sommerfeld--Dirac spectrum

In the external Coulombian limit:

$$
H_D
=
c\boldsymbol\alpha\cdot\mathbf p
+\beta m_ec^2
-\frac{Z\alpha\hbar c}{r}.
$$

The states have the form:

$$
\psi_{E\kappa m}
=
\frac1r
\begin{pmatrix}
G_{E\kappa}(r)\Omega_{\kappa m}\\
iF_{E\kappa}(r)\Omega_{-\kappa m}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The number $\kappa$ is:

$$
\kappa
=
\begin{cases}
-(j+1/2), & j=\ell+1/2,\\
+(j+1/2), & j=\ell-1/2.
\end{cases}
$$

The spectrum is:

$$
E_{n\kappa}
=
m_ec^2
\left[
1+
\frac{(Z\alpha)^2}
{
\left(
n-|\kappa|+\sqrt{\kappa^2-(Z\alpha)^2}
\right)^2
}
\right]^{-1/2}.
$$

## 1. Calculated Values

With electron--proton reduced mass:

| level | binding energy |
|---|---:|
| $1s_{1/2}$ | $-13.598468300828\,{\rm eV}$ |
| $2s_{1/2}$ | $-3.399628390092\,{\rm eV}$ |
| $2p_{1/2}$ | $-3.399628390092\,{\rm eV}$ |
| $2p_{3/2}$ | $-3.399583130609\,{\rm eV}$ |

Hence:

$$
E(2p_{3/2})-E(2p_{1/2})
=
4.525948315859\times10^{-5}\,{\rm eV}.
$$

And:

$$
E(2s_{1/2})-E(2p_{1/2})
=
0
$$

in the pure Coulomb--Dirac operator.
