# 22.2. Effective Dirac--Bismut operator

The effective operator of the electronic probe is:

$$
\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}\psi
=
\left[
i\hbar c\,\gamma^a e_a{}^\mu
\left(
\nabla_\mu^B+\frac{iQ}{\hbar c}A_\mu^{(p)}
\right)
-m_ec^2
\right]\psi.
$$

With:

$$
\nabla_\mu^B
=
\partial_\mu
+\frac14
\omega_{\mu ab}^{B}
\gamma^a\gamma^b,
$$

and:

$$
\omega_{\mu ab}^{B}
=
\omega_{\mu ab}^{\rm LC}
+K_{\mu ab}^{B}.
$$

> ![[notes/atomic/dirac_bismut_operator]]

## 1. External Limit

In the weak and external limit:

$$
\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}
=
\mathcal D_{\rm Coul}
+\delta\mathcal D_{\rm tor}
+\delta\mathcal D_{\rm near}.
$$

The first term yields Sommerfeld--Dirac. The torsional term provides magnetic/hyperfine structure. The near-field term breaks degeneracies such as the Lamb shift.

## 2. Status

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal D_{p,e}^{B}
\text{ is the effective operator of the GDQ Hessian, not a new fundamental action.}
}
$$
