# 09.2. Conserved positive density of GDQ

GDQ possesses a positive density before any measurement postulate. The constitutive definition is:

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2}.
$$

With:

$$
S_R=\frac{\hbar}{2i}(f-\bar f),
$$

the regular sector can be parameterized by:

$$
\Psi=\sqrt\rho\,e^{iS_R/\hbar}.
$$

Continuity in physical time, obtained in the previous chapter by hydrodynamic reduction and phase conservation, has the form:

$$
\partial_t\rho+\nabla_i(\rho v^i)=0.
$$

If the domain does not allow net flux through the boundary,

$$
\rho v^i n_i\big|_{\partial\Sigma}=0,
$$

then:

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int_\Sigma\rho\,d\Sigma=0.
$$

Therefore, if:

$$
\int_\Sigma\rho\,d\Sigma=1
$$

on an initial leaf, normalization is preserved.

## What this demonstrates

This demonstrates that GDQ has a conserved positive measure in the regular physical sector. This is the geometric foundation of probability.

## What this does not demonstrate on its own

This does not select the basis of a detector. Nor does it prove, by itself, that probabilities in an arbitrary basis are given by projectors. This step uses the reconstructed physical Hilbert space and the operational measurement structure.
