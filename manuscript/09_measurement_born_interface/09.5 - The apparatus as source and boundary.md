# 09.5. The apparatus as source and boundary

The apparatus is not added to the official action as a new fundamental operator. In GDQ, it enters as external classical data of source, constraint, or boundary. This input changes the effective variational problem, not the ontology of the theory.

The chain is:

$$
J_{\rm app}^{\rm classico}
\to
\delta\Phi_{\rm app}
\to
\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\text{R}_{\rm app}
\to
\text{spectral response}
\to
\text{record}.
$$

Here:

- $J_{\rm app}^{\rm classico}$ represents the field, support, detector, or macroscopic material provided by the experiment;
- $\delta\Phi_{\rm app}$ is the induced geometric deformation;
- the Hessian of the official action provides the physical rigidity;
- $\text{R}_{\rm app}$ is the boundary response operator;
- the record is the stabilized macroscopic sector.

A typical reduced form is the Schur complement:

$$
\text{R}_{\rm app}
=
K_{\partial\partial}
-
K_{\partial I}K_{II}^{-1}K_{I\partial}.
$$

Block $I$ represents internal degrees of the apparatus or the interface. Block $\partial$ represents the accessible data on the boundary with the measured system.

This is the technical reason why the measurement is apparatus-dependent without being arbitrary. The apparatus selects the domain and the impedance; the official action provides the Hessian used in this selection.
