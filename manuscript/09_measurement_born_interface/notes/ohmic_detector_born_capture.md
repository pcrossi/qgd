# Ohmic detector, causal filtering and Born capture

This note preserves the positive part of the interface theory: an open apparatus generates dissipation and information through its own macroscopic channel. The construction is effective and conditional on a quadratic reduction of the physical Hessian; it does not alter the official action of GDQ.

## 1. Open channel of the apparatus

Consider an output coordinate $x\ge0$ in the apparatus and a normalized physical mode $y(x,t)T_y$ obtained by modal projection of the physical Hessian of the macroscopic background. The reduced quadratic form is:

$$
S_{\rm canal}^{(2)}
=
\frac{\zeta_A}{2}
\int dt\int_0^\infty dx
\left[
\frac1{c_A^2}(\partial_ty)^2
-(\partial_xy)^2
\right].
$$

The coefficients come from inner products of the projected Hessian:

$$
\frac{\zeta_A}{c_A^2}
=
\langle T_y,K_tT_y\rangle_{\mathcal U_*},
\qquad
\zeta_A
=
\langle T_y,K_xT_y\rangle_{\mathcal U_*}.
$$

Therefore $\zeta_A$ and $c_A$ are data of the concrete apparatus. They are not fundamental constants.

## 2. Retarded DtN

The channel equation is:

$$
\frac1{c_A^2}\partial_t^2y-\partial_x^2y=0.
$$

With $y(0,t)=X(t)$ and an outgoing radiation condition at infinity, write:

$$
y(x,t)
=
\int\frac{d\omega}{2\pi}
e^{-i\omega t}y_\omega(x).
$$

The causal outgoing solution is:

$$
y_\omega(x)=X_\omega e^{i\omega x/c_A}.
$$

Hence:

$$
\partial_xy_\omega(0)
=
\frac{i\omega}{c_A}X_\omega.
$$

The normal momentum of the channel is:

$$
\Pi_A(\omega)
=
-\zeta_A\partial_xy_\omega(0).
$$

Thus, the retarded Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator is:

$$
\Lambda_A^{\rm ret}(\omega)
=
-i\omega\frac{\zeta_A}{c_A}.
$$

Defining:

$$
\gamma_A=\frac{\zeta_A}{c_A}>0,
$$

we have:

$$
\Lambda_A^{\rm ret}(\omega)
=
-i\gamma_A\omega.
$$

This is the geometric origin of ohmic friction. It was not inserted manually; it is the response of an open domain with a causal radiation condition.

## 3. Energy and causal mobility

The energy of the channel is:

$$
E_A
=
\frac{\zeta_A}{2}
\int_0^\infty dx
\left[
\frac1{c_A^2}(\partial_ty)^2
+(\partial_xy)^2
\right].
$$

For outgoing waves, the average power radiated through the interface is:

$$
\mathcal P_{\rm out}
=
\gamma_A\dot X^2\ge0.
$$

In the overdamped limit, the pointer mobility is:

$$
\mathcal M_X=\gamma_A^{-1}.
$$

## 4. Conditioned pointer

For two channels $\kappa=\pm1$, use the reduced potential:

$$
U_\kappa(X)
=
-\frac A2X^2+\frac B4X^4-g_X\kappa X.
$$

Near a stable record $X_\kappa^*$:

$$
U_\kappa'(X_\kappa^*)=0,
\qquad
k_\kappa=U_\kappa''(X_\kappa^*)>0.
$$

The linearized pointer equation is:

$$
M_X\ddot x+\gamma_A\dot x+k_\kappa x=\xi_A(t).
$$

At temperature $T_A$, the classical fluctuation-dissipation relation is:

$$
\langle\xi_A(t)\xi_A(t')\rangle
=
2\gamma_Ak_BT_A\,\delta(t-t').
$$

## 5. Normalized record and informational rate

After whitening the apparatus noise, the record satisfies:

$$
dY_t
=
2\sqrt{\Gamma(t)}\,\kappa\,dt+dW_t.
$$

Here $\Gamma(t)$ is the physical informational rate extracted from the spectral kernel of the apparatus. For the simple overdamped ohmic detector:

$$
\Gamma
=
\frac{g_X^2}{8\gamma_Ak_BT_A}.
$$

This formula shows the physical role of the parameters: greater coupling increases the information; greater friction or temperature reduces the discrimination rate.

## 6. Filtering and martingale

Define:

$$
p_t
=
\Pr(\kappa=+1\mid\mathcal F_t^Y),
$$

where $\mathcal F_t^Y$ is the filtration generated by the record up to $t$. The innovation is:

$$
d\widetilde W_t
=
dY_t
-2\sqrt{\Gamma(t)}(2p_t-1)dt.
$$

The filtering equation for two signals is:

$$
dp_t
=
4\sqrt{\Gamma(t)}\,p_t(1-p_t)d\widetilde W_t.
$$

Since there is no drift term:

$$
\mathbb E[p_t]=p_0.
$$

Therefore $p_t$ is a bounded martingale. If the accumulated information:

$$
\mathcal I(t)=\int_0^t\Gamma(s)ds
$$

diverges, the likelihood ratio separates the two channels and:

$$
p_t\to p_\infty\in\{0,1\}
\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

Since $\mathbb E[p_\infty]=p_0$, we obtain:

$$
\Pr(p_\infty=1)=p_0.
$$

Thus, the apparatus dynamics converts the initial Born weight into record frequencies without postulating collapse.

## 7. Status

The result is an effective conditional theorem:

$$
\boxed{
\text{QND channel}
+\Gamma(t)\ge0
+\mathcal I(\infty)=\infty
+\text{stable records}
\Longrightarrow
\text{asymptotic capture with frequency }p_0.
}
$$

The calculation of $\zeta_A$, $c_A$, $g_X$, and $T_A$ for a real apparatus belongs to the apparatus metrology. These data enter as background, material, and boundary; they are not modifications of the official action.
