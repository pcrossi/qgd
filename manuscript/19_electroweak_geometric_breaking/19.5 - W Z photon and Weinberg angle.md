# 19.5. W, Z, photon and Weinberg angle

In the effective laboratory readout, the derivative of the mode is written as:

$$
D_\mu\Phi
=
\left(
\partial_\mu
-igW_\mu^i\frac{\sigma_i}{2}
-ig'\frac12B_\mu
\right)\Phi.
$$

Here $g$ and $g'$ are effective names. In GDQ, they represent internal norms and stiffnesses:

$$
\frac1{g^2}
=
\mathcal N_W
\int_{\mathcal I}\|\xi_W\|^2d\mu_g,
$$

$$
\frac1{g'^2}
=
\mathcal N_Y
\int_{\mathcal I}\|\xi_Y\|^2d\mu_g.
$$

The charged masses are:

$$
\boxed{
m_W=\frac{gv}{2}.
}
$$

In the neutral sector, the matrix appears:

$$
\frac{v^2}{8}
\begin{pmatrix}
W_\mu^3 & B_\mu
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
g^2 & -gg'\\
-gg' & g'^2
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
W^{3\mu}\\
B^\mu
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The determinant is zero:

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
g^2 & -gg'\\
-gg' & g'^2
\end{pmatrix}
=0.
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
m_\gamma=0,
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
m_Z=\frac v2\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}.
}
$$

The effective Weinberg angle is:

$$
\boxed{
\tan\theta_W=\frac{g'}{g}.
}
$$

At the common geometric point, the geometric taxonomy and the electroweak breaking obtain:

$$
\sin^2\theta_W=\frac38.
$$

The broken local interface preserves this value:

$$

$$

The operational transport that approximates the observed electroweak scale is:

$$

\quad
\Longrightarrow
\quad
\sin^2\theta_W=\frac29.
}
$$

This last step is global/boundary transport. It must not be attributed to the homogeneous local stoma.

The reduced form needs the differential transport of the norms:

$$
\frac1{g_{\rm EW}^2}
=
Z_W\frac1{g_{\rm match}^2},
\qquad
\frac1{g_{\rm EW}'{}^2}
=
Z_Y\frac1{g_{\rm match}'{}^2}.
$$

Since the common point has $g'^2/g^2=3/5$, the operational value $\sin^2\theta_W=2/9$ is equivalent to:

$$
\frac{g_{\rm EW}'{}^2}{g_{\rm EW}^2}
=
\frac27,
$$

and, therefore:

$$
\frac35\frac{Z_W}{Z_Y}
=
\frac27
\quad
\Longrightarrow
\quad
\boxed{
\frac{Z_W}{Z_Y}=\frac{10}{21}.
}
$$

$$
