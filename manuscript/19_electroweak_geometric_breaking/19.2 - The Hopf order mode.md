# 19.2. The Hopf order mode

The geometric taxonomy provides the effective decomposition of the internal bundle:

$$
E_{\rm int}
=
E_C\oplus E_W\oplus L_Y.
$$

The electroweak mode must live in the weak sector and carry half hypercharge:

$$
\Phi_{\rm EW}
\in
\Gamma(E_W\otimes L_Y^{1/2}).
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
\Phi_{\rm EW}\sim(1,2)_{1/2}.
}
$$

In the local link $S^3\subset\mathbb C^2$, we write:

$$
u
=
\begin{pmatrix}
z_1\\
z_2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
u^\dagger u=1.
$$

The reduced representative of the mode is:

$$
\boxed{
\Phi_{\rm EW}
=
\frac{\rho}{\sqrt2}u.
}
$$

As a fundamental harmonic on $S^3$:

$$
-\Delta_{S^3}u
=
\frac3{R^2}u.
$$

Choosing the orientation:

$$
u_0
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\
1
\end{pmatrix},
$$

the preserved generator is:

$$
\boxed{
Q=T_3+Y.
}
$$

Physically, this means that the new geometric basin breaks the charged weak direction, but leaves a massless electromagnetic circulation.
