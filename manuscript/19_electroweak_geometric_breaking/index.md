# 19. Electroweak Geometric Breaking

This chapter organizes the response of GDQ to electroweak breaking.

The thesis is simple:

$$
\boxed{
\text{the effective Higgs is a geometric mode of the GDQ Hessian.}
}
$$

This does not mean introducing a new fundamental Higgs field, nor rewriting GDQ as the Standard Model. The correct chain is:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_\ast
\to
P_{\rm phys}\delta^2\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}[\Phi_\ast]P_{\rm phys}
\to
\Phi_{\rm EW}
\to
V_{\rm eff}
\to
W,Z,\gamma
\text{ effective}.
$$

The usual letters $g$, $g'$, $\theta_W$, $m_W$, $m_Z$, $G_F$, and $Y_f$ will be used only as operational readout names for norms, stiffnesses, eigenvalues, and overlaps in the laboratory.
