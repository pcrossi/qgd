# Yukawas as geometric overlaps

In the effective language:

$$
m_f=\frac{y_fv}{\sqrt2}.
$$

In GDQ, $y_f$ is not a free fundamental parameter. It is the abbreviation of an overlap integral:

$$
Y_{ij}^{\rm geom}
=
\mathcal N_Y
\int_{\mathcal I}
\langle
\psi_{L,i},
\Phi_{\rm EW}\psi_{R,j}
\rangle
d\mu_g.
$$

The ingredients are:

1. $\psi_{L,i}$ and $\psi_{R,j}$, modes of the effective Dirac-Bismut operator;
2. $\Phi_{\rm EW}$, the geometric Hopf mode;
3. $d\mu_g$, internal measure induced by the background;
4. $\mathcal N_Y$, kinetic normalization of the channel.

Thus:

$$
M_{ij}
=
M_{ij}^{(0)}
+
\frac{v}{\sqrt2}Y_{ij}^{\rm geom}.
$$

The physical meaning is that electroweak breaking does not replace the primary geometric spectrum of masses; it provides mixing and effective response.
