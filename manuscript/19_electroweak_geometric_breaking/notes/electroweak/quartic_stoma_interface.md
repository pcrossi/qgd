# Positive interface quartic

Consider the interface as a radial graph:

$$
r(Y)=R(1+\varepsilon Y),
$$

with:

$$
\langle Y^2\rangle=\frac14,
\qquad
\langle Y^4\rangle=\frac18.
$$

The relative area is:

$$
\frac{A_\varepsilon}{A_0}
=
1+\frac{9}{8}\varepsilon^2-\frac{5}{64}\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6).
$$

The relative volume is:

$$
\frac{V_\varepsilon}{V_0}
=
1+\frac{3}{2}\varepsilon^2+\frac{1}{8}\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6).
$$

Imposing fixed volume, the common scale corrects the area by $(V_\varepsilon/V_0)^{-3/4}$. Thus:

$$
\left.
\frac{A_\varepsilon}{A_0}
\right|_{V=V_0}
=
1+\frac{5}{128}\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6).
$$

The quadratic term cancels. The first physical penalty of the interface is quartic and positive.

The torsional surface stiffness used is:

$$
S_\partial
=
\alpha
\left(
\frac{3\pi}{2}
+
\frac{3}{4\pi^3}
\right).
$$

With:

$$
\varepsilon=\frac{\beta}{b_0},
\qquad
b_0=\frac1{\pi R^3},
$$

it follows:

$$
a_4^\partial
=
\frac{5S_\partial}{32b_0^4}.
$$

For:

$$
R=1{,}998411184770,
\qquad
b_0=0{,}03988371206,
$$

we obtain:

$$
a_4^\partial=2134{,}360262.
$$

Adding:

$$
a_4^{\rm bulk}=-0{,}805755288,
$$

we have:

$$
a_4^{\rm total}=2133{,}554507>0.
$$
