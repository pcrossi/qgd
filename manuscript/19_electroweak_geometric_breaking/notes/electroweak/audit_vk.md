# Audit of the auxiliary scale $v_K$

The auxiliary scale:

$$
v_K
=
\frac{M_e}{\alpha}
\left(
1-\frac{3}{4\pi^2}
\right)^{-1/2}.
$$

With $M_e=0{,}51099895\,{\rm MeV}$ and $\alpha^{-1}=137{,}035999$:

$$
v_K\simeq72{,}85\,{\rm MeV}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
v_K\neq246\,{\rm GeV}.
}
$$

This formula may remain as a low-energy auxiliary scale, but it should not be used as the electroweak expectation value.

The electroweak scale in the chapter is not defined by $v_K$. The dimensionless order mode is defined by the variational basin:

$$
\mathcal V(\beta)
=
\frac12a_2\beta^2
+
\frac14a_4\beta^4.
$$

The non-trivial stationary point satisfies:

$$
a_2\beta_\ast
+
a_4\beta_\ast^3
=
0,
$$

therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\beta_\ast^2
=
-\frac{a_2}{a_4}.
}
$$

The physical canonical variable is:

$$
\Phi_c
=
\sqrt{Z_\beta}\,\beta,
$$

and:

$$
v
=
\sqrt{Z_\beta}\,\beta_\ast.
$$

In the operational writing as an effective doublet, the factor $1/\sqrt2$ can shift the expression to the conventional form:

$$
v^2
=
-\frac{2a_2}{a_4},
$$

but the quantity directly calculated by the preserved dimensionless coefficients is $\beta_\ast^2=-a_2/a_4$.

The reduced geometric scale adopted for the global normalization is:

$$
v
=
m_p\frac{6\pi^5}{7}.
$$
