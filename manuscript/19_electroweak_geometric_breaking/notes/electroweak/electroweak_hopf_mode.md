# Electroweak Hopf mode

On the link $S^3\subset\mathbb C^2$, use:

$$
u
=
\begin{pmatrix}
z_1\\
z_2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
u^\dagger u=1.
$$

Under $SU(2)_L$, $u$ is a fundamental doublet. Through the Hopf fiber, it receives effective hypercharge:

$$
Y=\frac12.
$$

Thus:

$$
u\sim(1,2)_{1/2}.
$$

The GDQ mode is:

$$
\Phi_{\rm EW}
=
\frac{\rho}{\sqrt2}u.
$$

On the round $S^3$ of radius $R$:

$$
-\Delta_{S^3}z_a
=
\frac3{R^2}z_a.
$$

Thus:

$$
-\Delta_{S^3}u
=
\frac3{R^2}u.
$$

The choice:

$$
u_0
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

satisfies:

$$
T_3u_0=-\frac12u_0,
\qquad
Yu_0=\frac12u_0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
(T_3+Y)u_0=0.
$$

This is the geometric reason why $Q=T_3+Y$ remains unbroken.
