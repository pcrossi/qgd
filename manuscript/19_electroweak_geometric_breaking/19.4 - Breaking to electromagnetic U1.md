# 19.4. Breaking to electromagnetic U(1)

With the choice:

$$
\langle\Phi\rangle
=
\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\
v
\end{pmatrix},
$$

the preserved direction is:

$$
Q=T_3+Y.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y
\longrightarrow
U(1)_{\rm EM}.
}
$$

This expression uses the usual effective language, but the GDQ interpretation is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{the electromagnetic generator is the circulation that remains compatible with the new interface.}
}
$$

The associated conservation is a flux conservation. It is not necessary to separately postulate that the photon must remain massless. It is the kernel mode of the neutral matrix after breaking.
