# 20.5. Linear dilution, 28 channels and alpha2 projection

The dark energy formula has three factors that need to be understood:

1. the linear dilution $r_p/R_H$;
2. the $28$ Cartan channels;
3. the $\alpha^2$ projection.

## 1. Linear dilution

The asymptotic profile used in global transport is:

$$
f(r)
\sim
\ln\left(\frac{r}{r_p}\right).
$$

Therefore, the Perelman weight satisfies:

$$
e^{-f(r)}
=
\frac{r_p}{r}.
$$

Integrating radially:

$$
\int_{r_p}^{R_H}
e^{-f(r)}r^2\,dr
=
\int_{r_p}^{R_H}
\frac{r_p}{r}r^2\,dr
=
\frac{r_p}{2}
\left(R_H^2-r_p^2\right).
$$

Dividing by the cosmological volume leaves, in the limit $R_H\gg r_p$:

$$
\rho_{\rm diluted}
\propto
\frac{r_p}{R_H}.
$$

Therefore, the dilution is not flat volumetric $1/R_H^3$. It is a dilution weighted by the dilaton.

## 2. Twenty-eight channels

In the effective real space of dimension eight, the antisymmetric torsion channels belong to:

$$
\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^8).
$$

Its dimension is:

$$
\dim\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^8)
=
\binom{8}{2}
=
28.
$$

Physically, these are the independent shear/torsion channels through which the vacuum tension can be distributed.

## 3. The $\alpha^2$ projection

The density before the projection belongs to the complete complex geometric channel. Macroscopic gravity reads a normalized real projection.

If the projected amplitude of the channel is normalized by $\alpha$, the measured density, being quadratic in the amplitude, receives:

$$
\rho_{\rm grav}
=
\alpha^2\rho_{\rm eff}.
$$

This $\alpha$ is the geometric normalization of the primitive channel inherited from the global-local bridge. It is not an import of QED as a fundamental ontology.
