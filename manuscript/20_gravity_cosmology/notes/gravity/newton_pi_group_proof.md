# Newton's dimensionless group

The objective of this note is to separate physical dimension from geometric content.

Newton's constant has the dimension:

$$
[G]
=
L^3M^{-1}T^{-2}.
$$

The product $\hbar c$ has the dimension:

$$
[\hbar c]
=
ML^3T^{-2}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\left[
\frac{G M_p^2}{\hbar c}
\right]
=
\frac{
(L^3M^{-1}T^{-2})M^2
}{
ML^3T^{-2}
}
=
1.
$$

Thus:

$$
\Pi_G
=
\frac{G M_p^2}{\hbar c}
$$

is the dimensionless geometric number associated with the gravitational coupling at the baryonic scale.

Inverting it:

$$
G
=
\frac{\hbar c}{M_p^2}\Pi_G.
$$

This equation is not a fit. It is the correct dimensional way to reconstruct $G$ once $\Pi_G$ has been determined by a global response.

$$

=
\frac{2GE_H}{c^4}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
G
=
\frac{c^4R_H}{2E_H}.
$$
