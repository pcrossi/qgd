# Newton's reduced calculation thermal-axial chain

Newton's reduced calculation preserves a conditional route for the exponential factor appearing in the reduced formula for $\Pi_G$.

## 1. Horizon regularity

Euclidean regularity imposes:

$$
\beta_E
=
2\pi R_H.
$$

The first relative thermal winding in eight real dimensions has the core:

$$
I_1(\tau)
\propto
\tau^{-4}
e^{-\beta_E^2/(4\tau)}.
$$

The saddle satisfies:

$$
\frac{d}{d\tau}
\left[
-4\ln\tau
-\frac{\beta_E^2}{4\tau}
\right]
=
0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
-\frac{4}{\tau}
+\frac{\beta_E^2}{4\tau^2}
=
0,
$$

and:

$$
\tau_\ast
=
\frac{\beta_E^2}{16}.
$$

## 2. Axial bundle

For the fundamental axial cycle:

$$
f_v=1.
$$

For the first odd mode of the axial bundle:

$$
\lambda_{\rm ax}
=
\frac{2}{R^2}.
$$

The relative action is:

$$
\Delta u_v
=
\tau\pi^2\lambda_{\rm ax}.
$$

At the saddle:

$$
\Delta u_v
=
\frac{\beta_E^2}{16}
\pi^2
\frac{2}{R^2}
=
\frac{\pi^4}{2}\frac{R_H^2}{R^2}.
$$

## 3. Gluing condition

If global gluing imposes:

$$
R
=
\pi^2\sqrt{\alpha}\,R_H,
$$

then:

$$
\Delta u_v
=
\frac{1}{2\alpha}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\frac{\mathcal U_\ast}{\mathcal U_0}
=
e^{-1/(2\alpha)}.
$$

## Status

The saddle calculation is direct. The condition $R=\pi^2\sqrt{\alpha} R_H$ is a global gluing condition. The chapter does not treat it as a derivation from the isolated local bulk.
