# 20.4. Vacuum energy and protonic UV tension

Vacuum energy in GDQ is not treated as an unrestricted flat sum of zero-point modes. It is a geometric tension stabilized by matter.

The proton is the first stable baryonic soliton of the project. Therefore, it provides the materialized UV scale:

$$
\rho_{\rm UV}^{p}
=
\frac{M_pc^2}{V_p},
\qquad
V_p
=
\frac{4\pi}{3}r_p^3.
$$

With the current proton radius,

$$
r_p
=
0.840778765450\,{\rm fm},
$$

we obtain:

$$
\rho_{\rm UV}^{p}
\simeq
6.04\times10^{34}\,{\rm J/m^3}.
$$

## 1. Why the proton?

The proton is used because it is the smallest stable baryonic configuration. It represents the tension that the vacuum supports when geometry organizes itself into persistent matter.

This does not mean that the universe is made only of protons. It means that the proton provides a stable material scale for the UV density of the geometric medium.
