# 20. Gravity, vacuum and cosmological boundary

This chapter organizes the gravitational and cosmological parts of GDQ.

The central idea is to separate three levels that cannot be mixed:

1. the official local bulk;
2. the Einstein cosmological space;
3. the macroscopic projection that the observer reads as gravitation.

The official local bulk remains

$$
M_{\rm loc}
=
\mathbb R^4\times T^4.
$$

The cosmological/spectral space used for global normalizations is

$$
M_E
=
T^5\times S^3.
$$

The value of $G$, the dark energy density and the galactic acceleration scale are not extracted from an infinitesimal local fiber. They belong to the global boundary value problem. The local geometry must transport this data and be compatible with it, but it does not replace the cosmological boundary.

## Chapter Chain

The chapter uses the chain:

$$
\mathcal S_{\rm GDQ}
\to
\Phi_\ast^{\rm cos}
\to
K_{\rm cos}^{\rm phys}
\to
\text{global boundary}
\to
G,\rho_\Lambda,a_0.
$$

When the complete cosmological Hessian has not been diagonalized, the result is classified as structural or conditional. When a reduced formula has already been derived, it is evaluated numerically and compared with accepted values.
