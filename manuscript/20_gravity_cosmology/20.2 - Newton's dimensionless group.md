# 20.2. Newton's dimensionless group

Every dimensional constant must be read as a ratio after the scale of units is chosen. For gravitation, the relevant quantity is the dimensionless group:

$$
\Pi_G
=
\frac{G M_p^2}{\hbar c}.
$$

Here $M_p$ is the proton mass used as a stable baryonic scale. Newton's constant is then reconstructed by:

$$
G
=
\frac{\hbar c}{M_p^2}\Pi_G.
$$

This writing separates what is metrology from what is geometry. The dimensionless number $\Pi_G$ is the geometric response. The conversion to SI units uses the conventional units of mass, length, and time.
