# Noether and the constants of classical motion

## 1. Why conservations survive the limit

The semiclassical limit eliminates a small dynamical correction; it does not eliminate symmetries. When the reduction and the boundary preserve a symmetry of the action, the corresponding Noether current passes to the limit, provided there is no anomaly in the sector or unaccounted external flux.

## 2. Temporal homogeneity

For a Hamiltonian with no explicit time dependence, $\partial H/\partial t=0$. Along a characteristic,

$$
\frac{dH}{dt}
=\frac{\partial H}{\partial x^i}\dot x^i
+\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i}\dot p_i
+\frac{\partial H}{\partial t}.
$$

Using Hamilton's equations,

$$
\frac{dH}{dt}
=\frac{\partial H}{\partial x^i}
\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i}
-\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i}
\frac{\partial H}{\partial x^i}=0.
$$

Thus, temporal homogeneity yields conservation of energy.

## 3. Spatial homogeneity

If $H$ does not depend on a coordinate $x^a$, then

$$
\dot p_a=-\frac{\partial H}{\partial x^a}=0.
$$

Translation in that direction conserves the conjugate momentum.

## 4. Isotropy

For a central potential, define $\mathbf L=\mathbf x\times\mathbf p$. Then

$$
\frac{d\mathbf L}{dt}
=\dot{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf p
+\mathbf x\times\dot{\mathbf p}=0,
$$

since $\dot{\mathbf x}\parallel\mathbf p$ and $\dot{\mathbf p}=-\nabla V$ is radial. The conservation of angular momentum is the classical consequence of isotropy.

## 5. Global phase and transported charge

The symmetry $S_R\mapsto S_R+S_0$ produces the phase current of Chapter 5. In the Madelung sector, its reduction is the continuity. In the classical limit, the integrated charge remains conserved when the lateral flux vanishes.

This Noether charge must not be automatically identified with all topological or electrical charge. Each identification requires its sectoral mapping and normalization.

## 6. Methodological consequence

If a classical approximation appears to violate energy, momentum, or probability, one must look for boundary flux, external time dependence, eliminated degrees of freedom, symmetry breaking by the apparatus, or a reduction that did not preserve the action.
