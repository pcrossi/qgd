# 21.7. Monopoles, vorticity and boundaries

In GDQ, the local magnetic field is read as vorticity/shear of the flow:

$$
\mathbf B
\propto
\nabla\times\mathbf v.
$$

In a regular domain:

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B
\propto
\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf v)
=
0.
$$

Therefore, a local point magnetic monopole does not appear as a regular elementary source.

> ![[notes/topology/monopoles_vorticity_boundary]]

## 1. What This Excludes

This excludes the ontology of an isolated radial point magnetic charge as a fundamental local source of magnetism.

Physically, magnetism requires an axis, return, and circulation. A vortex is not a radial emission without return.

## 2. What This Does Not Exclude

This does not exclude global bundle topology. In regions with multiple charts, boundaries, solenoids, defects, or gluing, there may appear:

1. Chern classes;
2. potential transitions;
3. quantized fluxes;
4. holonomies;
5. monopole-like effective configurations.

The difference is ontological:

$$
\boxed{
\text{GDQ rejects the local point monopole as a fundamental particle, not the global flow topology.}
}
$$
