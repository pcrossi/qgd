# CP periodicity by integer topological charge

The strong CP sector depends on a topological phase. In the effective color sector induced by GDQ, the density is written as:

$$
q_C
=
\frac{1}{8\pi^2}
\operatorname{Tr}(F_C\wedge F_C).
$$

The total charge is:

$$
Q_C
=
\int_N q_C.
$$

For admissible bundles with compatible decay or boundary gluing, this integral is an integer:

$$
Q_C\in\mathbb Z.
$$

The angular term enters the effective Euclidean phase as:

$$
\exp(i\theta Q_C).
$$

If we shift:

$$
\theta
\mapsto
\theta+2\pi,
$$

then:

$$
\exp(i(\theta+2\pi)Q_C)
=
\exp(i\theta Q_C)
\exp(2\pi i Q_C).
$$

Since $Q_C$ is an integer:

$$
\exp(2\pi i Q_C)=1.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\exp(i(\theta+2\pi)Q_C)
=
\exp(i\theta Q_C).
$$

Thus:

$$
\theta\sim\theta+2\pi.
$$

This is the reason why the global potential cannot be an isolated parabola. The energy must be a periodic function of the angle. The minimal form compatible with stability in the CP sector is:

$$
V_{\rm CP}(\theta)
=
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}
(1-\cos\theta).
$$

Its local expansion near the minimum is:

$$
V_{\rm CP}(\theta)
=
\frac12
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}\theta^2
+O(\theta^4).
$$

Therefore, the quadratic potential is only the local approximation of the periodic potential, not the global form of the CP sector.
