# Monopoles, vorticity and boundary

In the regular sector, magnetism is vorticity:

$$
\mathbf B
=
\lambda_B
\nabla\times\mathbf v.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B
=
\lambda_B
\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf v)
=
0.
$$

This excludes a regular point magnetic source in the local domain.

In form language, if $A$ is a local potential:

$$
F=dA.
$$

Then:

$$
dF=d^2A=0.
$$

A global magnetic charge only appears if $A$ cannot be defined on a single global chart, requiring chart transitions and topological classes.

Thus, GDQ distinguishes:

1. local point monopole as a fundamental particle: excluded in the regular sector;
2. global bundle topology: allowed as a boundary configuration.
