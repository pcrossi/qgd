# Torsional normalization $f_B$

The torsional angular mode is:

$$
\vartheta_B\sim\vartheta_B+2\pi.
$$

The canonical normalization is defined by:

$$
a=f_B\vartheta_B.
$$

If the second variation of the official action in the torsional sector produces:

$$
\delta^2\mathcal S_{\rm tor}
=
\frac12
\int
f_B^2
(\partial\vartheta_B)^2
d{\rm vol},
$$

then $f_B$ is the kinetic rigidity of the mode.

The proposed geometric value uses the Kähler volume:

$$
V_K=6\pi^5,
$$

and:

$$
f_B
=
M_{\rm Pl}^{\rm red}
\sqrt{
\frac3{\sqrt{V_K}}
}.
$$

With $M_{\rm Pl}^{\rm red}=2.435\times10^{18}\,{\rm GeV}$:

$$
f_B
\simeq
6.44\times10^{17}\,{\rm GeV}.
$$

The correct status is conditional: the formula is geometrically motivated, but the final metrological value requires extracting this coefficient from the physical Hessian of the torsional background.
