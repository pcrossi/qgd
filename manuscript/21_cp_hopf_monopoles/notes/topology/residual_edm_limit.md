# Residual EDM and experimental limit

For small angles:

$$
d_n
\simeq
C_n\theta\,e\,{\rm cm}.
$$

Using:

$$
C_n
=
3.8\times10^{-16},
$$

and the experimental limit:

$$
|d_n|
<
1.8\times10^{-26}\,e\,{\rm cm},
$$

we have:

$$
|\theta|
<
\frac{1.8\times10^{-26}}{3.8\times10^{-16}}
=
4.7368\times10^{-11}.
$$

The reduced torsional flow has the implicit solution:

$$
\tan\frac{\theta(\tau)}2
=
\tan\frac{\theta_0}2
\exp(-\kappa\chi\tau).
$$

Therefore, for any $\theta_0\ne\pi\pmod{2\pi}$, there is a finite flow time to cross the experimental EDM limit.
