# 21. Strong CP, Monopoles and Hopf

This chapter brings together three themes that seem separate but use the same geometric structure:

1. the relaxation of the strong CP angle;
2. the absence of an isolated local magnetic monopole;
3. the Hopf fibration as a geometry of circulation and half-monodromy.

The thesis of the chapter is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{topological angles, magnetism and spin are readouts of circulation on boundaries.}
}
$$

In GDQ, this does not require introducing a new fundamental particle of the axion type, nor turning monopoles into point magnetic sources. What appears is a torsional angular mode, a periodic potential of topological origin, and a fiber bundle geometry that quantizes circulation.

$$

$$
\text{stoma}
\to

\text{half-monodromy}.
$$

The monopole sector will be treated by the chain:

$$
\mathbf B
\sim
\nabla\times\mathbf v
\to
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0
\to
\text{no local point magnetic source}.
$$
