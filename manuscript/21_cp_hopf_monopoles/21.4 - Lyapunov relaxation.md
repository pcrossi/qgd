# 21.4. Lyapunov relaxation

Define:

$$
V(\theta)
=
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}
(1-\cos\theta),
\qquad
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}>0.
$$

The reduced dissipative dynamics is:

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\tau}
=
-\kappa_{\rm CP}
\frac{\partial V}{\partial\theta},
\qquad
\kappa_{\rm CP}>0.
$$

Since:

$$
\frac{\partial V}{\partial\theta}
=
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}\sin\theta,
$$

it follows:

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\tau}
=
-\kappa_{\rm CP}
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}
\sin\theta.
$$

Along the flow:

$$
\frac{dV}{d\tau}
=
\frac{\partial V}{\partial\theta}
\frac{d\theta}{d\tau}
=
-\kappa_{\rm CP}
\left(
\frac{\partial V}{\partial\theta}
\right)^2
\le0.
$$

Therefore $V$ is a Lyapunov function.

## 1. Critical Points

The critical points satisfy:

$$
\sin\theta=0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\theta=n\pi.
$$

The second derivative is:

$$
\frac{d^2V}{d\theta^2}
=
\chi_{\rm top}^{\rm GDQ}\cos\theta.
$$

Thus:

$$
\theta=0\pmod{2\pi}
$$

is a stable minimum, while:

$$
\theta=\pi\pmod{2\pi}
$$

is an unstable maximum.

## 2. Result

For initial data outside the unstable maximum:

$$
\theta_{\rm eff}(\tau)
\to
0\pmod{2\pi}.
$$

This is the structural closure of strong CP in the torsional sector of GDQ.
