# Terminological agreement

An extensive theory can become artificially difficult when every concept is accompanied, at all times, by the names of all authors and formalisms that participated in its formation. Expressions such as "Madelung representation," "Perelman flow," "Bismut connection," "Bohm potential," and "Sudarshan prescription" are historically useful, but their continuous repetition would fragment the reading and could suggest that GDQ is merely the juxtaposition of these works.

In this manuscript, each historical origin will be presented when the concept is introduced and discussed in the corresponding references. After that, we will adopt a stable internal vocabulary. When we employ a term without additional qualification, it will have the meaning defined in this section, unless the text explicitly declares another convention.

## The theory

**Quantum Geometrodynamics**, abbreviated as **GDQ**, designates the construction developed in this manuscript from a dynamic complex geometry, a complex field, and a weighted measure. The term will not be used as a generic name for any attempt to quantize geometry.

**Official action** designates exclusively the fundamental functional of GDQ. Reduced actions, auxiliary geometric functionals, effective energies, and probe functionals will not be called the official action.

## The geometric space

**Geometry**, **manifold**, or **bulk** designate the Hermitian complex domain in which the official action is defined. In the main local sector, this domain has complex dimension four and a real structure associated with $\mathbb R^4\times T^4$.

**Mesh** or **geometric fabric** are intuitive expressions for this same dynamic structure. They do not mean a material discrete lattice, except when a numerical model is explicitly introduced.

**Vacuum** designates an admissible geometric background of GDQ. It does not mean "absolute absence of structure," nor should it be automatically identified with the perturbative vacuum of another theory.

**Physical space-time** designates the Lorentzian geometry reconstructed or projected from the bulk. Bulk and physical space-time will not be treated as synonyms.

**Cosmological geometry** designates a global background used for cosmological conditions or spectral calculations. In particular, $T^5\times S^3$ will not be silently identified with the local bulk $\mathbb R^4\times T^4$.

## The Hermitian structure and torsion

**Hermitian structure** means the pair formed by the metric and the compatible complex structure. The term **Kähler** will be reserved for the case in which the fundamental form is closed. We will not call a geometry with non-zero torsion strictly Kähler.

**Torsional connection** designates the Hermitian connection with totally antisymmetric torsion used by GDQ, historically associated with the Bismut connection.

**Torsion**, represented by $H$ or $B$ depending on the context, designates the real 3-form of this connection. When a spatial projection, a 2-form, or its vector dual are used, the projection will be declared; these objects will not be confused with the original 3-form.

## The field, density and phase

**Complex field** designates the fundamental field $f$ and its conjugate $\bar f$. Its decomposition defines two derived physical variables:

$$
\rho=e^{-(f+\bar f)/2}
$$

and

$$
S_R=\frac{\hbar}{2i}(f-\bar f).
$$

**Density** means $\rho$, unless otherwise indicated. It is positive in the regular domain and participates in the geometric measure. A charge, energy, or spin density will always receive an explicit qualifier.

**Phase** means $S_R$. In simply connected regions without defects, it can be represented locally by a regular function; around zeros of the density, its connection and its holonomy can possess topological content.

**Official measure** means

$$
\mathcal U=\frac{\rho}{(4\pi z_\tau)^n},
$$

along with the volume element indicated in the action. The measure will not be replaced by an arbitrary probability distribution.

## The fluid and the flow

**Fluid** or **hydrodynamic representation** designate the writing of the density and phase as transport fields, historically associated with the Madelung transformation. The term does not postulate a microscopic classical liquid, ordinary viscosity, or universal incompressibility.

**Current velocity** designates the field derived from the gradient of the phase in the regime where this reduction is valid. **Osmotic velocity** designates the field derived from the gradient of the density. Both are derived variables, not new fundamental fields.

**Flow** or **geometric runoff** designate the evolution of the geometry and the measure with respect to the parameter $\tau$, related to the generalized Ricci–Perelman flow in the appropriate sector. The term does not automatically designate a solution of the Navier–Stokes equations.

**Flow parameter**, written as $\tau$, is the real parameter of scale and diffusion that orders the geometric evolution. It has the dimension of area and does not constitute a field, a second physical temporal coordinate, or a fifth dimension. Its relation to physical time occurs through the dimensionally homogeneous complex causal variable $z_\tau=\tau+i\nu_0t$.

**Physical time**, written as $t$, is the temporal parameter of the reconstructed dynamics and experiments. It must not be confused with $\tau$.

**Complex causal variable**, written as $z_\tau$, participates in the measure and the causal prescription. **Causal contour**, written as $\gamma$, designates the integration path that selects the physical composition of the causal branches. Its choice is part of the problem, not a notational decoration.

## Geometric pressure and potential

**Geometric pressure**, **counter-pressure**, or **geometric potential** designate the term derived from the density which, in the hydrodynamic reduction, assumes the form of the quantum potential historically associated with Bohm. When necessary, it will be written as:

$$
V_{\mathrm{geom}}
=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\Delta\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho},
$$

with the metric and torsional corrections corresponding to the considered background. The term does not represent an external force added to the theory.

## Matter, solitons and stomata

**Matter** designates localized and stable or metastable configurations of the fields and geometry of GDQ.

**Soliton** designates one of these coherent configurations when its stability results from the balance between flow, curvature, density, phase, and boundary conditions.

**Stoma** (plural **stomata**) designates the defect or density opening associated with the topological core of a configuration. Stoma and soliton are not synonyms: the former is an internal structure; the latter is the complete physical configuration.

**Particle** may be used as a phenomenological term for a soliton viewed at the experimental scale. Its use does not imply that matter is point-like.

## Charge, circulation and spin

**Charge** designates the conserved flux associated with the corresponding symmetry, normalized by the declared topological condition. An internal decomposition of fluxes will not be automatically interpreted as the existence of fractional point charges.

**Circulation** designates the integral of a current or connection along a closed cycle. The cycle, its orientation, and the normalization must always be identified.

**Spin** designates the sector of circulation and holonomy that realizes the double covering of physical orientations. When we say "spin orientation," we refer to the corresponding point in the orientation module, and not to the rotation of a rigid sphere.

Chern number, phase holonomy, circulation, and magnetic moment are related quantities, but they will not be identified without the geometric map that establishes their relation.

## Sources, constraints and boundary conditions

**External source** designates a datum controlled by the experimental setup, such as an applied field. The source is not varied with the internal fields of the object.

**Boundary condition** designates the datum imposed on the physical or asymptotic boundary of the domain. Dirichlet, Neumann, and Robin conditions will always be accompanied by the domain to which they belong.

**Constraint** designates a restriction on the configuration space, such as fixing a topological flux.

**Multiplier** designates the conjugate variable introduced to impose a constraint in the variational principle. It is determined by the solution and must not be confused with a free parameter adjusted to the data.

## Quantum and classical regimes

**Quantum** designates the regime in which phase, interference, holonomy, and coherent internal modes remain physically resolvable.

**Classical** designates the effective regime in which only robust collective variables are tracked and microscopic phases are not resolved. The two regimes do not correspond to different fundamental substances.

**Measurement** designates an interaction that correlates a sector of the object with a distinguishable, amplified, and stable macroscopic register. Not every interaction is a measurement.

## Reading rule

The definitions in this agreement remain valid throughout the manuscript. When a chapter uses a term in a historical, comparative, or more restricted sense, the change will be explicitly indicated. This convention allows the text to speak of geometry, flow, density, phase, torsion, and matter without repeating the entire genealogy of its concepts at every occurrence.
