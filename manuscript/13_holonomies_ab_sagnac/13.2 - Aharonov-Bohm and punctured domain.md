# 13.2. Aharonov-Bohm and punctured domain

In the ideal Aharonov--Bohm effect, the charged packet travels along the exterior of a solenoid. The exterior domain is:

$$
M_{\rm ext}
=
\mathbb R^3\setminus\mathcal S.
$$

Outside the ideal solenoid:

$$
B=0,
\qquad
F=dA=0.
$$

Even so:

$$
\pi_1(M_{\rm ext})\simeq\mathbb Z.
$$

Thus, $A$ can be locally pure gauge and globally non-exact:

$$
dA=0,
\qquad
A\ne d\chi
\quad
\text{globally}.
$$

The observable is the holonomy:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma(A)
=
\exp\left[
\frac{iq}{\hbar c}
\oint_\gamma A
\right].
$$

If $\gamma$ encloses the solenoid:

$$
\oint_\gamma A=\Phi.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\Delta\varphi_{\rm AB}
=
\frac{q\Phi}{\hbar c}.
$$

The exterior is locally field-free, but globally non-trivial.

This sentence is the essential part. The effect does not require a magnetic force in the packet path. It only requires that the connection cannot be trivialized by a single global gauge function in the punctured domain.

In the ideal cylindrical model, the harmonic class can be represented by:

$$
A_{\rm harm}
=
\frac{\Phi}{2\pi}\,d\theta.
$$

In standard vector coordinates:

$$
\boldsymbol A_{\rm harm}
=
\frac{\Phi}{2\pi r}\,\boldsymbol e_\theta.
$$

In the exterior of the solenoid:

$$
dA_{\rm harm}=0.
$$

But, for a loop $\gamma$ that circles the axis once:

$$
\oint_\gamma A_{\rm harm}
=
\int_0^{2\pi}
\frac{\Phi}{2\pi}\,d\theta
=
\Phi.
$$

Therefore, the relative phase between two paths with different linking numbers with the solenoid is:

$$
\Delta\varphi_{\rm AB}
=
\frac{q}{\hbar c}
\oint_\gamma A_{\rm harm}
=
\frac{q\Phi}{\hbar c}.
$$

For the electron, $q=-e$; the observed sign depends on the orientation chosen for the circuit and for the flux.

In standard SI units, the periodicity is written using the flux quantum:

$$
\Phi_0
=
\frac{h}{e}
=
4.135667696924\times10^{-15}\,{\rm Wb}.
$$

So, in modulus:

$$
|\Delta\varphi_{\rm AB}|
=
2\pi\frac{\Phi}{\Phi_0}.
$$

Some direct values are:

| $\Phi/\Phi_0$ | $|\Delta\varphi_{\rm AB}|$ | $\operatorname{Hol}$ |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $0$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| $1/4$ | $\pi/2$ | $i$ |
| $1/2$ | $\pi$ | $-1$ |
| $1$ | $2\pi$ | $1$ |

These values are not a fit: they follow from the exact definition of $\Phi_0=h/e$ and the holonomy of the punctured domain.

The script [[scripts/ab_symbolic_holonomy.py|ab_symbolic_holonomy.py]] symbolically verifies the representative $A_{\rm harm}=(\Phi/2\pi)d\theta$, showing that $dA_{\rm harm}=0$ on the exterior, but $\oint_\gamma A_{\rm harm}=\Phi$ on the punctured loop. The script [[scripts/ab_ideal_phase.py|ab_ideal_phase.py]] recalculates these numbers self-contained and preserves the output in [[scripts/output_ab_ideal_phase.md|output_ab_ideal_phase.md]].
