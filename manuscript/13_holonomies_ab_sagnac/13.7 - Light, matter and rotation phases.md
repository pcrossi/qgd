# 13.7. Light, matter and rotation phases

For light, the phase is:

$$
\Delta\varphi_\gamma
=
\omega\Delta t.
$$

With:

$$
\omega=\frac{2\pi c}{\lambda},
$$

and:

$$
\Delta t_{\rm Sag}
=
\frac{4\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf A}{c^2},
$$

we obtain:

$$
\Delta\varphi_{\rm Sag}^{\gamma}
=
\frac{8\pi}{\lambda c}
\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf A.
$$

For non-relativistic matter, the effective rotation phase is:

$$
\Delta\varphi_{\rm Sag}^{m}
=
\frac{4m}{\hbar}
\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf A.
$$

These formulas do not come from a local centrifugal force on the beam. They come from the clock holonomy on the rotating boundary.
