# 13.9. Metrological limits and real apparatus

The chapter closes the ideal holonomy effects. What remains for metrology:

1. real solenoids with leakage, surface and shielding;
2. real optical fibers in Sagnac, dispersion and losses;
3. physical clocks and synchronization in rotating apparatus;
4. matter interferometers with gravity, vibration and noise;
5. calculation of $\mathsf R_{\rm app}$ via Hessian/Schur for concrete apparatuses.

These dependencies belong to the apparatus. They do not change the official action nor reopen ideal AB or Sagnac.

The conceptual result is:

$$
\text{no local force}
\quad
\nRightarrow
\quad
\text{no physical effect.}
$$

If the connection has global holonomy, the phase or the clock can change.
