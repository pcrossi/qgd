# Audit of pending Fano factors

## Status

Audit/metrology.

Fano factors and auxiliary numerical corrections should only enter the main manuscript after:

1. identification of the parameters used;
2. dimensional verification;
3. separation between apparatus data and universal coefficient;
4. comparison with experimental references;
5. verification that no fitting to the target was done.

Until then, they remain as a pending metrological program.
