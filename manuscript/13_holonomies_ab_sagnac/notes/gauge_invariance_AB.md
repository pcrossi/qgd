# Gauge invariance in AB

## Statement

The closed-loop AB phase is invariant under single-valued gauge transformations.

## Proof

Under:

$$
A\to A+d\lambda,
$$

the integral changes by:

$$
\oint_\gamma A
\to
\oint_\gamma A
+
\oint_\gamma d\lambda.
$$

For single-valued $\lambda$:

$$
\oint_\gamma d\lambda=0.
$$

Thus:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma(A+d\lambda)
=
\operatorname{Hol}_\gamma(A).
$$

## Scope

Multivalued transformations must preserve the single-valuedness of the physical section. The observable is the holonomy class, not the local representative.
