# COW as gravitational interferometry

## Statement

The COW effect can be treated as a reduced extension of gravitational phase in a matter interferometer.

## Estimation

For effective area $A$, acceleration $g$, mass $m$ and velocity $v$:

$$
\Delta\varphi_{\rm COW}
=
\frac{m g A}{\hbar v}.
$$

## GDQ Interpretation

The result is a phase accumulated via the difference in effective connection/potential along the arms. It belongs to the same interferometric family, but not to the AB/Sagnac core.

## Scope

Metrological comparisons require real parameters of the interferometer and control of vibrations, local gravity and geometry.
