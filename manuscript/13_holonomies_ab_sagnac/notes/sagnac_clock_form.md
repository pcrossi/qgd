# Sagnac as clock-form

## Statement

The Sagnac effect is the holonomy of the simultaneity form on a rotating boundary.

## Reduced proof

For angular rotation $\boldsymbol\Omega$:

$$
\mathbf v_{\rm rot}
=
\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r.
$$

The time difference is:

$$
\Delta t_{\rm Sag}
=
\frac{2}{c^2}
\oint_\gamma
\left(
\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r
\right)\cdot d\mathbf r.
$$

By Stokes:

$$
\oint_\gamma
\left(
\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r
\right)\cdot d\mathbf r
=
2\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf A.
$$

Thus:

$$
\Delta t_{\rm Sag}
=
\frac{4\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf A}{c^2}.
$$

The reduced clock-form is:

$$
\Theta_t
=
dt
-
\frac{1}{c^2}
\left(
\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r
\right)\cdot d\mathbf r.
$$

## Scope

$\boldsymbol\Omega$ belongs to the apparatus/rotating boundary. It is not a new term of the official action.
