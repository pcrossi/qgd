# Potential as connection in GDQ

## Statement

In GDQ, the effective potential $A$ must be read as a phase/shear connection of the reduced sector, not as a hidden local force.

## Formulation

The coupled momentum 1-form is:

$$
\omega
=
\left(
\nabla S_R-\frac qcA
\right)\cdot dx.
$$

If:

$$
F=dA=0
$$

in the exterior, there is no local magnetic force. But if:

$$
\oint_\gamma A\ne0,
$$

the holonomy alters the phase.

## Interpretation

The physical reality of the potential means:

$$
\text{reality of the connection/holonomy},
$$

not:

$$
\text{mysterious local force}.
$$

## Scope

This is the structural interpretation of GDQ. The effective form $A$ belongs to the reconstructed connection sector, it is not a new fundamental action.
