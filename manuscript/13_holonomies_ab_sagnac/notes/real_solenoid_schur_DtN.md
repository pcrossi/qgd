# Real solenoid by Schur/DtN

## Statement

Real solenoid corrections must be treated via interface response:

$$
\mathsf R_{\rm sol}
=
K_{YY}
-
K_{YI}K_{II}^{-1}K_{IY}.
$$

## Construction

Write:

$$
A_{\rm eff}
=
A_{\rm harm}
+
\delta A_{\rm surf}.
$$

Then:

$$
\Delta\varphi
=
\frac{q}{\hbar c}
\oint_\gamma
\left(
A_{\rm harm}
+
\delta A_{\rm surf}
\right).
$$

In the ideal case:

$$
\oint_\gamma\delta A_{\rm surf}=0.
$$

In a real apparatus, $\delta A_{\rm surf}$ depends on the material, shielding, geometry and leakages.

## Scope

This is a metrological program. It does not reopen the ideal AB phase.
