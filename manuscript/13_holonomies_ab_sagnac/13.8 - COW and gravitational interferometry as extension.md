# 13.8. COW and gravitational interferometry as extension

The COW effect is another phase interferometry, now via a gravitational potential difference in arms with different heights.

In a reduced approximation:

$$
\Delta\varphi_{\rm COW}
=
\frac{m g A}{\hbar v},
$$

where $A$ is the effective area of the interferometer and $v$ is the longitudinal velocity.

This effect should be treated as an extension of the same principle:

$$
\text{phase}
=
\text{integral of the connection/effective potential along the path}.
$$

But COW is not the core of the chapter. It should appear as a bridge to gravitational interferometry and future metrological applications.

LAGEOS, Gravity Probe B comparisons and pending Fano factors should remain in the appendix/audit until references and parameters are verified.
