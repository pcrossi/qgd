# 13.4. Potentials as effective geometric connection

In GDQ, saying that the potential is real does not mean it is a hidden local force where $B=0$. It means that it is the effective connection that carries phase, shear and holonomic gluing of the domain.

In the reduced sector, the coupled phase momentum is:

$$
\omega
=
\left(
\nabla S_R-\frac qc A
\right)\cdot dx.
$$

If:

$$
dA=0
$$

in the exterior, there is no local magnetic force. But if:

$$
\oint_\gamma A\ne0,
$$

the accumulated phase changes.

Thus, GDQ uses the following mechanical-geometric interpretation:

$$
\text{potential}
=
\text{effective connection/shear of the geometry}.
$$

But it avoids the erroneous statement:

$$
\text{potential}
=
\text{mysterious local force where }F=0.
$$
