# Sachs form factors in GDQ

The form factors are transforms of the geometric densities:

$$
G_E^B(q^2)
=
\int
\rho_E^B(r)j_0(qr)d\mu,
$$

$$
G_M^B(q^2)
=
\int
\rho_M^B(r)j_0(qr)d\mu.
$$

The normalizations are:

$$
G_E^p(0)=1,
\qquad
G_E^n(0)=0,
$$

$$
G_M^p(0)=\mu_p,
\qquad
G_M^n(0)=\mu_n.
$$

For the proton in a shell:

$$
G_E^p(q^2)=j_0(qr_p).
$$

For the neutron, global neutrality does not prevent local polarization:

$$
\rho_E^n
=
A_n(\delta_{\chi_+}-\delta_{\chi_-}).
$$
