# Volume $6\pi^5$ as mass ratio

Each chamber of the braided torus has a volume:

$$
\operatorname{Vol}(\mathcal F_a)
=
2\pi^5.
$$

For three stomata:

$$
\operatorname{Vol}(T^5_{\rm braided})
=
3(2\pi^5)
=
6\pi^5.
$$

In the reduced normalization:

$$
\mathcal I_e=1,
\qquad
E_0=M_ec^2.
$$

Thus:

$$
\frac{M_B}{M_e}
=
\mathcal I_B.
$$

The number $6\pi^5$ is therefore the effective volume normalized by the electron. It is not an arbitrary fit to the proton.
