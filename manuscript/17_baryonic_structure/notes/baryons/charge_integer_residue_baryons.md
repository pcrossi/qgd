# Charge as Cauchy residue in baryons

The charge is:

$$
Q_B
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\oint_{\Gamma_B}
\frac{\phi'(z)}{\phi(z)}\,dz.
$$

By the argument principle:

$$
Q_B
=
N_{\rm zeros}-N_{\rm poles}.
$$

Thus:

$$
Q_B\in\mathbb Z.
$$

For proton and neutron:

$$
Q_p=+1,
\qquad
Q_n=0.
$$

The internal distributions are projections of a global integer residue onto the three throats. GDQ does not need to postulate fundamental fractional charges here.
