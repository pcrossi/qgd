# 17.2. Three stomata and glued solution

The effective cycle used to calculate baryonic invariants is:

$$
\mathcal C_B
\simeq
T^5_{\rm braided}
\times
S^3_{\rm hol}.
$$

The braided torus decomposes into three chambers:

$$
T^5_{\rm braided}
=
\bigsqcup_{a=1}^{3}\mathcal F_a.
$$

Each chamber has a volume:

$$
\operatorname{Vol}(\mathcal F_a)
=
2\pi^5.
$$

Thus:

$$
\operatorname{Vol}(T^5_{\rm braided})
=
3(2\pi^5)
=
6\pi^5.
$$

This number appears as a mass ratio because the electron fixes the reduced unit:

$$
E_0=M_ec^2,
\qquad
\mathcal I_e=1.
$$

Then:

$$
\frac{M_B}{M_e}
=
\frac{\mathcal I_B}{\mathcal I_e}
=
\mathcal I_B.
$$
