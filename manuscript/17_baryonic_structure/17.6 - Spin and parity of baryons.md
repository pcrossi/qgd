# 17.6. Spin and parity of baryons

The baryonic spin is circulation/holonomy:

$$
\oint_{\gamma_B}p_\mu dx^\mu
=
\frac{h}{2}.
$$

Equivalently:

$$
\operatorname{Hol}_{\gamma_B}=-1.
$$

Thus:

$$
J_p=J_n=\frac{\hbar}{2}.
$$

Parity comes from involution:

$$
\mathcal I_P:
\chi\mapsto\pi-\chi,
\qquad
(\theta,\phi)\mapsto(\pi-\theta,\phi+\pi).
$$

In the baryonic background:

$$
\mathcal I_P^*g_B=g_B,
\qquad
\mathcal I_P^*B_B=-B_B.
$$

Since spatial orientation also changes sign, the physical product is preserved:

$$
[H_{\rm GDQ}^{B},\mathcal P]=0.
$$

Thus the ground state is:

$$
J^P(p)=J^P(n)=\frac12^+.
$$
