# 17.3. Bulk mass and surface term

The reduced baryonic mass separates into bulk and surface:

$$
\mathcal I_B
=
\mathcal I_B^{\rm bulk}
+
\mathcal I_B^{\partial}.
$$

The bulk term of the proton is:

$$
\mathcal I_p^{\rm bulk}
=
6\pi^5.
$$

The surface correction comes from torsion via Stokes/transgression:

$$
\int_{\Sigma_B^\circ}d\mathcal T_{\rm top}
=
\int_{\partial\Sigma_B^\circ}\mathcal T_{\rm top}.
$$

For three stomata:

$$
S_{\rm CS}^{(3)}
=
\frac{3\pi}{2},
\qquad
\lambda_{\rm throat}^{(3)}
=
\frac{3}{4\pi^3}.
$$

Thus:

$$
\frac{M_p}{M_e}
=
6\pi^5
+
\alpha
\left(
\frac{3\pi}{2}
+
\frac{3}{4\pi^3}
\right).
$$

With $\alpha^{-1}=137.035999177$:

$$
\frac{M_p}{M_e}
\simeq
1836.1526731886.
$$

The physical meaning is that most of the proton's mass is reduced geometric volume; the fine difference comes from torsion localized on the surface of the throats.
