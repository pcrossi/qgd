# 17.5. Charge as integer residue

The effective electrical charge of the baryon is a phase index/residue:

$$
Q_B
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\oint_{\Gamma_B}
\frac{\phi'(z)}{\phi(z)}\,dz.
$$

By the argument principle:

$$
Q_B
=
N_{\rm zeros}-N_{\rm poles}
\in
\mathbb Z.
$$

Thus:

$$
Q_p=+1,
\qquad
Q_n=0.
$$

The internal fractions used in phenomenological language are effective projections of the global residue on the three throats. They are not fundamental fractional charges in GDQ.
