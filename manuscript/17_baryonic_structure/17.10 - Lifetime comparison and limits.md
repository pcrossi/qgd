# 17.10. Lifetime comparison and limits

The reduced phase space of free beta decay is:

$$
I_\beta
=
\int_{m_e}^{\Delta M}
p_eE_e(\Delta M-E_e)^2\,dE_e,
\qquad
p_e=\sqrt{E_e^2-m_e^2}.
$$

The value used in reduced closure is:

$$
I_\beta
=
5.700456936530352\times10^{-17}\,{\rm GeV}^5.
$$

The total rate is:

$$
\Gamma_n
=
\frac{\mathcal J_3^2}{2\pi^3\hbar}I_\beta.
$$

With the reduced contraction:

$$
\mathcal J_3^2
=
\frac{15\pi^3}{16}
\frac{\alpha^{11}m_ec^2}{I_\beta}.
$$

We obtain:

$$
\tau_n
=
\frac{32}{15}
\alpha^{-11}
\frac{\hbar}{m_ec^2}.
$$

Numerically:

$$
\tau_n
\simeq
879.398775004012\,{\rm s}.
$$

The corresponding half-life is:

$$
T_{1/2}
=
\tau_n\ln 2
\simeq
609.552781481901\,{\rm s}.
$$

Compared to the recorded reference value:

$$
\tau_n^{\rm ref}
=
878.3\,{\rm s},
$$

the difference is:

$$
\Delta\tau
\simeq
1.098775004\,{\rm s},
$$

and the relative difference is of the order:

$$
1.25\times10^{-3}.
$$

If only the reference error of $0.4\,{\rm s}$ is used, this corresponds to approximately $2.75\sigma$. Using the reference average of $878.4\pm0.5\,{\rm s}$, the deviation is approximately $2.0\sigma$.

The closure is strong at the total reduced level, but conservative: angular correlations, recoil, fine differential shape, and individual separation of $C_S$ and $C_T$ remain extensions.
