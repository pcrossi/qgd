# Ontological Note and Terminological Agreement

This manuscript proposes to resolve fundamental problems of modern physics through the application of advanced differential geometry and topology. However, so that the reading does not become a tedious mathematical deciphering exercise, we need to establish a **terminological agreement**.

Our goal is to maintain a clean, fluid, and accessible physical prose, without ever giving up the strict mathematical rigor that underpins the theory. For this, the exact terms defined below will be invoked only when the deduction requires the complete formalism. In the rest of the text, we will adopt their simplified forms, it being fundamental that the reader carries the full ontological meaning of each one.

## 1. Dictionary of Geometric Simplifications

*   **The Manifold / The Mesh / The Vacuum**
    *   *Rigorous Meaning:* **Compact Hermitian Manifold governed by the Bismut Connection**. The theory will often introduce problems through the lens of a pure Kähler Manifold (without torsion) for didactic simplicity. However, by coupling spin and fermionic currents, the vacuum requires a totally antisymmetric torsion to avoid collapse, which mathematically throws us from the Levi-Civita connection to the Bismut Connection.
    *   *In the text:* Whenever we say "the manifold" or "the mesh", implicitly assume that the space already incorporates the Bismut torsion.
    
*   **The Flow / The Geometric Flow**
    *   *Rigorous Meaning:* **Perelman's Generalized Ricci Flow**. We are not dealing with the classical fluid mechanics of Navier-Stokes (although deep analogies exist), nor just with the pure mathematical Ricci flow, but rather with its generalized version that incorporates the dilatonic field and torsion.
    *   *In the text:* We will simply call it "the flow" or "the geometric flow".

*   **The Potential / The Geometric Pressure**
    *   *Rigorous Meaning:* **Extended Bohm Quantum Potential**. In QGD, what Bohm called a "potential" is not an invisible external force, but the saddle counter-pressure generated by the scalar curvature of the fabric on the fluid density.
    *   *In the text:* We will call it "the geometric pressure", "the counter-pressure", or just "the potential".

*   **The Fluid**
    *   *Rigorous Meaning:* **Madelung-Perelman Rheological Condensate**. The probability density of the wave function (Born Rule) is treated here as the polar representation of a continuous and incompressible fluid in asymptotic regimes.
    *   *In the text:* We will call it "the fluid" or "the Madelung fluid".

*   **The Soliton / The Stoma**
    *   *Rigorous Meaning:* **Ricci Soliton (Locked Topological Defect)**. The use of the word "particle" brings vices from classical physics (such as imagining solid billiard balls floating in the void). Matter here is an anomaly in the continuity of the mesh, a node tied to a hole index.
    *   *In the text:* The word "particle" will be avoided whenever possible or used only in quotes to denote the conventional view. The main term will be "soliton" or, when focusing on its slit topology, "stoma".

## 2. Critical Ontological Warning: Emergent Time vs. The Real Flow ($\tau$)

To facilitate pedagogy, the initial chapters (especially those dealing with wave interference, potential wells, and the Double Slit experiment) evoke the mental image of a classical fluid "flowing along time $t$". 

**This is a didactic approximation (an emergent view).** 

The reader needs to know from the first page that the conventional wall clock (the temporal coordinate $t$ of the laboratory) **is not** the stage where the true quantum dynamics occurs. Quantum Geometrodynamics (QGD) is based on the conception of a **four-dimensional Block-Universe**. The chronological past and future ($t$) are already topologically crystallized into a single structure.

What really moves? What actually "evolves"? 

The true dynamics — the friction of the theory's gears — occurs strictly along a fifth rheological dimension: the **scale parameter or Perelman flow time ($\tau$)**. 

In this scenario, the four-dimensional universe ($x, y, z, t$) acts as a Non-Equilibrium Steady State (**NESS**). A quantum measurement, or the causal propagation of a soliton, is not the universe changing from time $t_1$ to $t_2$; it is the entire block-universe undergoing a profound geometric relaxation along $\tau$ until it reaches metric stability.

Keep this duality ($\tau$ versus $t$) in mind. It will be the only possible path to unlock paradoxes of apparent non-locality and apparent retrocausality (as in Wheeler's Delayed Choice) in the advanced chapters.
