### Theoretical Addendum: The Aharonov-Bohm Effect and the Physical Ontology of Gauge Potentials

This theme addresses one of the deepest interpretative discussions in quantum mechanics and field theory. In classical electromagnetism, the four-potential $A_\mu = (\phi, \mathbf{A})$ is considered a redundant mathematical convention due to gauge freedom ($A_\mu \to A_\mu + \partial_\mu \Lambda$). However, the Aharonov-Bohm (AB) Effect presents an important conceptual question: since the phase shift of interference fringes occurs in a region where observable fields are zero ($\mathbf{E} = 0, \mathbf{B} = 0$), conventional quantum mechanics frequently analyzes the phenomenon starting from the direct physical reality of a non-localizable potential or non-local correlations in Hilbert space.

In the scope of QGD, an alternative formulation is proposed. Under this representation, the Aharonov-Bohm effect can be described as a local and mechanical phenomenon, in which the soliton interacts with the Bismut velocity field rheologically altered in the vicinity of the solenoid.
